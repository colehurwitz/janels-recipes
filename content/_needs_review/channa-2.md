1 ½  cups garbanzos (soak overnight) or 2 cans garbanzos

1 large onion, finely chopped

1 ½” piece ginger, finely ground

6 cloves garlic, finely ground

5 tablespoons tomato puree

2 teaspoons salt (1 TEASPOON WITH CANNED BEANS)

3 teaspoons cumin

2 teaspoons coriander

1 teaspoon turmeric

Chili powder to taste

5 tablespoons cooking oil

3 cups water

  

Heat oil in pan and sauté onions until golden, about 10 minutes. Add ginger and garlic, stirring for another 2 minutes. Add salt and turmeric, stir for about 2 minutes. Add tomato paste and spices, stir well together. Add garbanzos. 

  

Now add 3 cups water and let simmer for at least 45 minutes, stirring a few times. More water can be added if you would like it to be soupy. Also vary spice quantity to taste. Turn off heat when the garbanzos are well cooked.

  

 From Simrat’s experience, canned beans do not taste as good as dried beans but if you are in a rush, use them
