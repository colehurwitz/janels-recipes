1 ½ cups garbanzos (soak overnight) or use 2 cans garbanzo beans 

1 large onion finely ground in blender

1 ½” piece of ginger finely ground

6 cloves garlic finely ground

5 tablespoons tomato puree

1 teaspoon salt (NOT WITH CANNED BEANS)

3 teaspoons cumin powder

2 teaspoons coriander powder

1 teaspoon turmeric

Chili powder to taste

5 tablespoons cooking oil

3 cups water

  

Heat oil in a heavy bottomed pot. Add onions, stirring frequently until they turn softened. Add ginger and garlic stirring for two minutes. Add salt and turmeric, stir for two minutes. Strain water from canned garbanzos, rinse and add to the pot. Cook on medium heat and stir frequently. Cook for 10 minutes. Add tomato puree and spices, stir well. Add three cups boiling water and let simmer for 45 minutes. Turn off the heat when garbanzos are well cooked.
