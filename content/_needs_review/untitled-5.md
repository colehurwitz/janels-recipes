6 or so Chicken thighs

1-2 cups rice

1 large onion chopped 

2-3 carrots chopped 

5-7 cloves garlic minced

  

Cook all but chicken in olive oil till fragrant.

Add chicken

Add red wine to cover.

Can mix with chicken broth.

Cook till rice is tender and ready to eat.
