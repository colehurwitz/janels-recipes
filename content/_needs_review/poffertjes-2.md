1 teaspoon instant yeast

1 tablespoon milk

¾ cup buckwheat flour

1 ½ cup flour

2 eggs

1 teaspoon sugar

½ teaspoon salt

1 ⅔ cups warm milk

1 tablespoon butter

  

Combine flours, sugar, salt to bowl. Slowly add milk to yeast. Add milk and yeast to bowl. Mix until incorporated. Add eggs to batter. Cover bowl. Let rest 1 to 1 ½ hours. 

  

Heat poffertjes pan. Melt butter in pan. Add batter. Flip using two forks.
