2 tablespoons lemon juice

Grated zest of 1 lemon

2 to 3 pounds brussels sprouts

2 tablespoons olive oil

2 tablespoons butter

3 garlic cloves, minced

2 tablespoons black mustard seeds, cumin seeds, or poppy seeds

¼ cup dry white wine or vermouth

Salt and pepper to taste

  

Place lemon juice in large bowl. Cut bottoms off sprouts. Working in batches, use a food processor to cut sprouts into thin slices. Transfer to bowl with lemon juice. Toss them in juice and use your fingers to separate leaves. Cover for up to 3 hours.

  

When ready to serve, heat oil and butter over high heat. Add sprouts, garlic, and seeds. Cook, stirring often until sprouts are wilted and lightly cooked, but still bright green and crisp, about 4 minutes. 

  

Add wine and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring 2 minutes more. Turn off heat, add salt and pepper to taste, add more lemon juice if desired. Stir in lemon zest. Serve\!
