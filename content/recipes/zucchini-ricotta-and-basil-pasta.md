Olive Oil

1 small onion, finely diced

2 pounds zucchini, sliced into ¼ inch pieces

Salt and pepper

2 garlic cloves, minced

About 2 cups loose Basil leaves

11 ounces ziti or fusilli pasta

8 to 12 ounces ricotta

Pinch of crushed red pepper

Zest of 1 lemon

Grated parmesan

  

Put a pot of water on to boil. In a large skillet, cook onions in 3 tablespoons of olive oil until softened, 3-5 minutes. Add zucchini, season generously with salt and pepper, and continue cooking, stirring until soft, about 10 minutes. Turn off heat.

  

Meanwhile, grind garlic, basil, and a little salt into a rough paste (use food processor). Stir in 3 tablespoons olive oil.

  

Salt the water and put in the pasta, stirring. Boil until al dente. Drain pasta, reserving 1 cup of cooking water.

  

Add cooked pasta to zucchini in a skillet and turn heat to medium high. Add ½ cup cooking water, then the ricotta, crushed red pepper, and lemon zest, stirring/tossing to distribute. Check seasoning and adjust. Cook for 1 minute more. Mixture should look creamy. Add a little more pasta water if needed. Add the basil paste and stir to incorporate.

  

Serve pasta and top with grated parmesan.
