Meat Layer:

1 pound ground beef    Dash pepper

½ chopped onion     ½ teaspoon allspice

1 clove garlic, minced    1 can tomato sauce

1 teaspoon salt     2 tablespoons tomato paste

         1 cup water

  

Brown ground beef. Add onion, garlic, and seasonings. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, and water. Simmer until thickened, about 30 minutes.

  

Macaroni Layer:

16 ounces noodles     4 tablespoons melted butter

       1 cup grated Romano cheese

  

Toss cooked macaroni with melted butter and grated cheese until thoroughly blended.

  

Spinach Layer:

20 ounces frozen, chopped spinach  ½ teaspoon salt

½ cup bread crumbs    ¼ teaspoon pepper

2 tablespoons melted butter   2 eggs

½ cup finely minced onion   ¾ cup mashed feta

  

Squeeze excess moisture from spinach. Combine with remaining ingredients.

  

To Bake:

  

Arrange layer of noodles. Spread with spinach mixture. Add remaining noodles. Spread meat sauce. Sprinkle with grated cheese. Bake at 350F for 30 minutes. Serve.
