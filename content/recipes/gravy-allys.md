Take juices out of roasting pan. Pour into pan through wire strainer. If there are carrots or celery from inside turkey, squish it through strainer.

Mix about 2 tablespoons cornstarch and whisk with water. Pour into mixture. Stir while bringing to boil. Let it simmer. Add more cornstarch and water if necessary.
