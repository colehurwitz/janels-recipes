2 pounds zucchini, cubed

4 eggs

½ cup milk

1 pound jack cheese, cubed

1 teaspoon salt

2 teaspoons baking powder

3 tablespoons flour

½ cup chopped parsley

Bread crumbs

¼ cube butter (you don’t need a lot - can use less)

1 can green chilies

  

Cook squash gently until al dente, rinse with cold water. Mix eggs, milk, cheese, salt, baking powder, flour, parsley, and chilies. Fold in cooked zucchini. Grease a 9 x 13 pan generously. Sprinkle with bread crumbs. Pour in squash mixture. Sprinkle with more bread crumbs on top. 

  

Bake at 325F for about 30 minutes.
