Layer one:

  

1 ⅓ cups sugar

1 jar marshmallow fluff

⅔ cup evaporated milk

3 tablespoons butter

Dash salt

12 ounce package chocolate chips

¾ cup nuts, optional

  

Line 8 inch square pan with foil. Mix sugar, marshmallow fluff, milk, butter, salt. Bring to boil. Boil, stirring constantly about 5 minutes. Remove from heat. Add 1 package chocolate chips. Stir until smooth. Stir in vanilla. Add nuts if desired. Pour into pan. Chill one hour.

  

Layer two:

  

12 ounce package chocolate chips

1 teaspoon vanilla

6 tablespoon light corn syrup

1 tablespoon water

  

In double boiler, stir one package chocolate chips, corn syrup, water until melted. Pour chocolate mix over cooled fudge. Chill 2 hours. Cut into 1 inch squares.
