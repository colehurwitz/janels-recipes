4 cups rolled oats

⅔ cup unsweetened large flake coconut

2 generous half cups nuts

Mix above ingredients together in a bowl

⅔ cup corn oil

⅓ cup honey

1 tsp vanilla

¼ salt

  

Combine wet ingredients in a saucepan, stir until honey is melted. Pour over dry ingredients, stir thoroughly. Line a 10x15x1 inch cookie pan with parchment paper. Spread granola into pan. Bake at 300 for 35 minutes, stir midway. Cool thoroughly, add half cup dry fruit.
