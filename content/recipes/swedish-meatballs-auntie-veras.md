1 pound hamburger

1 egg

1 small chopped onion

¾  teaspoon salt

¼ teaspoon pepper 

1 teaspoon allspice

¼ -½ cup breadcrumbs

  

Combine ingredients. I may use more than 1 teaspoon allspice - I add allspice until I get an aroma of allspice from the meat. Roll into balls. Fry in pan on all sides. Remove meatball from pans. In the drippings stir in one can Campbell’s Mushroom Soup and ¾ can milk. Return meatballs to pan. Cover. Let simmer for 30-60 minutes.
