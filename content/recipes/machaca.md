2 pounds boneless beef chuck

2 cups water

12 peppercorns

½ medium onion

Salt

2 garlic cloves

½ teaspoon salt

1 tablespoon vegetable oil

1 medium onion, chopped

3 Anaheim peppers, roasted, peeled\*

3 tomatoes, peeled, chopped

½ teaspoon cumin (to taste)

Pepper to taste

  

Place meat in a large saucepan. Add water, peppercorns, ½ onion and salt to taste. Bring to a boil; reduce heat. Cook until meat is very tender, about 1 ½ hours. Cool meat in broth. Reserve ⅔ cup broth. Shred meat with two forks. Mash garlic with ½ teaspoon salt to make a paste. 

  

Heat oil in a large skillet. Add chopped onion and garlic paste. Cook until onion is tender. Cut chile into short strips. Add chile strips and tomatoes to cooked onion. Cook 3 to 4 minutes. Add meat, cumin and pepper to taste. Stir in reserved broth.

  

Taste and add more salt if necessary. 

  

\*I found 505 Southwestern Flame Roasted Green Chili at Costco/Winco/New Seasons that I have used in place of roasted, peeled Anaheims.
