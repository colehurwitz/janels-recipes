1 pound zucchini, grated

2 large eggs

1 ½ cups chopped scallions, about 1 bunch

3 garlic cloves, minced

2 tablespoons dill (optional)(seems like a lot)

1 tablespoon lemon zest

1 teaspoon salt

2 ¼ cups panko breadcrumbs, divided

3 tablespoons flour

Avocado oil for the pan

  

(Can serve with tzatziki - recipe in appetizers)

  

Grate zucchini, place on kitchen towel, sprinkle with salt, let sit for at least 10 minutes. Squeeze out excess moisture.

  

In a large bowl, whisk the eggs. Add the grated zucchini, scallions, garlic, dill, lemon zest, salt, and several grinds of pepper (if desired).

  

Add 1 ½ cups panko, sprinkle in the flour, and fold to combine. Place the remaining ¾ cup panko in a shallow dish and set aside.

  

Preheat a cast-iron skillet to medium heat with avocado oil. Scoop a ¼ cup of the zucchini mixture and form a thin pattie. Coat in reserved panko. 

  

Cook the patties 2 -3 minutes until well browned, working in batches as necessary. Transfer paper towel lined plate to drain. Season to taste and serve with sauce.
