¾ to 1 cup sugar

½ cup butter, softened (I substituted ⅜ cup of safflower oil and it worked)

1 cup (120g) flour, sifted (I added zest from one lemon into the flour)

1 teaspoon baking powder

2 eggs

24 halves pitted purple plums (I tossed the plums in lemon juice, zest, and sparking        sugar)

Sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon for topping

  

Heat oven to 350F. Cream sugar and butter. Add flour (and zest), baking powder, eggs, and beat well. Toss the plums with sparkling sugar and lemon juice (and grated lemon zest if you like).

  

Spoon the batter into a springform pan. Place the plum halves skin side up on top of the batter. Sprinkle with about ½ to 1 teaspoon cinnamon, depending on how much you like cinnamon. I also sprinkled sparking sugar on the top. Because I had a handful of raspberries, I placed them around on the top.

  

Bake about 1 hour. Remove and cool. Serve plain or with whipped cream.
