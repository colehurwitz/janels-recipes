---
title: Hearty Tomato Basil Soup
category: Soups & Stews
notes: A splash of cream at the end makes it velvety; leave it out to keep things lighter.
---

Comfort in a bowl — pairs perfectly with a grilled cheese.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 (28 oz) cans whole tomatoes
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Soften the onion in butter over medium heat, then add the garlic for a minute.
2. Add the tomatoes (crushed by hand) and the broth; simmer 20 minutes.
3. Stir in the basil and blend until smooth.
4. Season to taste and warm through before serving.
