Tangzhong (starter):

  

3 tablespoons (43g) water

3 tablespoons (43g) whole milk

2 tablespoons (14g) bread flour

  

Dough:

  

2 ½ cups (300g) bread flour

2 tablespoons (14g) dry milk

¼ cup (50g) sugar

1 teaspoon (6g) salt

1 tablespoon instant yeast

½ cup (113g) whole milk

4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, very soft

  

To make the tangzhong:

  

Combine all ingredients in a small saucepan, whisk until no lumps remain. Cook over low heat, whisking constantly, until thick and the whisk leaves lines on the bottom of the pan, about 3-5 minutes.

  

Transfer the tanzhong to a small bowl and let it cool to room temperature.

  

To make the dough:

  

Activate the yeast by mixing it with a quarter cup lukewaywater and an ⅛ teaspoon of sugar. Let sit until yeast becomes foamy, about 5-10 minutes. Combine the activated yeast and tangzhong with the remaining dough ingredients, then mix and knead in a standing mixer until a smooth, elastic dough forms, could take 15 minutes. Shape the dough into a ball, and let it rest in a lightly greased bowl for 60-90 minutes, until doubled in bulk.

  

Gently deflate dough. Divide into 66g pieces and form into balls. Place in a lightly greased 9” round cake pan. Cover the pan and let the rolls rest for 40-50 minutes, until puffy.

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Brush the rolls with egg wash (1 large egg beaten with 1 tablespoon cold water), and bake for 25-30 minutes, until golden brown on top and a digital thermometer reads 190F. Remove rolls from oven. Let cool in pan for 10 minutes and then transfer them to steel rack to finish cooling. 

  

To make a loaf, after the dough’s initial rise, divide it into four equal pieces (about 170g each). Flatten each piece into a 5x8” rectangle, then fold the short ends in towards one another. Flatten the folded pieces into 3x6” rectangles. Starting with the short end, roll them into a log. Place the logs in a row of four, seam side down and side by side, in a lightly greased 9 x 5 loaf pan. Cover the loaf and let it rise about 40-50 minutes. Brush the loaf with egg wash and bake at 350F for about 30 minutes until it is golden brown on top and a digital thermometer reads 190F. Remove loaf from oven and let cool on a rack.
