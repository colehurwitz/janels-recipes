For the meringue:    For the cranberry jam:

½ cup plus 2 teaspoons sugar  12 ounce bag cranberries

1 ½ teaspoons cornstarch   ¾ cup sugar (150g)

2 egg whites     ½ cup orange juice

½ teaspoon cream of tartar  1 teaspoon minced, peeled ginger 

pinch of salt      

½  teaspoon vanilla extract  Or use raspberry puree instead of cranberry          

  

For the assembly:

  

About 1 ½ cups lemon curd  About 3 cups raspberries

1 ½ cups heavy cream, whipped Chopped pistachios (optional)

  

To make meringue: Center a rack in the oven and preheat it to 250F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or use a silicone mat.

  

Sift 2 teaspoons sugar and the cornstarch together; set aside. Whisk together egg whites, cream of tartar until soft peaks form. Add remaining ½ cup sugar, 1 tablespoon at a time. When incorporated, add vanilla and beat until stiff, glossy, white peaks form. Fold in sugar-cornstarch mixture.

Scoop meringue onto baking sheet and spread it about ½ inch thick. 

  

Bake, undisturbed, about 1 ¼ hours. It should be beige in color here and there. Turn off oven, prop door open just a crack, and leave the meringue to finish drying another 2 hours or overnight.

  

To make the jam: Put all ingredients in a saucepan and cook, stirring over medium heat, until the mixture bubbles, many of the cranberries pop, and the sauce starts to thicken, about 10 minutes. Let cool.

  

To assemble: For each serving, start with a few spoonfuls each of curd and jam. Add a few spoonfuls whipped cream and top with some berries. Crumble meringue over the dessert and sprinkle with pistachios. Serve.
