1 large onion, chopped

3 tablespoons bacon drippings

1 2-3 pound head red cabbage, cored, quartered, shredded

2 medium tart apples, peeled, cored, diced

½ cup red wine vinegar

1 tablespoon sugar

1 teaspoon salt

1 bay leaf and 2 cloves, tied in cheesecloth

½ cup red currant jelly

  

Sauté onions in bacon drippings until soft. Add cabbage and cook for about 10 minutes. Put in crockpot. Add apples, vinegar, salt, sugar, bay leaf and cloves. Cook on high for 3-4 hours. (Can also cook on stove in large pot on low heat). When cabbage is cooked down and tender, add jelly. Bring to boil. Heat and simmer 10 more minutes. Serve (goes well with mashed potatoes and pork chops\!)
