(JAPANESE CHICKEN AND EGG RICE BOWL)

  

3 cups sushi rice, keep hot

4 skinless, boneless chicken thighs cut into small pieces

1 onion, cut in half and sliced

2 cups dashi stock, made with dashi powder

¼ cup soy sauce

3 tablespoons mirin

1 teaspoon brown sugar

4 eggs

  

Cut chicken into small pieces and cook in a nonstick skillet over medium heat until the chicken is no longer pink inside, about 5 minutes. Stir in onion, and cook and stir until onion is soft, about 5 more minutes. Pour in the stock, and whisk in soy sauce, mirin, and brown sugar, stirring to dissolve sugar. Bring mixture to a boil, let simmer until slightly reduced, about 10 minutes.

  

Whisk eggs in a bowl until well-beaten and pour over chicken and stock. Cover the skillet, reduce heat, and allow to steam for about 5 minutes, until the egg is cooked. Remove from heat.

  

Serve over rice in a soup bowl. Top with chicken/egg mixture, and spoon some of the broth over the top.
