Glaze:

  

3 tablespoons honey

½ teaspoon black pepper

¼ cup water

Salt

  

Coating for chicken (do this twice, once for each pound of chicken):

  

2 tablespoons flour

1 ½ teaspoons turmeric

1 teaspoon salt

  

Remaining ingredients:

  

2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs cut in 1 inch pieces

1 ½ tablespoons coconut oil

2 good sized bunches asparagus, thinly sliced on an angle (ends trimmed)

  

After chicken is cooked, splash with:

  

2 teaspoons rice vinegar

2 teaspoons soy sauce

  

In a small bowl, stir together ¼ cup water with the honey, pepper, and ½  teaspoon salt. Set aside.

In a medium bowl, stir together flour, turmeric, and 1 teaspoon salt. Add 1 pound chicken pieces and toss to coat. Repeat this step with a second pound of chicken.

  

In a medium sauce pan or wok, heat oil over medium heat. Add the chicken and cook until the turmeric is fragrant and the chicken is browned on both sides, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Add the asparagus, season with salt, stir to combine, and cook until crisp-tender, 2 to 3 minutes.

  

Add the honey mixture and cook, stirring, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened, 2 to 3 minutes. Remove from heat, add vinegar and soy sauce. Serve over rice.
