Part A: 1 ½ cups warm water

2 cups Elmer’s glue

Food coloring (your choice)

  

Part B: 1 ⅓ cup warm water

3 tablespoons Borax (the kind used in washing machines)

  

Mix Part A and Part B. Knead. Store in an airtight container. Lasts about 1 week.
