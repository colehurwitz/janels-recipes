Preheat oven to 350F.

  

Melt together:

½ cup butter

4 ounces unsweetened chocolate

Cool this mixture or else brownies will be heavy and dry.

  

Beat until foamy in texture:

4 eggs

Dash salt

  

Add gradually and continue beating until well creamed:

2 cups sugar

1 teaspoon vanilla

  

With a few swift strokes, combine the cooled chocolate mixture and the eggs and sugar. Do this by hand.

  

Fold in by hand:

1 cup flour

1 cup nuts (optional)

  

Bake in greased 9 inch square pan for about 25 to 30 minutes. Let cool.
