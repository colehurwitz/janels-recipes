3 medium sized avocados

2 tablespoons lemon juice

½ teaspoon salt

Pepper to taste

1 cup sour cream

1 package taco mix

2 cans bean dip

1 large bunch green onions, chopped

3 medium tomatoes, chopped

2 cans pitted olives

1 package cheddar cheese, grated

Tortilla chips

  

Mash avocados with lemon juice, salt and pepper. Combine sour cream and taco seasoning in a bowl. Spread bean dip on a large shallow platter. Top with avocado. Layer with sour cream mixture. Sprinkle with onions, tomatoes and olives. Add a layer of grated cheese. Serve with chips.
