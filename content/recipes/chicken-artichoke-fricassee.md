1 - 9 ounce package frozen artichoke hearts

12 small potatoes, pared

2 ½ pounds chicken thighs and breasts, seasoned with garlic and salt

½ cup chopped green onion

2 - 6 ounce cans mushrooms

½ cup dry white wine

2 teaspoons salt

¼ teaspoon pepper

1 teaspoon chopped dill

⅔ cup yogurt

1 tablespoon flour

  

Arrange artichokes with small potatoes in a greased 2 ½ quart casserole. Cut fryer into serving pieces, and brown in oil in large skillet. Put chicken on top of vegetables. Put seasonings on top of chicken.

  

Cook chopped onion in pan drippings until soft. Stir in green onions and mushrooms with liquid and wine. Pour over chicken; cover and bake in 350F oven for about 1 ½ hours. 

  

Remove chicken and vegetables to platter. 

  

Combine yogurt with flour; add to juices from casserole, and heat stirring constantly (do not boil)- it should thicken a bit. 

  

Return chicken and vegetables to baking dish and pour sauce over. Serve\!
