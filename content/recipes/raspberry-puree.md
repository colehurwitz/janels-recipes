Remove seeds from raspberries (you can do this by pushing it through a strainer or use a Champion Juicer). To thicken juice, use cornstarch dissolved in water. Start with about 1 tablespoon cornstarch in a little water. Add to about 16 ounces raspberry juice/puree. Add sugar to taste. For 2 cups raspberry juice, I’d start with ½ cup sugar and then taste after it is mixed in to see if you want to add more sugar. It is to your determination as to the desired tartness. If juice is not thickened enough in your opinion, add a little more cornstarch dissolved in a little water. Cook until it starts to boil. It will thicken as it cools.
