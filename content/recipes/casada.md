Prepare black beans 

  

Sort through black beans to remove rocks, put in pot with 2 quarts water                 to start (may need to add more), 1 tablespoon dried chopped onion or onion powder, 2 teaspoons garlic powder, 1-2 tablespoons salt, 1 bay leaf.

  

Cook till ready to eat (tender). Adjust seasonings as needed. Roger uses an instant pot, but I cook them in a tall pot on the stove. The instant pot makes preparation relatively quick. My method takes about 4 hours of cooking.

  

Prepare rice - can be white or brown - up to you.

  

Once everything is ready, in a pan, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil. Sauce 1 large finely diced red bell pepper, 1 finely diced yellow onion, 2 minced garlic cloves. When they’re slightly tender (al dente), add to black beans. Season with ¼ cup salsa lizano (if you don’t have salsa lizano, add worcestershire sauce and cumin to taste).

  

Serve over rice. Can chop avocado on top or serve with a fried egg. Use your imagination\!
