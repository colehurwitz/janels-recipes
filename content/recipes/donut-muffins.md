6 oz butter (softened)

⅞ cup sugar

2 eggs

3 cups flour

½  tablespoon + 1 teaspoon baking powder

¼ teaspoon baking soda

½ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon nutmeg

½ cup + ⅓ cup milk

⅛ cup buttermilk

  

For dipping:

8 ounces butter

2 cups sugar

2 tablespoons cinnamon

  

Heat oven to 350F. Cream butter and sugar, beat in eggs. Combine dry ingredients alternately with milks. Mix well until well combined, do not over mix. Grease and flour a standard size muffin tin. Pour batter until even with rim of muffin tin. Bake muffins until firm to the touch, 30-35 minutes. 

  

To finish: Melt the butter for the dipping mixture, combine the sugar and cinnamon. When the muffins are just cool enough to handle, remove them, dip them in or brush all over with melted butter. Roll them in cinnamon sugar mix.
