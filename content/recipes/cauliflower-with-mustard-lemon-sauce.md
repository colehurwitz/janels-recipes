1 head cauliflower

1 teaspoon salt

3 tablespoons butter

2 tablespoons lemon juice

2 tablespoons Dijon mustard

  

Preheat oven to 400F. Grease baking sheet. Cut cauliflower in half, then cut into quarter inch thick slices. Arrange in single layer on baking sheet; sprinkle with salt. Roast until slightly softened, about 15 minutes. Whisk melted butter, lemon juice, and mustard together. Spoon over cauliflower and roast until crisp-tender, about 10 minutes longer. Serve.
