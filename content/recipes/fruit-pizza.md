Make a pie crust for one pie

  

Cover pizza pan with crust, form rim around edge. Bake at 425, 12 to15 minutes till lightly golden (won’t be uniformly golden). Cool.

  

Mix together in bowl:

  

1 - 3 ounce package **softened** cream cheese (cream cheese can be lumpy if you don’t make sure it’s room temperature)

¼ cup sugar

1 tablespoon milk

1 tablespoon orange rind

⅔ cup heavy cream

  

Beat until smooth and thick and creamy. Spread evenly on bottom of prepared crust.

  

Prepare glaze:

  

In sauce pan, mix together ½ cup sugar with 2 tablespoons cornstarch. Stir in ¼ cup water, ½ cup orange juice, ¼ cup lemon juice. Cook over medium heat until it boils thick and clear. Cook 1 minute. Remove from heat, cool.

  

Prepare fruit: 

  

Slice strawberries, kiwi, mandarin oranges, and banana. Form rings with fruit. Decorate with 3 strawberries with leaves in the center. Of course you can use any fruit you like on top\!

  

Cover fruit with glaze. 

  

Chill 2 hours.
