About 14 graham crackers

⅛ cup sugar

⅓ cup melted butter

  

In cuisinart, grind crackers to flour-like texture. 

  

Preheat oven to 375F. 

  

Mix crumbs, sugar, and melted butter. Press into pan. 

  

Bake about 8 minutes; let cool.
