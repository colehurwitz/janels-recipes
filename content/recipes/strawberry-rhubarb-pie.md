For the crust:

2 ½ cups flour

Dash salt

¾ teaspoon sugar

15 tablespoons butter, cold and cut into ¼ inch cubes

½ cup ice water

1 tablespoon milk

  

Prepare the pastry. Combine the flour, salt, sugar in large mixing bowl. Using a pastry cutter, work half of the butter into the flour until it resembles coarse meal. Quickly work in remaining butter until biggest pieces are the size of lima beans. Drizzle in the water in several additions, tossing and mixing between each addition. Stop adding water when a few bits of dry flour remain on the bottom of the bowl. Gather ⅔ of the dough and wrap tightly with plastic wrap. Do the same with the remaining third. Flatten into disks. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes. Roll out larger pice and line the pie pan

  

For the filling:

  

3 cups rhubarb cut in one-inch pieces (if thick, peel it)

4 cups strawberry halves

1 cup sugar

3 generous tablespoons minute tapioca

  

Combine rhubarb and strawberries in a bowl. Mix sugar and tapioca together and fold gently into the strawberries and rhubarb. Allow to macerate for 15 minutes. Preheat oven to 425F.

  

Spoon fruit into pie pan. Roll out remaining pastry, cut into strips, and weave a lattice over the pic. Seal, trim and crimp the edges.

  

Bake 15 minutes. Lower heat to 350F and bake 40 minutes longer until filling bubbles. Cool completely before serving. Leave in fridge 3 hours to set.
