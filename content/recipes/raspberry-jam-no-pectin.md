4 heaping cups raspberries

3 ¼ cups sugar

⅓ cup lemon juice

1 teaspoon butter

  

Stir raspberries, sugar, and lemon juice together in a bowl. Let the mixture stand, stirring gently once or twice, until the sugar has dissolve, about 2 hours.  Wash 4 half pint jars. Keep hot until needed.

Scrape the mixture into a large skillet or sauté pan. Add butter. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Boil 6 minutes. Remove from heat.

Wipe jar rim clean. Attach lid. Process in a boiling water bath for 10 minutes.
