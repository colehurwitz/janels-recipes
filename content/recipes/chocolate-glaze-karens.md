3 ounces semi-sweet or bittersweet chocolate

¼ cup powdered sugar, sifted to remove lumps

2 tablespoons butter

2 tablespoons rum (or water)

  

Melt all ingredients together but rum. Take off heat, add rum. Refrigerate until thickened, about 15 minutes.
