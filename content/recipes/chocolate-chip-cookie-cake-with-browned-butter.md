Preheat oven to 350F. Grease a springform pan (it can be cooked in a cake pan but you need to put parchment on the bottom and grease it well).

  

10 tablespoons (142g) unsalted butter, plus more for greasing the pan

½-1 teaspoon kosher salt 

165g brown sugar 

50g white sugar 

1 large egg + 1 egg yolk, cold from fridge

190g flour 

¼ teaspoon baking soda

¼ teaspoon baking powder 

2 teaspoons vanilla 

1 cup (170g) semi-sweet or bittersweet chocolate chips. 

  

Brown the butter. Melt 8 tablespoons/113 grams butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Being careful to not let it burn, stir constantly until all the milk solids turn golden and the butter has a nutty fragrance, about 4 to 5 minutes. 

  

Transfer hot brown butter to a large bowl, then cut the remaining 2 tablespoons butter into cubes, and add it to the bowl to melt. Add the brown sugar and granulated sugar and whisk to combine, then whisk in the whole egg and egg yolk, vanilla and salt. 

  

Whisk in the baking powder and baking soda, then fold in the flour with a rubber spatula. When a few streaks of flour remain, fold in the chocolate chips. 

  

Transfer to the pan, smoothing the top. Sprinkle a bit of flaky salt, if desired. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes or until set on the edges and still slightly soft in the center.
