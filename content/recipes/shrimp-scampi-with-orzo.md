1 pound shrimp (peeled, deveined and tail free is preferable)

1 tablespoon lemon zest

1 tablespoon lemon juice

½ teaspoon red pepper flakes

½ teaspoon salt

2 cloves garlic, minced

  

Marinate above ingredients in a medium bowl for 1 hour.

  

Add 2 tablespoons butter, 3 tablespoons olive oil, and 2 cloves garlic, minced in medium skillet (I used a cast iron pan). When butter bubbles, add 1 cup orzo and ½ teaspoon salt. 

  

Stir mixture about 2 minutes, until orzo appears toasted (not all the orzo looked toasted).

  

Add ⅓ cup white wine and stir until absorbed, about 1 minute. Repeat - add ⅓ cup white wine and stir until absorbed.

  

Stir in 1 ⅔ cup boiling water, stir in water, reduce heat to low, cover and cook until al dente, about 12 minutes.

  

Add shrimp in even layer on top of orzo (I also scraped in the remnants of the marinade). Cover and cook about 4 minutes. Turn stove off, let sit 2 minutes.

  

I served it with parmesan cheese, but the recipe didn’t suggest that, so your choice\!
