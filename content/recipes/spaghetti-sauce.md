1 pound Italian sausage

1 zucchini, grated

2 carrots, grated

1 pound spinach, chopped

1 large onion, chopped

5-6 medium garlic cloves, minced

1 jar spaghetti sauce like Ragu

1 can diced tomatoes, chopped in blender so its not chunky

(You can increase these quantities if you’re trying to make a lot)

I add leftover wine into the sauce as well - about 1 to 2 cups.

¼ cup Italian seasoning

Salt to taste

Pepper to taste

3 tablespoons basil

1-3 tablespoons sugar.

  

You can add crushed fennel (Jordan doesn’t like fennel, but then again he doesn’t like spaghetti sauce\!), or rosemary, or oregano. But Italian seasoning has a lot of different flavors so it’s good all around.

  

Cook and crumble sausage; set aside. Add olive oil to pot. Saute onions and garlic until soft. Return sausage to pot. Add grated zucchini and carrots, cook a couple of minutes. Add chopped spinach, cook until it is wilted. 

Pour in spaghetti sauce and diced tomatoes, mix in. Add seasonings. Don’t add sugar yet.

Let everything simmer on low heat, covered, for about an hour. Taste. Add seasonings if needed. 

  

Add 1 tablespoon sugar. Let it all simmer, mostly covered (leave a little space for steam to escape), about 15 minutes. Taste. Does it need more sugar? If it tastes a little bitter still, add more sugar. Continue to cook, with the lid slightly askew, until it reaches desired consistency. Serve over pasta with parmesan.

  

To make lasagne: start with layer of meat sauce. Layer of noodles. Layer of meat sauce. Cover with ricotta (I use the whole container on 1 layer), sprinkle mozzarella. Layer of noodles etc. repeat until pan is full. Bake about 45 minutes at 350 until bubbly and golden.
