3 egg whites

¾ cup sugar 

2 ½ cups shredded coconut, divided

¼ cup almond flour

  

Preheat oven to 275F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

  

Beat egg whites until soft peaks form. Stir 2 cups shredded coconut and flour into mixture until combined into a soft dough.

  

Shape dough into walnut-sized balls. Roll in remaining shredded coconut and place 2 inches apart on baking sheet. 

  

Bake until lightly golden colored, about 30 minutes. Let cool 1 minutes before removing to cooling rack.
