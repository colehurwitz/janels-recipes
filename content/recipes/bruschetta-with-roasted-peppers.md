1 sweet red pepper (roasted, peeled, seeded)

1 sweet yellow pepper (roasted, peeled, seeded)

3 tablespoons olive oil

1 clove garlic finely mixed

2 tablespoons salt cured capers

Balsamic to taste

  

Heat olive oil in a small skillet. Add peppers, cook over medium heat stirring frequently until soft but not browned. Add garlic, lower heat and cook 10 minutes. Add capers and vinegar, continue to cook until very soft. Add salt and pepper to taste. 

  

To make bruschetta, preheat oven to 350F. Slice bread into 3/4” slices. Brush both sides with olive oil and brown lightly on both sides.
