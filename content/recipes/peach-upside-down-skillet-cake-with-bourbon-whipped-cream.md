(can use this recipes with apples as well)

  

4 medium peaches, unpeeled and cut into ½ inch thick wedges

Juice of 1 lemon

1 cup flour

¾ teaspoon baking powder

¼ teaspoon baking soda

1 cup sugar

5 tablespoons butter (3+2) at room temperature

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

2 eggs

½ cup sour cream by

  

Heat oven to 350F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil in case the cake bubbles over during baking.

  

In a large bowl, toss the peaches with lemon juice. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder and baking soda.

  

In 10 inch cast iron skillet, cook ¼ cup of the granulated sugar over medium heat, stirring occasionally with a wooden spoon until the sugar melts and turns an amber color, about 10 minutes. 

Remove from heat and immediately add 2 tablespoons butter, stirring vigorously. The mixture may not appear smooth, but don’t worry, it will smooth out in baking. Arrange the peach wedges in concentric circles over the sugar mixture, overlapping as needed to make them fit. Before I arranged the peaches, I threw a handful of blueberries and raspberries onto the bottom of the skillet.

  

Beat remaining sugar, butter, and the vanilla extract until smooth. Add eggs one at a time, beating after each addition. Add the sour cream and beat until blended. With the mixer on low speed, gradually add the flour mixture, beating just until blended. Scrape bowl as necessary. Spoon batter over peaches in the skillet and spread to cover.

  

Place the skillet on the foil in the oven. Bake until golden brown and a tester inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean, 40-45 minutes.

  

Let the cake cool in the skillet for 10 minutes. Run a knife around the edge to loosen. If you see liquid around the edges of the skillet, carefully pour off into a measuring cup and set aside. Carefully invert the cake onto a serving plate and drizzle over any reserved liquid. Let cool about 10 minutes. 

  

Cut into wedges using a really sharp knife and serve with whipped cream.

  

Whipped cream:

1 cup heavy whipping cream

2 tablespoons bourbon/whisky

sugar to taste

Whip until firm, but not too firm. Refrigerate until you’re ready to use it.
