1 cup millet

2 cups flour

1 ¼ teaspoons baking powder

1/2 teaspoon baking soda

Dash salt

3 eggs

1 ¼ cup milk

1 tablespoon vanilla

1 ½ sticks (¾ cup) butter, softened

¾  cup brown sugar, packed

  

Preheat oven to 375F. Grease muffin cups. Cream butter and sugar. Alternately beat in dry ingredients and wet ingredients. Do not over beat. 

  

Spoon batter into pan and bake until golden and toothpick comes out almost clean, 20 to 25 minutes. Cool muffins and serve\!
