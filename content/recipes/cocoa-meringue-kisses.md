3 large egg whites

¾ cup superfine sugar

4 teaspoons unsweetened cocoa powder

3 ounces semisweet chocolate

  

Preheat oven to 225F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment.

  

Beat egg whites until foamy and to point of soft peaks. Add ½ cup sugar, 1 tablespoon at a time, beating at high speed after each addition, until a stiff, glossy meringue forms.

In small bowl, sift cocoa powder and remaining sugar (1/4 cup). Sift mixture over meringue. Using a large spatula, gently fold the cocoa mixture into the meringue.

  

Spoon cocoa meringue onto cookie sheet, making about 1 inch cookie, swirling the top to form a point in the center. Place about 1 inch apart.

  

Bake meringues in oven for about 2 hours until bottom of cookies are dry. Turn oven off and let kisses cool.

  

Meanwhile, gently melt chocolate in small saucepan over low heat.  When meringues are cool, dip the top into the chocolate. Place onto wire rack to dry.
