Bend a coat hangar into a loop.

  

Mix ½ cup Dawn soap with 5 cups water. Measure carefully - it makes a difference\!

  

Stir

  

Add two tablespoons glycerine (for more durable bubbles).

  

Now dip the coat hanger loop into the solution and wave it in the air. The solution gets better as it sits.
