You may want to double the recipe:

  

⅔ cup brown sugar, packed

8 tablespoons butter, softened

Dash salt

1 teaspoon vanilla

½ teaspoon baking soda

1 egg

1 cup flour

Coating (mix in ziplock bag):

2 tablespoons sugar (I like to use coarse grain sugar)

Dash salt

  

Preheat oven to 375F. Combine sugar, butter, salt, vanilla, and baking soda. Beat until smooth. Add egg. Add flour, mixing until smooth. Refrigerate overnight or for at least 4 hours.

Scoop chilled cookie dough into 1 inch balls. Shake in coating until covered.

  

Place about 2 inches apart on greased cookie sheet. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until deep golden brown (in my oven, 13 minutes is the sweet spot, no pun intended\!). 

  

I found that if, after rolling the cookies into balls and coating them in sugar,  you allow the batter to warm up a little, they spread as they cook, which is what you want. If they’re really cold, they don’t spread as much (but still taste good). So if you roll all the cookies before you start baking, they should be warm enough to spread out on the cookie sheet while baking.
