2 cups (470g) slightly warm water

2 teaspoons salt

1 tablespoon olive oil

2 teaspoons yeast

4 cups (560g) bread flour

  

Mix water, salt, olive oil, and yeast until yeast is dissolved. Mix in bread flour until dough forms. Cover, let sit 30 minutes (use proofer if possible). 

  

After 30 minutes, wet hands and grab one end of dough and pull it over the top to opposite end. Turn bowl one quarter. Repeat process. Turn bowl. Repeat. Turn bowl. Repeat.

  

Cover, let sit in proofer 30 minutes. Repeat above “kneading” process. 

Cover, let sit in proofer 30 minutes. Repeat above “kneading” process. 

Cover, let sit in proofer 30 minutes. Repeat above “kneading” process.

  

Cover, put in refrigerator. Let it sit overnight. OR\*  

Take dough out of refrigerator and let it return to room temperature, 3-4 hours. 

  

\*You can do it the same day - Do one last kneading process and then cover and let sit 30 minutes. If it’s not bubbly enough, let it sit in the proofer another 30 minutes. Then follow the rest of the recipe as you would with overnight dough.

  

Repeat “kneading” process.

  

Prepare 9x13 pan by pouring 3 tablespoons olive oil to pan. Grease all sides.

  

Pour dough into the pan. Pour olive oil over dough. Cover and let rise 60 minutes.

  

Preheat oven 400F, preferable to use convection.

  

Pour more olive oil on top. Then dimple and wiggle being gentle not to pop bubbles.

  

Bake 20-22 minutes. Let cool. Enjoy\!
