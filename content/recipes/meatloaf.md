1 ½ pounds ground turkey or beef

1 small onion finely chopped

½ cup ketchup

½ to ¾ cups breadcrumbs

1 egg

1 package onion soup mix

  

Mix together, form into loaf. Rub on additional ketchup. Bake an hour at 350F.
