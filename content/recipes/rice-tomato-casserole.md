¼ cup butter (or half olive oil)    1 ½ cups boiling water

1 medium onion, chopped    1 teaspoon salt

1 clove garlic, minced     Pepper to taste

1 green or red pepper, chopped   1 can sliced olives

1 cup raw rice      1 ½ cups grated cheese

15 ounce can tomato sauce

  

Heat butter in heavy saucepan, add the onion, garlic, pepper, rice and cook, stirring, until lightly browned

  

Add boiling water, tomato sauce, salt and pepper. Simmer, covered, stirring occasionally to keep rice from sticking to the bottom of the pan. When water has been mostly absorbed, remove from heat and leave covered to fluff rice.

  

Line a greased one-quart casserole with half the rice. Sprinkle with cheese and olives. Top with remaining rice. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake in 400F oven until cheese is browned and heated through.
