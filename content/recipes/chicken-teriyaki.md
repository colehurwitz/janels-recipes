3 ½ pounds chicken pieces

  

Sauce:

  

⅓ cup soy sauce

2 tablespoons (or up to ¼ cup) sugar

⅓ cup mirin

¼ teaspoon ginger

2 tablespoons water

1 tablespoon cornstarch

  

Preheat broiler until very hot. Place chicken on oiled roasting pan. Cook until brown on both sides. Lower heat. Remove fat and add sauce over chicken and cook a few minutes on low heat. Turn pieces until sauce is well mixed. Serve over rice.
