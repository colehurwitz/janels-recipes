Feeds 5-6 people. Reduce quantities proportionately to reduce quantity.

  

7 packs udon noodles

7 tablespoons sesame sauce

Equal amount Oriental Worcestershire Sauce

5 tablespoons soy sauce

1 ½ teaspoons sugar

Spinach or greens

Beef or chicken

  

Marinate beef or chicken in 1 puréed asian pear, 4 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon mirin, 1 tablespoon rice wine. Let sit for at least 30 minutes. Longer if possible, up to overnight. Toss beef in a flour/salt mixture, pan fry. 

  

Meanwhile boil udon noodles until tender. Rinse with cold water. Put in a large bowl. Make dressing (sesame, Worcestershire, soy sauce, sugar - adjust quantities as needed). 

  

While the noodles are cooking, rinse and cook spinach or other green. 

  

Toss udon noodles with sauce. Add beef, add spinach, serve.
