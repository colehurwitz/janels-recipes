⅔ cup brown sugar

⅔ cup dark corn syrup

2 teaspoons ginger

2 teaspoons cinnamon

½ teaspoon cloves

¾ tablespoon baking soda

⅔ cup butter

1 egg

5 cups flour

  

Heat sugar, syrup and spices to boiling point. Add baking soda and pour mixture over butter in bowl. Stir until butter melts. Add egg and sifted flour and blend thoroughly. Knead on mat. Chill. Roll out and cut with cookie cutters. 

  

Bake in 325F oven 8 to 10 minutes.
