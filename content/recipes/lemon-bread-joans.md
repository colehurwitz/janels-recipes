1 ½ cups flour

1 cup sugar

1 teaspoon baking powder

Dash salt

2 eggs

½ cup EACH milk and vegetable oil

1 ½ teaspoons grated lemon peel

  

Glaze:

  

4 ½ tablespoons lemon juice and ⅓ cup sugar. Cook until sugar is dissolved.

  

Stir together flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. In separate bowl, beat eggs with milk, oil, and lemon peel. Add liquid mixture to the flour mixture and mix until just blended. Pour batter into a greased/floured loaf pan. Bake at 350F for 40-45 minutes.

  

Prepare glaze: When bread is baked, use a wooden skewer to poke holes to bottom of loaf. Drizzle glaze over top so it slowly soaks into the bread

. Let bread cool in pan, about 15 minutes.
