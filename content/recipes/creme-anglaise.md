1 cup whole milk or cream

2 tablespoons sugar

2 teaspoons vanilla

4 egg yolks + 2 tablespoons sugar

  

Heat whole milk or cream, 2 tablespoons sugar, and vanilla in a pan until little bubbles form around edges. Whisk egg yolks and 2 tablespoons sugar together. Pour a little hot milk into the egg yolk to temper it. Slowly pour egg yolk mixture into hot milk in pan. Return to stove and cook over low heat until it thickens (coats the back of the spoon). 

You may want to strain it through a sieve to get a nice, creamy texture.

  

Alternate recipe:

  

1 cup cream

1 cup whole milk

⅓ cup sugar

1 vanilla bean

6 egg yolks

  

Add cream, milk, half the sugar, scraped vanilla bean. Simmer until it starts to bubble around the edge of pan. Whisk egg yolks with remaining sugar. Ladle hot mixture into egg mixture and cook over medium heat stirring constantly until it coats the back of a spoon. 5 to 7 minutes. Pour through strainer.
