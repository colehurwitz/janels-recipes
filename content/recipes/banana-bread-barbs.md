½ cup butter

2 eggs

2 cups flour

1 teaspoons vanilla

1 cup sugar

3 bananas (crushed)

1 teaspoon baking soda

  

Cream butter and sugar. Add eggs, flour, baking soda, crushed bananas and mix. Place in two greased loaf pans. Bake at 350F for 30 minutes.
