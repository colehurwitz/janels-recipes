2 cups flour

1 tablespoon baking powder

1 ½ teaspoon salt

3 tablespoons sugar

6 tablespoons butter

2 eggs

¼ cup cream

½ cup currants or chocolate chips or cherries or ? (you decide\!)

  

Preheat oven to 400F. In a food processor, mix dry ingredients, cut in butter. Beat eggs and cream together, fold in dry ingredients. Blend until dough just comes together. Brush tops with beaten egg and sprinkle with sugar. 

  

Bake 12-15 minutes or until golden on top.
