For the overnight sponge:

1 cup (240 grams) sourdough starter 

1 cup (224 grams) buttermilk

1 cup (120 grams) flour

1 tablespoon (13 grams) brown sugar

For the batter:

¼ cup melted butter

½ teaspoon vanilla

½ teaspoon salt

1 teaspoon baking soda

  

Step 1

Put the sourdough starter in a large bowl and add the buttermilk, flour, and sugar, then stir to combine. Cover the bowl and allow it to rest overnight at room temperature.

  

Step 2

When you are ready to cook, whisk the egg, melted butter and vanilla in a small bowl. Add to rested sponge. Add the salt and baking soda to the batter and mix to combine.

  

Step 3

Prepare on griddle or cook in waffle iron.
