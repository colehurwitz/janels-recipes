Olive oil.

Then slice and place onion. Sprinkle turmeric, cinnamon, cumin, salt, and pepper. Add meat. Sprinkle with spices. Place potatoes around meat. Place shallots on the top and around meat. Place carrots and angling up. Place quartered tomato on top. Place Anaheim pepper at angles up.

  

Cook on stovetop on low heat until done.
