Hot Chili Mayo:

  

⅔ cup Japanese mayonnaise

1 tbsp sriracha

  

Meatballs:

  

Either buy New Seasons Banh Mi meatballs OR:

  

1 pound ground pork      1 tablespoon hot chili sauce

¼ cup finely chopped basil     1 tablespoon sugar

4 garlic cloves, minced     2 teaspoons cornstarch

3 green onions, finely chopped    Salt and pepper to taste

1 tablespoon fish sauce

  

For the sandwiches:

2 cups coarsely grated carrots    1 teaspoon salt

2 cups coarsely grated peeled daikon   1 teaspoon sesame oil

¼ cup unseasoned rice vinegar    Hoagi rolls

¼ cup sugar       Thinly slice jalapenos

  

Make mayo. Cover and chill.

  

Toss vegetables, rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Let stand at room temperature for an hour, tossing occasionally.

  

Mix meatball ingredients in a large bowl. Roll meat mixture into 1 inch meatballs. The recipe says to 

brown the meatballs in sesame oil and then  bake it at 300F about 15 minutes. I think you also could cook them in a pan.

  

Cut baguette or Hoagi rolls in half. Spread hot chili may over each bread shell. Arrange jalapeños, then cilantro, fill with meatballs. Place picked vegetables on top of meatballs. Serve\!
