Cook fish as desired. To cook Salmon, I like to salt it and let it sit for 30 minutes. Then I rinse it off and pat it dry. Then I rub it with olive oil, sprinkle it with dill, and bake for 12-14 minutes at 350F until it reaches about 125F internal temperature.

  

Prepare topping in cast iron pan:

  

8 ounces cherry tomatoes, halved (can use more if desired) 

⅓ c pitted castrelavano olives, chopped (can use more if desired) 

1 tablespoon capers, chopped 

1 tablespoon olive oil 

1 tablespoon lemon juice 

Caramelize 2 shallot bulbs (can add chopped garlic if desired)

  

In a 12” skillet, heat oil with 1 tablespoon butter over medium high heat. When foaming subsides, add tomatoes, cooking until tomatoes start to release juices. Stir in capers, olives, lemon juice, and caramelized shallots. 

  

Serve over the top of the prepared fish.
