---
title: No-Knead Rustic Bread
category: Breads
notes: The long, slow rise does the work for you — start it the night before.

A crackly, bakery-style loaf with almost no effort and no kneading at all.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1¼ tsp salt
- ½ tsp instant yeast
- 1½ cups warm water

## Directions

1. Stir the flour, salt, and yeast together, then mix in the water to a shaggy dough.
2. Cover and let rise at room temperature for 12–18 hours.
3. Turn out onto a floured surface, fold over a few times, and shape into a ball.
4. Rest 30 minutes while a covered Dutch oven heats at 450°F (230°C).
5. Drop the dough in, cover, and bake 30 minutes; uncover and bake 15 more until deep golden.
6. Cool completely before slicing.
