1 pound dried black beans

6 cups water

2 green peppers

⅓ cup olive oil

1 medium onion

4 cloves garlic

4 teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon oregano

1 bay leaf

1 tablespoon sugar

1-2 Italian sausages or chorizo; another variation is pork jowls

  

Chop everything up and throw it in the crock pot. Let it cook all day. Right before you serve, add 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar, 2 tablespoons cooking wine, and 2 tablespoons olive oil.
