100 grams/ ½ cup butter, melted

225 grams/ ⅔ cup honey

2 teaspoons vanilla extract

2 teaspoons almond extract

2 eggs, plus 2 egg yolks

240 grams/ 1 cup creme fraiche

210 grams/ 1 ½ cups flour

100 grams/ 1 cup almond flour

2 teaspoons baking powder

¼ teaspoon baking soda

½ teaspoon salt

  

Frosting:

  

225 grams/ 8 ounces cream cheese, room temperature

85 grams/ 6 tablespoons butter, softened

115 grams/ ⅓ cup honey

2 teaspoons vanilla extract

1 teaspoon almond extract

Dash salt.

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Butter and flour bottom and sides of 9x13 inch pan.

Whisk together butter, honey, vanilla, and almond extract. Whisk in eggs and egg yolks. Whisk in creme fraiche. 

  

Mix together flours, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Gently fold dry ingredients into wet ingredients. Scrape batter into prepared pan. Bake for 30-40 minutes. Let cool.

To make frosting, beat cream cheese until smooth. Add butter. Add honey, vanilla, almond extract, and salt.

  

Frost cake when cooled.
