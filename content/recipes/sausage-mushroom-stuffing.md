8 cups stale bread, broken into small cubes

  

In large skillet, cook:

  

2 tablespoons butter, 4 tablespoons olive oil, 1 ½  cups chopped onion, 1 cups chopped celery until tender. Cook sausage. Mushrooms are optional. Combine mixture with bread and season with 2 tablespoons chopped parsley, 1 tablespoon salt, 2 tablespoons poultry seasoning, ¼ teaspoon pepper. Moisten with 1 cup chicken broth and 1 egg, lightly beaten together. 

  

Bake until warm.
