½ cup butter, melted (plus a tablespoon of butter for greasing 8” pan)

10 ounces fresh hulled strawberries, chopped (I use cuisinart and pulse)

⅔ cup (150 g) brown sugar, divided in two

½ cup whole milk, at room temperature

Dash salt

1 cup (130 g) flour

1 teaspoon baking powder

  
  

Measure out ½ cup milk, let sit so it warms up.

  

Heat oven to 350F; grease 8” baking dish. Set aside. 

  

Chop strawberries, stir in ⅓ cup (75g) brown sugar; set aside to mascerate.

  

In a medium bowl, whisk melted butter, remaining ⅓ cup (75g) brown sugar, room temperature milk, salt, then add flour and baking powder. Whisk till smooth. (It may work better to put the flour and baking powder in a bowl and then pour the butter mixture over it, stirring while doing it, to prevent lumps).

  

Transfer to buttered baking dish, spread evenly.

  

Spoon strawberries and juices over top of cake batter. Bake till cake is 200F in center or toothpick comes out with a crumb or two. Recipe says to bake it 20-25 minutes, but because I put in double the amount of strawberries called for by the recipe, I have found it takes an additional 10-20 minutes for it to be done.

  

Serve plain, with whipped cream, or with ice cream.
