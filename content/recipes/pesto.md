3 cups loosely packed fresh basil

¾ cup olive oil

¼ cup pine nuts

3 garlic cloves

1 teaspoon salt

½ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

3 tablespoons Romano cheese

  

Put basil, oil, pine nuts, garlic, and salt into a blender or food processor. Process until smooth.\* Pour sauce into a small bowl. Add Parmesan cheese and Romano cheese. Mix to blend. 

  

Taste and adjust for seasoning. Makes one cup.

  

\*can freeze this for later use at this point. Add cheese later when defrosted.
