The berries:

4 ½ cups fresh blueberries

⅓ cup sugar

2 tablespoons flour

Zest of one lemon

  

The dough:

1 ½ cups flour

Dash salt

1 ½ tablespoons sugar

2 ¼ teaspoons baking powder

6 tablespoons cold butter, but into ½ inch pieces

¾ cup heavy cream, plus additional for serving if desired

  

Heat oven to 375F. Place berries in bowl, and toss with flour and sugar. Set aside.

To make the dough, mix the flour, salt, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl. Cut in the butter until mixture resembles coarse meal. Add the cream and mix lightly, just until the dry ingredients are moistened.

  

Put the blueberries in a 1 ½ quart baking dish. Make patties out of the dough, arrange them over the top of the berries.

  

Bake until the topping is brown and the juices bubble thickly around it, about 35 to 40 minutes.

Let cool slightly. Serve warm with cream to pour on top if desired.
