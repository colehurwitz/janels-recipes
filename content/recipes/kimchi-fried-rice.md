1 ½ cups kimchi

1 tablespoon vegetable oil

1 tablespoon sesame oil

1 tablespoon gochujang (Korean chili bean paste)

4 cups cooked short grain rice

3 spring onions, finely sliced

1 tsp salt

1 egg, optional

1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds

  

Squeeze as much juice as you can from the kimchi and set it aside. Finely slice the kimchi.

  

Heat wok over high heat and add the oils. Add kimchi and toss frequently until it is very fragrant. Add the gochujang and toss in wok for about a minute until it is fragrant as well.

  

Add rice, pressing it onto the sides of the wok to separate the grains. Add the reserved kimchi juice, spring onions, and salt. Mix everything very well, allowing rice to very lightly toast against the sides of the wok.

  

In a separate frying pan, fry the egg in a little oil to your liking.

  

Serve egg on top of rice, and scatter sesame seeds over.
