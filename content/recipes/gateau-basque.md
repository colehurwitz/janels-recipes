Makes 9 inch cake (springform pan)

  

Cake ingredients:

  

1 cup unsalted butter, softened

½ cup granulated sugar

3 large egg yolks

3 large eggs

3 ¾ cups (469g) flour

1 tablespoon (15g) baking powder

½ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon vanilla

1 tablespoon water

Garnish confectioner’s sugar

  

In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, beat butter and sugar until fluffy, 3-4 minutes, stopping to scrape sides of bowl. Add egg yolks and 2 eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Add vanilla.

  

In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. Reduce mixer speed to low. Gradually add flour mixture to butter mixture, beating just until combined. Turn out dough, and shape into 2 disks. Roll each disk between two sheets of parchment paper to an 11” circle. Refrigerate for at least one hour or overnight.

  

Spray a 9” springform pan with baking spray.

  

Cut one of the disks to fit the bottom. Fit the extra pieces around the side. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

  

Prepare custard filling (recipe follows).

  

Pour custard filling into prepared crust and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes

  

Cut remaining disk to fit over top (use base of springform pan to estimate size). Crimp edges. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes.

  

Preheat oven to 400F. In small bowl, whisk together 1 tablespoon water and remaining 1 egg. Brush top of dough with egg wash. (I don’t typically do this).

  

Bake for 20 minutes. Reduce oven temperature to 350F, and bake about 25 minutes more, covering with foil if necessary to avoid excess browning. Allow to cool completely. Garnish with confectioner’s sugar.

  
  

Custard Filling

  

2 ¼ cups (540g) whole milk

1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise, scraped and reserved

½ cup sugar

3 tablespoons cornstarch

6 large egg yolks

  

In a medium saucepan, heat milk and vanilla bean scrapings over medium heat. 

In a medium bowl, whisk together sugar, cornstarch, and egg yolks. Slowly add hot milk mixture into sugar mixture while whisking. Return mixture to saucepan, and cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until bubbly and very thick, about 2-5 minutes.

  

Remove from heat and cover with plastic wrap, pressing directly onto surface of custard. Let cool to room temperature, about 1 hour.
