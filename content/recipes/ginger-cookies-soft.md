2 ¼ cups flour

2 teaspoons ginger

1 teaspoon baking soda

¾ teaspoon ground cinnamon

½ teaspoon cloves

¼ teaspoon salt

¾ cup butter

1 cup sugar

1 egg

1 tablespoon water

¼ cup molasses

2 tablespoons sugar

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Sift together flour, baking soda, and spices. Set aside.

In a large bowl, cream together butter and 1 cup sugar until fluffy. Beat in egg, then stir in molasses and water. Gradually mix in dry ingredients. Shape dough into walnut sized balls and roll them in the remaining 2 tablespoons sugar. Place cookies 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie sheet, and flatten slightly.

  

Bake 8 to10 minutes. Allow cookies to cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes before removing to wire rack to cool completely.
