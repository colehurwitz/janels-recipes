3 small ears corn

1 garlic clove

½ teaspoon salt

1 tablespoon oil

1 small onion, chopped

3 small tomatoes, peeled and chopped (¾ pound)

2 tablespoons canned, chopped green chiles

1 quart chicken broth

½ teaspoon dried oregano

¼ cup whipping cream (or use cashew crema\!)

  

Cut kernels from cob. Scrape cobs with a sharp spoon; discard cobs. Measure corn. You should have about 1 ½ cups. Puree ¾ cup corn in blender or food processor; set aside. Mash garlic with salt to make a paste. Heat oil in a large saucepan. Add onion and garlic paste. Cook until onion is tender but not browned. Add tomatoes and green chiles. Cook slowly 10 minutes, mashing tomatoes with a spoon. Add broth, oregano, pureed corn and whole corn kernels. Taste and add salt if needed. Bring to a boil; reduce heat. Cover and simmer 30 minutes. Stir in cream. Cooke until heated through. Serve.
