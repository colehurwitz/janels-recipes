Bring 4 cups water to boil.

Put 20 grams of tea in a bag and add to boiling hot water.

Let sit 20 minutes.

Add 1 cup sugar, stir until mixed and dissolved.

Fill gallon jar ½ full with filtered water.

Pour tea/sugar mixture into gallon jar.

Fill jar to neck with water.

Let cool.

A couple of days later, put flavoring into bottles. Some options are: 

100 milliliters passion fruit juice

50 milliliters Yuzu with 25 milliliters simple syrup

2 packs lime flavoring with 25 milliliters simple syrup

75 milliliters raspberry puree with 25 milliliters simple syrup

100 milliliters pineapple juice

Fill bottles with kombucha tea. Let sit for 2-3 days. Refrigerate. Serve.
