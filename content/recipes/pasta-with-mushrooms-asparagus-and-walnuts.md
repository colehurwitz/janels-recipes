Salt

1 pound pasta

3 tablespoons butter

1 pound mushrooms, thickly sliced

1 pound asparagus, trimmed, cut into 1 inch pieces

1 onion, chopped

1 large clove garlic, minced

1 cup mascarpone cheese

¾ cup walnuts or pine nuts, optional

1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

  

1 cup grated Romano cheese

Bring a pot of water to boil. Add pasta and cook until al dente. Drain, reserving some pasta water.

Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onions, garlic, and mushrooms. Add some salt. 

  

Sauté until tender and most of the juices from the mushrooms have evaporated, about 5 minutes. Add the asparagus and some salt; sauté until the asparagus is crisp-tender, about 5 minutes. Add pasta. Stir in the mascarpone cheese and toss until the cheese coats the pasta. Add reserved liquid if necessary. 

  

Toss in Parmesan and Romano. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with additional Parmesan.
