1 cup sugar

⅓ cup cornstarch (too much will make it too thick)

1 ½  cup cold water

4 egg yolks

Zest of one lemon

½ cup lemon juice

2 tablespoons butter

9” baked pie crust

4 egg whites

¼ teaspoon cream of tartar

⅓ cup sugar

  

Make pie crust for one pie. Pre-bake pie crust 25 minutes at 375F until lightly tinged golden (won’t be uniform coloring).

  

In medium saucepan, combine 1 cup sugar and cornstarch. Gradually add water; stir until smooth. Stir in egg yolks.

  

Stirring constantly, bring to boil over medium heat and boil 1 minute. Remove from heat, stir in lemon zest, lemon juice, and butter. Pour filling into pie crust.

  

In medium bowl with mixer at high speed, beat egg whites with cream of tartar until soft peaks form. Gradually beat in remaining ⅓ cup sugar. Beat until stiff peaks form. Spread meringue evenly over hot filling, sealing to edge of crust.

  

Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until golden. Cool. Refrigerate.
