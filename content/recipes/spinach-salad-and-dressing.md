Salad:

  

1-2 bags spinach leaves

About ¾ to 1 cup grated Swiss cheese

About ½ to ¾ cup crumbled bacon pieces

  

Dressing:

  

¼ to ½ onion

1 tablespoon yellow mustard

1 teaspoon celery seeds

½ cup oil cup 

¼ cider vinegar

⅛ cup to ¼ cup sugar

  

Throw dressing ingredients in a blender and adjust to taste. Be careful about onion. It can be a strong flavor. Start with less, you can always add more.
