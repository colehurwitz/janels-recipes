6 firm apples, such as Granny Smith, Pink Lady

4 tablespoons butter, softened

1 cup sugar

Dash salt

Vanilla bean seeds

Juice of ½ lemon

  

1 sheet puff pastry, chilled (a circle sheet works better)

Whipped cream or vanilla ice cream for serving

  

Preheat oven to 425F. Peel and core the apples, then cut into quarters.

  

Spread the butter on the bottom of a 12 inch oven safe skillet. Mix sugar, salt, and vanilla together to evenly distribute the caviar. Sprinkle the sugar mixture evenly over the butter. Squeeze over the lemon juice.

  

Place apples cut side up around pan. Put the pan over medium heat and cook until the sugar melts, caramelizes, and begins to thicken. The apples will release their juices during this process - it could take 10-15 minutes. If juices start getting too dark, lower the heat a little.

  

While apples are cooking, take the puff pastry out of the fridge. Use a knife to cut a circle just big enough to fit over the top of the apples in the skillet. Keep chilled.

  

When the sugar has caramelized, place the pastry circle over the apples, gently tucking in the sides. 

  

Transfer to the oven and bake until the pastry is risen and golden brown, 15 to 20 minutes. Let rest 10 to 15 minutes.

  

Carefully turn the taste satin out onto a serving plate. Serve with whipped cream.
