Time: 1 ½ hours plus 14-20 hours rising.

  

3 cups all purpose or bread flour, more for dusting (155g/cup)

½ teaspoon instant yeast 

1 ¼ teaspoons salt

1 ⅝ cups water

Cornmeal or wheat bran as needed

  

In a large bowl, combine flour, yeast, and salt. Add 1 ⅝ cups water, and stir until blended; dough will be shaggy and sticky. Cover bowl with plastic wrap. Let dough rest at least 12 hours at warm room temperature, about 70F.

Dough is ready when the surface is dotted with bubbles. Place dough on lightly floured surface. Sprinkle it with a little more flour and fold it over on itself once or twice. Place on floured parchment paper. Cover loosely with cloth or plastic wrap and let rest for 15 minutes.

Using just enough flour to keep dough from sticking to work surface or to your fingers, gently and quickly shape dough into a ball. Generously coat a cotton towel and dust with more flour. Cover with another cotton towel and let rise for about 2 hours. Dough will be more than doubled in size.

Preheat oven to 450F. Put a 6-8qt heavy covered pot in oven as it heats. When dough is ready, carefully remove pot from oven.  Lift up parchment paper and fit into pot. It will straighten out as it bakes. Cover with lid and bake 30 minutes. Then remove lid, pull out parchment paper, and bake another 15-30 minutes until loaf is beautifully browned.
