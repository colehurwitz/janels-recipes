1 .25 ounce envelope active dry yeast

¼ cup warm  water

6 cups all purpose flour (768 grams)

⅞ cups white sugar

3 eggs

¼ cup melted butter, cooled

½ teaspoon salt

1 ¼ cups milk

  

In a small bowl, dissolve yeast in warm water with a pinch of the sugar. Set aside to ferment, about 10 minutes (it will get foamy). 

  

Transfer yeast to a large bowl, and stir in the sugar, eggs, salt, flour, and milk until the dough comes together. Stir in melted butter, then turn the dough out onto a floured surface, knead for about 10 minutes (or if using standing mixer, knead about 8 minutes using dough hook), until smooth and elastic. Cover dough with a cloth and set aside to rise until doubled in bulk, about 1-2 hours.

  

Divide dough into about 15-20 pieces, and shape them into flat round discs about ½ inch thick. Place a cloth on the table and dust it with flour. Arrange the cakes on the cloth, allowing space for rising. Allow them to set for 1 ½  hours.

  

Place the cakes on a ungreased griddle, and cook over low heat. Cook the cakes on each side until golden and internal temperature reaches 200F.
