2 ¾ - 3 cups flour

1 package yeast (2 ¼ teaspoons)

¼ teaspoon baking soda

1 cup buttermilk (can substitute 1 tablespoon lemon juice + milk)

¼ cup butter

¼ sugar

1 egg

⅓ cup brown sugar

1 generous tablespoon cinnamon

2 tablespoon butter softened

  

Combine 1 ½ cups flour, yeast, baking soda. Heat buttermilk, butter, sugar and salt until warm (115-120) stirring constantly. Add buttermilk mixture to dry ingredients. Add egg. Beat at low speed half a minute. Beat 3 minutes at high speed, stir in remaining flour. Knead about 5 minutes. Cover and let rest about 10 minutes. Roll into a 14x6 inch rectangle. Spread with butter, sugar and cinnamon. Fold in half lengthwise to 14x3 inches. Cut in fourteen 1 inch strips. Twist. Arrange on baking sheet, let rise one hour. Bake at 375F for 15 minutes. Cool. Frost with powdered sugar frosting while warm.

  

Powdered sugar frosting: can be found on box of C & H Confectioner’s sugar box, but it’s basically a mix of butter, confectioner’s sugar, milk, and vanilla extract. For sure, cut the recipe down that they have on the box because it makes way too much.

3 3/4 cups Confectioners Sugar
