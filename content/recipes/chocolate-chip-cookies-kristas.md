2 ¼ cups flour

1 teaspoon baking soda

¼ teaspoons baking powder

1 cup butter

¾ cup sugar

¾ cup brown sugar, packed

Dash salt

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla

2 cups chocolate chips

  

Combine flour, soda, and baking powder. Set aside. Melt butter in saucepan. Remove from heat. Stir in sugars and salt. Mix in eggs and vanilla. Stir in flour mixture until all ingredients are moistened. Let cool. Stir in chocolate chips. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours.

Bake at 375F, 9 to .11 minutes or until golden brown at edges and no longer wet on top.
