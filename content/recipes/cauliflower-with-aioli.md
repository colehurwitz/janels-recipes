1 head cauliflower, cut into florets

Steam/boil about 5 minutes until just tender

Beat 2 eggs and salt. 

  

Pour oil into pan and heat to about 355F. Dip florets into egg and place in oil. Brown on all sides. Drain on paper towels. Serve with aioli.

  

Aioli:

  

2 medium garlic cloves

Salt to taste

1 egg yolk

1 tablespoon lemon juice 

1 cup olive oil

  

Mince/smash garlic; add salt. Put egg yolk in mixing bowl. Put garlic paste in bowl. Whisk to blend. On low speed, pour olive oil in thin, steady stream until creamy sauce forms. Add lemon juice at end and keep mixing so it stays smooth.
