4 tablespoon olive oil

6 chicken thighs, skin removed

Salt and pepper to taste

1 large onion halved and thinly sliced

1 teaspoon turmeric

1 teaspoon ground ginger

½ teaspoon cinnamon

1 ½ cups large pitted prunes

  

Heat oil. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Place in pan, cook, turning once until browned. Transfer to plate. Add onions and ¼ cup water to pot; cook stirring to loosen browned bits on bottom. 

  

Add turmeric, ginger, and cinnamon; cook, stirring occasionally until onion is softened. Return chicken to pot. Add 2 cups water and prunes. Bring to boil. 

  

Reduce heat; partially cover and simmer until chicken is cooked through and very tender. 

  

Serve over rice.
