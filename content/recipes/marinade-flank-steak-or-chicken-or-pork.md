¾ cup oil

¼ cup soy sauce

1 tablespoon honey

2 tablespoons vinegar

1 ½ teaspoons garlic powder

1 teaspoon ginger powder

1 chopped onion

  

Marinate overnight. Slice thin on angle when cooked.
