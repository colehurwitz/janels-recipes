2 tablespoons butter

1 onion, diced

2 large garlic cloves, minced

8 ounces spinach, coarsely chopped

1 teaspoon salt

1 ¾ cup chicken stock 

1 c orzo

1 teaspoon lemon zest

¾ cup feta, crumbled

  

Melt butter, add onions, garlic. Cook about 3 minutes.

Add spinach and salt.

  

Stir in orzo, lemon juice, and stock. Simmer until orzo is done, about 10 to 15 minutes.

  

Add feta, cook 1 minute until done.

  

Serve\!
