1 cup plus 4 tablespoons butter, softened

1 ¾ cups powdered sugar, sifted

1 egg

2 ½ cups flour

½ cup unsweetened cocoa

1 teaspoon baking soda

  

Mix butter, sugar. Add egg. Add rest of ingredients. Divide dough into two parts. Flatten into disk and wrap. Refrigerate.

  

Preheat oven to 325F. Roll out and cut out shapes. 

  

Bake 8-10 minutes until lightly browned.
