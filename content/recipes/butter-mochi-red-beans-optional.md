1 stick butter, melted

2 cups whole milk

4 eggs

2 teaspoons vanilla

1 can coconut milk

1 lb (16 ounce bag) glutinous rice flour

2 teaspoons baking powder

1 ½ cups sugar

  

Optional: 

1 can sweetened red beans (but then add less sugar)

  

Preheat the oven to 350F. Spray pan with cooking spray. Melt butter. Combine 2 cups milk, eggs, and vanilla in a small bowl. Combine dry ingredients into a larger bowl. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients. Add melted butter and coconut milk. Stir until smooth.

  

Pour into prepared pan. Bake for 60-70 minutes. If the top starts looking too browned, lightly cover with foil.
