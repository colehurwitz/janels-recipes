For the vinaigrette (or just used bottled Italian):

  

2 ½ tablespoons red wine vinegar

2 tablespoons dried oregano

Freshly squeezed juice from ½ lemon (1 tablespoon) or more to taste

2 medium cloves garlic, smashed

½ teaspoon kosher salt

¼ teaspoon pepper

1 ½ cups olive oil

  

For the salad:

  

½ small red onion, cut in half from top to bottom

1 head iceberg lettuce

1 head radicchio

1 pint small, sweet cherry tomatoes

1 ½ cups chickpeas, drained and rinsed

½ pound provolone, (1/8 inch thick slices, then 1/4 inch wide strips)

¼ pound salami, (1/8 thick slices, 1/4 inch wide strips)

5 peperoncini, cut into thin slices

Freshly squeezed juice for ½ lemon (1 tablespoon)

Dried oregano if desired

Sunflower seeds, roasted and salted

  

For the vinaigrette: Whisk together vinegar, oregano, lemon juice, smashed garlic, salt, and pepper. in a bowl. Whisk constantly to form an emulsified vinaigrette. Taste for seasoning; add salt or lemon juice as needed.

  

For the salad: Separate the layers of the onion and stack two or three layers on top of one another, then cut them lengthwise into 1/16 inch wide strips. Repeat with the remaining onion layers. Place the onion in a small bowl of ice water to sit while you prepare the rest of the ingredients. Drain the onion and pat dry before adding to salad.

  

Cut the iceberg lettuce in half. Remove core; discard outer leaves. Cut into bite sized pieces. Thinly slice radicchio. Cut tomatoes in half, season them with salt if desired. Combine lettuce, radicchio, tomatoes, chickpeas, provolone, salami, peperoncini, and onion in large bowl. Drizzle vinaigrette over salad. Toss gently to coat. Taste and add lemon juice or additional vinaigrette if desired. Sprinkle oregano and sunflower seeds on top.
