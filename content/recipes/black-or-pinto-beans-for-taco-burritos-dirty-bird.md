Start this around 1 p.m. for a 5 to 6 p.m. dinner. You can cut the time in half by soaking the beans the night before. I usually don’t presoak only because I don’t plan that far in advance\!

  

1 bag of dried pinto or black beans

Sort through beans to remove any stones/pebbles you may find.

  

3 quarts (12 cups) water (to start - you will add water as you go)

  

Put beans in large pot (6 quart pot or larger)

  

Add 6-7 good size whole garlic cloves.

  

Bring to boil, uncovered.

  

Boil until water starts to turn color of beans. Add salt to taste. Start with 2 teaspoons, but keep tasting water to see if it’s the right amount of salt. Add less salt rather than more as you can always add salt later but can’t take it out\!

  

Boil uncovered (gentle boil, but not simmer) for 2 hours, stirring from time to time, and keeping an eye on the water level. You likely will need to add water. After about 3 hours, taste a bean to see how cooked it is. You judge how much water you need based on how done the bean is and how much water is left in the pot. If it’s crunchy and the water level is low, you want to add 2-4 cups of water. Water, along with boiling, is what softens the beans. You may need to add water multiple times during the cooking process.

  

Once the beans are soft, and the water level is quite reduced, you need to stir more often so they don’t burn. Once the beans are soft, you don’t want to add any more water, you just want to cook them until most of the water is gone and the beans look ready to you. They can be whole bean style or the beans can break down into more of a refried looking bean. But be sure to stir because they’ll start to stick to the bottom of the pot.

  

Enjoy\!
