2 cups thick yogurt

1 cup or so cucumber, finely chopped

1 clove garlic, crushed or chopped finely

2 teaspoons olive oil

Fresh or dried dill to taste

1 tablespoon lemon juice

  

To thicken yogurt, place 3 1/2 cups yogurt mixed with 1 teaspoon salt in dish towel (or start with Greek yogurt and skip these yogurt related steps\!). Hang over bowl until dripping stops, about 2 hours. Sprinkle salt over cucumber; let stand 15 minutes and press dry. Combine all ingredients. Chill.
