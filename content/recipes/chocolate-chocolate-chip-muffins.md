4 ounces semi-sweet chocolate

⅓ cup butter

1 cup buttermilk

½ cup white sugar

1 egg

1 ½ teaspoons vanilla extract

1 ⅔ cups flour

1 teaspoon baking soda

Dash salt

1 cup mini semi-sweet chocolate chips

  

Preheat oven to 400F. Line 12 muffin cups with paper. In a small saucepan over low heat, melt the semi-sweet chocolate with butter, cool. Lightly beat the egg. In a small bowl, stir together the buttermilk, sugar, egg and vanilla until blended well. In a large bowl stir together flour, soda, and salt. Make a hole in the center of the dry ingredients, pour in the chocolate mixture, and stir until just combined. Stir in the mini chocolate chips, spoon the batter into the lined muffin cups. 

  

Bake at 400F for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
