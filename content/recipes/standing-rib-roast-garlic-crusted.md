1 standing rib roast

20-30 cloves peeled garlic

2-3 tablespoon kosher salt

2-3 tablespoon freshly cracked black pepper

1-2 tablespoon olive oil

  

Preheat oven to 450F. Place garlic cloves, salt, pepper in food processor. Process until chunky paste is reached. Drizzle in olive oil and process. Place rib roast fat side up on rack in roasting pan. Pat dry with paper towels. Rub garlic paste over all surfaces, distributing evenly. You can let it sit for a couple hours if you like. 

  

Bake at 450F for 30 minutes. Reduce heat to 350F and continue to bake 12-15 minutes per pound or until center of roast reaches 125-130 for rare/medium rare. Remove from heat, cover and let rest for 30-35 minutes before carving. INTERNAL TEMPERATURE WILL CONTINUE TO RISE 10-12 DEGREES.

  
  

TENDERLOIN ROAST - (same recipe)

  

9 pounds tenderloin

  

Dry meat with paper towel. Rub with salt liberally. Remove excess fluid. Let come to room temperature. 

  

Heat cast iron pan with avocado oil. Heat oil to smoking. Sear on all sides.

  

Rub garlic, salt, pepper, olive oil paste liberally.

  

Put in oven.

  

Cook to internal temperature of 120F.
