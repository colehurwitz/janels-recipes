category: "Miscellaneous"

---

8 cups chopped apricots

½ cup lemon juice

6 cups sugar

½ tablespoon butter

  

Put ingredients in big pot.  Bring to boil.  Boil for about 20 minutes until reaches desired consistency (maybe 145 degrees?). Pour into prepared jars to ¼ inch from top.  Boil in canner for 10 minutes.  Remove.  Let cool.
