Marinade:

  

6 tablespoons olive oil

3 garlic cloves, peeled and minced

1 teaspoon salt

2 tablespoons lemon juice

  

In a large, sealable plastic bag, combine the marinade ingredients. Add fish. Seal bag and turn to coat. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes or up to 2 hours.

  

In a large, flat skillet, pour a little olive oil in pan; tilt to spread evenly. Warm over high heat until 

smoking, then add fish fillets. Brush top and sides with marinade. Cook until seared, about 3 minutes. Turn and sear other side, about 3 more minutes. Brush top and sides with marinade again. Reduce heat; cook until centers of fillets are just opaque, 2 to 4 minutes more on each side, depending on thickness. 

  

Serve\!
