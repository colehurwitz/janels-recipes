1 ¼ cups powdered sugar, sifted to remove lumps

1 cup cake flour

1 ½ cups egg whites (12-14 egg whites)

1 ½ teaspoons cream of tartar

1 ½ teaspoons vanilla extract

¼ teaspoon almond extract

1 cup sugar

  

Preheat oven to 375F. In a small bowl, sift confectioners sugar and cake flour; set aside.

Add egg whites, cream of tartar, vanilla, salt, and almond extract to large bowl; beat at high speed until soft peaks form. Beating at high speed, sprinkle in sugar, 2 tablespoons at a time; beat just until sugar dissolves and whites form stiff peaks. Do not scrape bowl during beating.

  

With rubber spatula, fold in flour mixture, about ¼ at a time, just until flour disappears. Pour mixture into UNGREASED 10 inch tube pan and cut through batter with a knife to break any large air bubbles.

  

Bake about 30 minutes or until top of cake springs back when lightly touched with finger. Any cracks on surface should look dry. Invert cake pan; cool completely. Loosen cake from pan and remove to plate.
