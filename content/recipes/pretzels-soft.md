2 packages active yeast

1 ½ cups warm water

2 tablespoons sugar

1 teaspoon salt

4 cups flour

1 egg, beaten with fork

  

Mix yeast and warm water in bowl. Add sugar and salt. Add flour, mix. Knead dough until smooth. Cut off pieces of dough to roll into a pretzel or bread sticks.

Place them on a greased cookie sheet. Brush with egg, then salt them, if desired. No need to let them rise. Bake at 425F for 12-15 minutes.
