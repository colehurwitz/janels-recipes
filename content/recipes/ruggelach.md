1 cup softened butter

6 ounces cream cheese

2 ¾ cups flour

dash salt

Filling:

¾ cups semi-sweet mini chocolate chips

¾ cups finely chopped walnuts

½ cup sugar

1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa

½ teaspoon cinnamon

  

Glaze:

1 beaten egg yolk

¼ cup water

¼ cup sugar

  

Cream butter and cream cheese until light and fluffy. Add flour and salt; knead until well-mixed. Wrap in waxed paper or plastic and chill for 2 hours.

Prepare the filling by mixing all ingredients together.

  

When dough has chilled, divide dough into 9 pieces. Work with one at a time, keeping the rest refrigerated. Roll each piece into an 8” circle. Sprinkle dough with about 1/9 of filling. Press filling into pastry. Cut circle into 8 pie shaped wedges. Starting at base of each wedge, roll to the point to form horn-shaped pastries. Place each ruggelach, point side down, on an ungreased baking pan. When almost done, preheat oven to 375F. Brush tops with glaze and sprinkle with sugar. Bake 15 minutes or until golden brown.
