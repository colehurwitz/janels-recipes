300-330 grams GF flour, 1-1 type (she uses Namaste brand from Costco, needs  xanthan gum)

100 grams sugar

30 grams powdered sugar

5-8 grams salt (Redmond)

1 cup unsalted butter, melted

1 teaspoon almond extract

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

65 grams chopped pecans

50 grams semi-sweet chocolate chunks chopped

  

Instructions:

  

Mix dry ingredients in a large bowl until well blended. Melt butter and add vanilla and almond extracts. Pour butter mixture into dry ingredients and mix well. The dough should just come together but not be smoothly blended. Turn dough out on parchment and shape into a square (or round) log. Wrap in parchment and cool in the refrigerator until firm.

  

Pre-heat oven to 375.

  

Remove dough from fridge and slice into ½ thick cookies and place on parchment lined baking tray. Reduce oven heat to 300, place cookies in oven and bake about 45 minutes until very slightly golden. Remove and let cool on a wire rack. Try not to eat them all\! Can be frozen.
