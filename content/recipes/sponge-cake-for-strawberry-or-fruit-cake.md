⅓ cup (40 g) flour

½ cup (60 g) cornstarch

¼ teaspoon baking powder

¼ teaspoon salt

4 large eggs (whites and yolks separated)

⅛ teaspoon vinegar

⅔ cup (132 g) sugar (divided in half; ⅓ cup for egg white, ⅓ cup for egg yolks)

1 ½ tablespoons warm water

2 teaspoons vanilla

2 tablespoons neutral vegetable oil (canola, safflower)

  

Preheat oven to 320F and line the bottom of a 9” springform pan with parchment paper. DO NOT GREASE SIDES OR BOTTOM.

  

In a bowl, combine flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt. Set aside

  

In a medium sized bowl, add the egg whites and white vinegar, and whip until soft peaks. Gradually add ⅓ cup of the sugar and then continue mixing to stiff peaks. 

  

In a large bowl, add ⅓ cup sugar and warm water. Mix so that the sugar is coated in the water. Add in the egg yolks and vanilla and with a mixer, whip on medium high speed for 4 minutes, then turn the mixer down to low and mix another minute. Your mixture should now be thick and you should be able to create ribbons.

  

Stream in the vegetable oil on medium speed.

  

Add in half of egg whites to the yolk mixture, and using a spatula fold together until combined.

  

Sift in your dry ingredients and gently fold until combined.

  

Add in remaining egg whites and fold until combined.

  

Put batter in tin and tap lightly to remove large air bubble.

  

Bake about 25 minutes. It’s done when you gently touch tops and it indents and slowly bounces back. Turn upside down on a wire rack to completely cool.
