240 grams strong flour

10 grams buckwheat flour

5 grams caster sugar

5 grams salt

30 grams fresh yeast

200 milliliters warm milk

100 milliliters + 75 milliliters warm water

½  teaspoon baking soda

Vegetable oil

  

To make the crumpets, place all dry ingredients, along with 5 grams salt, the yeast, milk and 100 milliliters warm water, into the bowl of a stand mixer and mix with a paddle for 5 minutes. Remove the bowl and leave to rest, covered with clingfilm, for 1 hour in a warm place. Then fold in 75 milliliters warm water and the baking soda.

  

Heat a non-stick frying pan over a medium heat, place 8 8 centimeter buttered rings into the pan and drizzle a little oil into each ring. Once hot, divide the mix between the rings and gently cook until the bubbles set on top and the crumpets feel firm enough to flip. Once turned, cook for a minute or so and drain the crumpets using a clean kitchen towel. When ready to serve, heat in oven or toaster.
