1 head cauliflower, chopped into florets and boiled until tender, but not overcooked. Strain water.

Put in food processor, Add ⅛ cup milk/cream, generous ½ cup Parmesan cheese, ½ cup Romano cheese, salt. Blend. Adjust as needed for texture and taste (so it’s not too dry - also may need more salt)

  

You could do this in a blender as well.
