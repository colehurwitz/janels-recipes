1 pound blueberries

⅓ cup sugar

1 ½ teaspoon lemon juice

1 teaspoon lemon zest

pinch salt

  

Cook blueberries and sugar over moderate heat until blueberries begin to burst, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat. Use potato masher to smash about half of the blueberries. Stir in lemon juice, zest, and salt. Let cool.
