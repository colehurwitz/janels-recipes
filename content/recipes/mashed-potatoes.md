6 good sized potatoes (not small)

⅛ cup whole milk or half and half (you may need more)

1 teaspoon salt (you may need more)

½ cup sour cream (you may need more)

¾ stick butter

  

Boil potatoes until a fork goes in easily. Drain water. But butter in bottom of bowl. Put hot potatoes on top of butter. Using a beater, break up potatoes (you run the beater, not just hit it with the beater\!). Add sour cream, milk, salt). Whip together with beater. Now taste. Is it too dry? Add milk. Does it need salt? Does it need more of a tangy flavor? Add sour cream. Enjoy\!
