2 cups whipping cream

2 cups lebne (middle eastern yogurt)

½ cup sugar

2 tablespoons gelatin

½ teaspoon rose water

Caramel:

5 tablespoons sugar

2 tablespoons water

2 cups sugar syrup

  

Soften gelatin over ½ cup milk. In a saucepan over medium heat, warm milk and cream. Stir in labneh. Add sugar, gelatin mixture, and rose water, stirring until well-combined; do not boil. Remove from heat and set aside. 

  

Divide mixture among small ramekins. Refrigerate at least 2 hours.

  

Meanwhile make caramel. In a saucepan over medium heat, cook sugar and water until lightly browned. Let cool. Add syrup to caramel and bring to a boil over medium heat. Remove from heat and set aside. Garnish with rose petal jam and raspberries. Sprinkle with ground pistachios.
