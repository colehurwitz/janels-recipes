½ cup olive oil

4 carrots, thinly sliced

1 onion, chopped

1 teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon pepper

1 teaspoon cumin

1 teaspoon turmeric

½ head cabbage, chopped

5 potatoes, peeled and cut into 1 inch cubes

  

Heat olive oil in skillet over medium heat. Cook carrots and onion in the oil, about 5 minutes. Stir in spices - consider adding extra if you desire. Add cabbage, cook another 15-20 minutes. Add potatoes; cover. Reduce heat to medium low and cook until potatoes are soft, about 20-30 minutes. Season to taste.
