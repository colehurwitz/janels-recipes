Use Springform pan, spray.

  

¾ cup (90g) flour

¼ cup (24g) Almond flour

1 teaspoon baking powder

Dash salt

6 tablespoons (85g) melted butter

2 large eggs

¾ cup (160g) brown sugar (packed)

½ teaspoon almond extract\*

3 large apples, peeled and sliced about ¼” thick (550g or 41/2 cups)

  

\*You can vary this flavor. You could use cinnamon instead. Or 1 teaspoon vanilla and 3 tablespoons rum. I’m sure there are other options\!

  

Preheat oven to 350F. 

  

Weigh your flours. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flours, baking powder, and salt.

In a large gowl, whisk together the melted butter, eggs, sugar, and extract until creamy, lighter in color, and slightly foamy, about 30 seconds to a minute. Whisk in dry ingredients just until combined.

  

Use a rubber spatula to fold the apples into the batter until evenly coated (it will look like there’s not much batter, but don’t panic).

  

Scrape batter into the pan and use your spatula to nudge the apples into a mostly even layer.

  

Bake until golden-brown on top and a toothpick or paring knife inserted into the center comes out clean, about 50 to 55 minutes. 

  

Remove the cake and let cool for 15 minutes on a wire rack. Run a knife around the edges of the pan and release sides of an. Let cool for 15 minutes more. Use serrated knife to cut cake into pieces because it can be difficult to cut through apple pieces with a dull knife\!
