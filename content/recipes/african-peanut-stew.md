1 tablespoon olive oil

1 onion, finely chopped

1 jalapeño, cored, seeded, and finely chopped

4 garlic cloves, minced

2 inch knob ginger, peeled and minced

2 teaspoons cumin

¼ teaspoon cayenne

3 tablespoons tomato paste

1 large sweet potato, peeled and cut into 1” cubes

½ cup unsweetened peanut butter

4 cups broth

1 cup water

1 bunch greens, chopped, stems removed

fresh cilantro, cooked brown rice, roasted peanuts and lime juice for serving

  

In a large pot over medium heat, warm the olive oil. Add onion, sprinkle with salt, and cook for 3 minutes until translucent. Add garlic, jalapeño, ginger, cumin, and cayenne and stir together for about 2 minutes.

  

Add tomato paste and stir together. Add sweet potato, peanut butter, broth, and water. Stir together and then bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover and cook for 15 minutes. Add chopped greens to the pot, stir, and continue to cook another 15 minutes until the sweet potato is tender.

Mash some of the sweet potato to help thicken the broth. Boil uncovered for 5 minutes. Serve warm with garnishes.
