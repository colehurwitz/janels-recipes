Cookies:    Filling:

1 cup unsalted butter  2 tablespoons butter  

Dash salt    1 ½ cups confectioner’s sugar

¼ cup granulated sugar  Dash salt

½ cup maple sugar   2 tablespoons maple syrup

1 teaspoon maple flavor  ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

2 ¼ cups flour   ¼ teaspoon maple flavor

¾ cups almond flour  1 tablespoon milk, optional if needed

  

Beat together butter, salt, sugars, and maple flavor. Add the two flours and mix just until the dough comes together. Divide the dough in half, wrap each half in plastic, and chill for 2 hours.

  

Preheat oven to 300F. Remove one piece of dough from the refrigerator. Scoop scant to full tablespoonfuls of dough onto one of the baking sheets. Lightly dip the tops of each piece of dough in flour then dip a cookie stamp in flour. Center the stamp over a piece of dough and press it down until the stamp meets the baking sheet. Remove the stamp, tapping the edge firmly against the pan if the dough sticks. Bake the cookies for 23 to 28 minutes until they begin to brown. Remove the cookies from the oven, cool for a few minutes. Transfer to rack and cool completely.

  

To make filling, mix together all the filling ingredients except the milk, stirring until smooth. Add milk if necessary. Spread the bottom half of the cookies with filling. Top with another cookie.
