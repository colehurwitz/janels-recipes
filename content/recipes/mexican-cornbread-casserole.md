1 pound hamburger cooked with taco seasoning

Chili powder, cumin, garlic powder to taste

1 onion chopped and sautéedi

1 large can (28oz) whole tomatoes drained and chopped

1 can corn

1 can chopped chilies

1 can pinto beans

1 can sliced olives

1 cup cubed Monterey Jack cheese

Prepare a batch of cornbread (uncooked)

  

Cook hamburger, drain, remove from pan. Cook onions. Add hamburger, taco seasoning, beans, chilies, olives, corn, tomatoes. 

  

Pour into 9x13 pan add cheese; top with cornbread batter. 

  

Bake at 375F for 30 minutes.
