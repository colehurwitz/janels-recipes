Tall, tender pancakes for a slow weekend morning.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1¼ cups buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp melted butter

## Directions

1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet into the dry and stir just until combined — leave it lumpy.
4. Cook ¼-cup scoops on a buttered griddle over medium heat.
5. Flip when bubbles form and the edges look set; cook another minute.
