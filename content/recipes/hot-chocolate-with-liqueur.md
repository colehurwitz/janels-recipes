4 cups milk

6 ounce dark chocolate chips

1 to 2 teaspoons vanilla

2 ounces rum

2 ounces Kahlua

Marshmallows

  

Heat mugs. Warm milk, chocolate, vanilla. Whisk until smooth. Add liqueur, still until blended. Top with marshmallows. Serve\!
