1 cup brown sugar

6 tablespoons milk

1 teaspoon orange peel

Dash salt

¼ teaspoon cinnamon

1 teaspoon vanilla

3 cups walnuts

  

Combine ingredients in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat to 236F (softball). Remove from heat. Add 1 teaspoon vanilla and 3 cups of walnuts. Stir until mixture changes from shiny to dull. Separate the nuts on sheets of waxed paper. Cool.
