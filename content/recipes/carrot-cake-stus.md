1 cup oil (safflower)

1 cup sugar

4 eggs

2 cups flour

2 teaspoon baking soda

½ teaspoon baking powder

2 teaspoon cinnamon

Dash salt

3 cups carrots, grated finely, pressed down into measuring cup

Nuts and raisins, optional (½ cup each)

  

Combine oil and sugar. Add eggs. Beat until light and fluffy. Mix in carrots. Add flour, baking soda, baking powder, dash of salt, and cinnamon. Mix in optional raisins and nuts.

Bake in a 9x13 pan that is greased, at 350F for about 25 to 30 minutes until done. When cool, frost using recipe below.

  

Cream Cheese Frosting: (This is the banana cake frosting recipe, too\!)

8 ounces cream cheese, room temperature

4 tablespoon butter, softened

1 3/4 cups powdered sugar, sifted to remove lumps

1 teaspoon vanilla

Juice of 1 lemon (to taste)

Lemon zest

  

Mix cream cheese and butter. Add vanilla, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Mix in powdered sugar. You may want less or more sugar; more or less lemon juice - taste it and see what you think\!
