1 pound pasta

6 tablespoons unsalted butter

12 garlic cloves, finely chopped (about ⅓ cup)

Salt and pepper

¼ cup gochujang paste

¼ cup honey

¼ cup sherry vinegar or rice vinegar

  

Bring large pot to boil. Add pasta and cook. Reserve 1 cup cooking water. Drain pasta and return it to pot.

  

While the pasta cooks, melt 4 tablespoons butter in a skillet over medium low heat. Add the garlic and season generously with salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the garlic starts to soften but not brown, about 1-3 minutes.

  

Stir in the gochujang, honey, and vinegar, and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring constantly until the mixture reduces significantly, 3-4 minutes; when you drag a spatula across the bottom of the pan, it should leave behind a trail that stays put for about 3 seconds. Remove from heat.

  

Transfer the sauce to the pot with the pasta and add the remaining 2 tablespoons butter. Vigorously stir until the butter melts. Add splashed of pasta cooking water as needed to thin out the sauce. Taste and season with salt and pepper. Top with scallions if desired.

  

Serve immediately\!
