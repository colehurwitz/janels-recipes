2 cups dried black beans

10 cups hot water

1 medium onion, chopped

4 garlic cloves (can use whole cloves or mince garlic)

1 teaspoon salt

Whole onion, chopped

1 tablespoon olive oil 

5 tomatoes peeled, chopped

½ teaspoon salt

  

Place beans in large stockpot. Rinse beans; drain. Add 10 cups hot water, onion, garlic. Bring to boil. Cover and simmer 2-3 hours. Add 1 teaspoon salt. Simmer 2 hours longer or until very tender. Cool beans in their liquid. Puree in blender. Press through a sieve to remove skins. Return to large stockpot. In a medium saucepan, cook chopped onion in oil until translucent. Add tomatoes. Cook until softened. Puree tomato mixture in blender. Add to beans with ½ teaspoon salt. Taste and adjust seasonings. Simmer 15 minutes longer.
