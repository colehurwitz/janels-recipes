1 medium onion chopped

2 cups celery chopped

5 ½ tablespoons butter, divided

¼ cup flour

3 cups milk

2 cans tuna, drained

4 tablespoons soy sauce

1 tablespoon lemon juice

1 teaspoon dill

10 ounces noodles

Potato chips

2 cups grated cheddar cheese

1 tablespoon butter

  

Cook onion and celery in 1 ½ tablespoons butter in a skillet over low heat, stirring until softened. Place onions and celery in a large mixing bowl. Add 4 tablespoons butter to pan. Melt. Whisk in flour. Add milk, bring to a boil while whisking. Continue to simmer and whisk until thickened 5 minutes. 

  

Add to bowl with onions and celery. Fold in tuna, soy sauce, lemon juice, and dill. Cook noodles. Drain well and add to tuna mixture gently folding in noodles.

  

Transfer mixture to baking pan, sprinkle with grated cheese and potato chips.

  

Bake at 375F until top is brown and bubbling, about 30 to 35 minutes.
