6 tablespoons butter

1 pound top sirloin, cut into 1x2.5 inch strips

⅓ cup shallots or 1 small onion

½ pound crimini or regular mushrooms

Salt to taste

Pepper to taste

½ teaspoon dry tarragon

1 cup sour cream, at room temperature

Garlic

¼ cup white wine

  

Melt 3 tablespoons butter in a large skillet on medium heat. Increase the heat to medium/high and add the strips of beef. Cook quickly, browning both sides. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Take the beef out and place it in a bowl. 

  

In the same pan, reduce the heat, add the shallots/onions. Cook the shallots/onions for a minute or two. Remove the shallots and place them in the bowl with the beef. In the same pan, melt 3 tablespoons butter, increase the heat to medium/high and cook the mushrooms. Cook for four minutes. Sprinkle with tarragon. 

  

Reduce the heat to low and add the sour cream to the mushrooms. You may want to add a tablespoon or two of water to thin the sauce. Mix the sour cream thoroughly, do not let it curdle (so do not boil it). Mix sauce into beef/onion mix. 

  

Serve immediately over noodles.
