3 tablespoons olive oil

1 yellow onion, finely chopped

4 garlic cloves, finely chopped

Salt to taste

1 6 ounce can tomato paste

¼ cup vodka

½ teaspoon red pepper flakes

2 15 ounce cans white beans (cannellini or Great Northern) or chickpeas, drained but not rinsed. 

1 bunch Kale, ribs removed, leaves torn or coarsely chopped

Cashew crema to taste (put ¾ cup cashews in blender and cover with water, let sit 30 minutes, grind into creamy sauce)

  

Grated parmesan as needed

  

In dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, season with salt, and cook until softened, 3-5 minutes. 

  

Add the tomato paste, vodka, and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring until the tomato paste is a shade darker and starts to stick to the bottom of the pot, 2-3 minutes.

  

Add 2 cups water, the beans and the kale, season with salt and pepper and bring to a simmer. Reduce heat to maintain a simmer, cover, and cook until the liquid is flavorful and the kale is tender, 7-10 minutes.

  

Remove from heat, stir in cashew cream. Adjust seasonings. Stir in some parmesan. Serve with more parmesan on top.
