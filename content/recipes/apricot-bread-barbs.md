Soak 1 cup dried apricots in warm water for 30 minutes. Drain and cut into quarter pieces with scissors. Mix thoroughly ¾ cup sugar, 2 tablespoons soft butter, and one egg. Stir in ¼ cup water, ½ cup orange juice. 

  

Stir in 2 cups flour, 2 teaspoons baking powder, ¼ teaspoon baking soda, and apricots. Line bottom of greased loaf pan with waxed paper. 

  

Pour batter and let stand for 20 minutes. Bake at 350F for 55-65 minutes. Remove paper immediately.
