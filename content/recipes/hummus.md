2 - 15 ounce cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed

⅓ cup olive oil

5-6 medium cloves garlic, chopped finely

¼ cup lemon juice

3 tablespoons tahini

Salt to taste

May add cumin if desired

¼ cup water to reach desired thickness

  

Sauté garlic in olive oil. Place half the chickpeas and olive oil in a blender. Pulse several times, stopping to stir as needed. When mixture is almost pureed and creamy, add the remaining chickpeas and oil, lemon juice, tahini, garlic, cumin if desired, and salt to taste. Pulse until very creamy. Taste and adjust seasonings. May need more lemon juice. Chill before serving.
