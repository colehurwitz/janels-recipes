9x13 pan, grease with butter and dust with powdered sugar. Sprinkle 3 envelopes Knox gelatin (3 tablespoons) over ½ cup cold water in bowl of mixer. Let stand 10 minutes.

  

Meanwhile mix 2 cups sugar, ⅔ cup corn syrup, and ¼ cup water in pan. Cook over low heat until sugar is dissolved. Increase heat to medium. Allow to boil until it reaches 250F.

  

Run mixer at low while drizzling in boiled syrup. Then turn up the mixer speed a little and add dash of salt. When mixture begins to fluff, scape down bowl and turn up speed to high. After it is big and fluffy, add 1 teaspoon vanilla. Then stop mixer (takes about 8 minutes).

  

Pour into pan and smooth. Cool and let set at room temperature. Turn over cutting board covered in powdered sugar. Cut and serve\!
