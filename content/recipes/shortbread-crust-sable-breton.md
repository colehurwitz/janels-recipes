For 8x8, 9x9, or 9” round:                       For 9x13:

1 stick butter, softened                             1 ½ sticks butter

⅓ cup sugar (65g)                                     ½ cup sugar (98g)

½ teaspoon vanilla                                     ¾ teaspoon vanilla

pinch salt                                                    pinch salt

1 cup+2 tablespoons flour (134g)              1 ½ +3 tablespoons flour (200g) 

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Oil pan or line with parchment. In stand mixer, combine all ingredients at once. Mixture will be crumbly. Using damp hands, transfer to prepared pan and pat down evenly. Poke with fork.

  

Bake 20-23 minutes until edges are lightly browned. If filling will not need to be baked, bake for 30-33 minutes. Let cool completely.
