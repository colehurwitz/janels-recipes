1 head cauliflower

2 cups 2% milk

3 tablespoons butter

3 tablespoons flour

Salt, pepper to taste

1 cup grated cheddar cheese

1 ½ tablespoons butter

½ tablespoon dijon mustard

1 cup breadcrumbs

  

Cut cauliflower into florets. Boil until al dente, about 3 minutes. Immerse in cold water to stsop cooking. 

  

Melt butter in pan; add flour. Whisk in milk. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook until it thickens.

  

Spread cauliflower in 9x13 pan. Pour milk mixture over cauliflower. Sprinkle generous 1 cup grated cheddar cheese on top.

  

Melt 1 ½ tablespoons butter. Mix in ½ tablespoon dijon mustard. Add 1 cup breadcrumbs. Stir until browned. Sprinkle on top of cheese.  

  

Bake at 375F for about 20 minutes.
