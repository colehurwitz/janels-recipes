1 cup orzo

1 pound asparagus, tried and thinly sliced on diagonal, about 1/4”

5 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

1 teaspoon lemon zest plus 3 tablespoons lemon juice

½ cup panko bread crumbs

1 garlic clove, finely grated

¼ cup finely grated Parmesan, plus more for serving

  

Bring a medium pot of salted water to a boil. Add the orzo and cook until al dente, according to package directions. Two minutes before the orzo is done, add the asparagus. Drain the orzo and asparagus. Wipe out and reserve the pot.

  

While the orzo and asparagus cook, make the dressing: in a large bowl (into which you will put the entire dish), stir together 3 tablespoons oil and the lemon zest and juice; season to taste with salt and pepper. Add the drained orzo and asparagus and toss to coat. Set aside while you toast the bread crumbs.

  

In the reserved pot, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium heat. Add the panko and cook, stirring until golden brown, 3 to 5 minutes. Remove from heat, then stir in the garlic and season with salt and pepper. 

  

Stir the Parmesan into the orzo. Taste and season.
