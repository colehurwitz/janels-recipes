1 liter aquavit

1 bottle Claret or red wine

10 cardamoms

5 cloves

3 orange peels

4 figs

1 cup blanched almonds

1 cup raisins

1 cinnamon stick

½ pound sugar

  

Pour spirits into kettle. Add remaining ingredients except sugar. Cover and heat slowly to boiling point. Remove from heat. Pour glogg over sugar until sugar melts (set glogg on fire). Cool. Keep in well corked bottles. Heat before serving but do not boil.
