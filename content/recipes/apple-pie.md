4-6 apples peeled and sliced 1/8-1/4” thick

2 tablespoons lemon juice

¼ cup brown sugar

¼-½ cup white sugar

¼ cup flour

1 tablespoon cinnamon

  

Toss apples in lemon juice, brown sugar, white sugar, flour and cinnamon. Bake for 30 minutes at 425F with foil; remove foil and bake an additional 30 minutes.

  

To transform into a crumble, prepare apples as above but don’t toss with white sugar and flour (toss with remaining ingredients). For topping, combine:

  

1 ½ cups flour

1 cup brown sugar

1 cup oats

1 cup chopped pecans (optional)

12 tablespoons butter melted (substitute half coconut oil)

  

Sprinkle over apples.

  

Bake at 350F for 1 hour.
