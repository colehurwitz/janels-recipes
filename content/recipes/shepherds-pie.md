Prepare mashed potatoes (about 8 potatoes, sour cream, milk, salt, all to taste - whip in standing mixer. You can leave potato peels on)

  

2 tablespoons olive oil

1 pound ground lamb

1 large carrot, diced

1 large onion, diced

Bag frozen peas

Fresh rosemary

Fresh thyme

4 cloves minced garlic

Salt

Pepper

Several splashes Worcestershire sauce

2 tablespoons tomato paste

¼ to ½ cup red wine

¼ cup chicken stock

  

Cook meat. Add spices and veggies, Worcestershire sauce, tomato paste, wine and broth. Simmer together to soften vegetables.

  

Scoop into casserole; put mashed potatoes over top and sprinkle with parmesan cheese. 

  

Bake at 375F, 18-20 minutes.
