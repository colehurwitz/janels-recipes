6 eggs

@1 cup flour

  

Add flour and beat until it is the consistency of a Fenton’s milkshake (on the thin side). Add about 2 cups milk and slowly continue beating. Batter will get to consistency of thick whip cream (when unwhipped)

Dash salt

  

Add: 1 teaspoon vanilla

½ cup sugar

½ cup melted butter

  

Cook like crepes.
