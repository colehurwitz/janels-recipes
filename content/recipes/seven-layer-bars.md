½ cup butter

1 cup graham cracker crumbs

1 14 ounce can sweetened condensed milk

1 cup shredded coconut

6 ounce package semi-sweet chocolate chips

6 ounce package butterscotch chips

1 cup chopped walnuts

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Melt butter in 9x13 pan. Sprinkle graham cracker crumbs over butter; tap sides to distribute. Pour sweetened condensed milk evenly over crumbs. Sprinkle on coconut, then chocolate, and then butterscotch chips. Top with walnuts. Press lightly into pan. 

  

Bake 25-30 minutes. Cool in pan.
