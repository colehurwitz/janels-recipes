1 cup sugar

6 tablespoon butter

Dash salt

½  teaspoon vanilla

½  cup whipping cream

  

Heat sugar over medium heat. Heat until melted and brown at edges, about 3 minutes. Pull melted sugar toward center until all sugar is melted and amber, about 3 more minutes.

Immediately whisk in butter, salt, and vanilla. Whisk in cream.

Done\!

BOBBY FLAY’S SALTED CARAMEL SAUCE

  

1 cup sugar

¼ cup water

½ cup whipping cream

2 tablespoons butter

Salt to taste

  

In a saucepan, combine sugar and water; stir to combine. Over medium heat, cook without stirring until sugar has reached a deep amber color, about 10-12 minutes. Warm up cream. When the sugar reaches the proper color, stir in the warm cream and mix until smooth, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat. Add 2 tablespoons butter, stir until mixed. Add salt to taste (recipe says ¾ teaspoon, but I used ¼ teaspoon).
