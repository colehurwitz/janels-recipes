Cornbread (6 cups?)

Onions (1 onion sautéed)

Pork sausage (¾ pound cooked)

Apples (1 diced and cooked with onion)

Butter (2 tablespoons? use to cook onions and apples)

Celery (3 stalks, diced, cooked with onion and apple)

Basil

Thyme

Sage

Marjoram

Savory

Salt, pepper

  

Mix all ingredients together in bowl. Bake at 350F until nice and warm. Add 1 egg and some chicken broth if too dry (before baking).

  

Corn Bread - Buttermilk - New Seasons

1 ½ cups cornmeal

1 ½ cups flour

2 ½ teaspoons baking powder

½ teaspoon salt

1 ¼ cups buttermilk

½ cup oil

3-5 tablespoons sugar

2 eggs

  

Preheat oven to 400F. Butter square pan. Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients and add to dry ingredients. Transfer to prepared pan. Bake about 25 minutes.
