---
title: "Olive, Pomegranate, and Walnut Dip (Zeytoon Parvardeh)"
category: "Appetizers"
image: ""
notes: ""
review: false
---

6 medium cloves garlic, minced

1 ½ cups walnut halves, ground coarsely

Salt and pepper to taste

1 to 2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses

½ cup olive oil

16 ounces green pitted olives

½ cup fresh pomegranate seeds

Lemons if needed

  

Combine garlic and walnuts in mixing bowl. Stir in molasses and oil to form thick paste (food processor makes this easier). Mix in olives, stirring gently to incorporate. Let sit in refrigerator. To serve, top with pomegranate seeds.
