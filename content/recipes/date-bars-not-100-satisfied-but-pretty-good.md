Filling: 

  

1 ½ - 2 cups dates

½ cups water

½ teaspoon vanilla extract

  

Oatmeal Crust:

  

3 cups oats

1 ½ cups flour

1 cup packed brown sugar

½ teaspoon baking soda

⅛ teaspoon salt

1 teaspoon cinnamon

1 ¼ cup cold unsalted butter, cut into pieces

  

Place dates and water in a saucepan. Cook over low heat about 5-10 minutes until smooshed. Add vanilla. Purée in blender.

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Line bottom of 9” square pan with parchment paper.

  

Place oats, flour, sugar, baking soda, salt, cinnamon in food processor. Add butter, pulse until mixture just comes together. Press ⅔ of mixture onto bottom of pan. Bake for about 15 minutes. Spread date mixture over oatmeal. Sprinkle remaining dough evenly over top of dates, press gently to compress. 

  

Bake 30 to 40 minutes until golden brown.
