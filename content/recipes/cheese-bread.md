1 ¾ cup water

½ cup cornmeal

2 teaspoons salt

½ cup molasses

2 tablespoons butter

½ cup warm water

1 package yeast

1 pound of cheddar cheese

4 to 4 ½  cups flour

  

Combine water, cornmeal, salt in a pot. Bring to a boil, continually stirring. Cook until the mixture is slightly thickened. Remove from heat. Stir in molasses and butter. Cool. Soften yeast, blend in cornmeal. Add flour to form dough. Knead five minutes.

  

 Let rise. Line pans with tin foil. Sprinkle dough with cornmeal and work in cheese 1/4 at a time. Let rise until dough has doubled. 

  

Bake at 350F, 45-55 minutes.
