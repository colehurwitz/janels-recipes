Heat 2 cups Grade A maple syrup to 234F, no stirring needed. 

Place pot in ice bath, cool to 95F. 

Stir for up to 30 minutes until you obtain maple cream consistency. 

  

It’s a lot of stirring. Sakineh and I did it together and took turns (it gets harder and harder). But it’s really good\!
