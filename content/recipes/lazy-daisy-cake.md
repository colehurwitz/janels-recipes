2 tablespoons unsalted butter, plus more for the pan

2 large eggs

¾ cup (150g) granulated sugar

1 cup (128g) flour

1 teaspoon baking powder

½ teaspoon salt

2 teaspoons vanilla extract and/or 1 teaspoon ground cardamom (optional)

½ cup (118ml) whole milk

  

For the topping:

  

½ cup (113g) unsalted butter

¾ cup (165g) packed brown sugar

¼ c (60ml) while milk

Pinch of salt

1 ¼ cups (105g) shredded coconut, unsweetened

  

Prepare the cake: Heat oven to 350F. Butter a 9 inch square or round baking pan and line the bottom with parchment paper, then putter the paper.

  

Using a mixer, beat the eggs and sugar until fluffy and pale yellow, about 2 minutes. Beat in flour, baking powder and salt. Beat in vanilla and/or cardamom if using.

  

In small saucepan, heat milk and 2 tablespoons butter, stirring until the butter melts and the milk steams, but don’t let it boil. Pour the hot mixture into the eggs, eating briefly until you have a smooth, runny batter. Pour into the prepared pan.

  

Bake 22-32 minutes, until the surface is pale golden and the top springs back when lightly pressed with your finger.

  

While the cake is baking, prepare the topping. In a small saucepan, melt butter. Add brown sugar, milk and salt. Stir until it comes to a simmer. Turn off heat and add coconut.

  

When cake is done, pour the coconut mixture evenly on top and place it under the broiler for 1-4 minutes until the topping is bubbling and browned. Watch it carefully so it doesn’t burn.

  
Cool.
