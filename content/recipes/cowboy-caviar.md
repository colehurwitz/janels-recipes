15 ounce can black eyed peas (rinse, drain, and put in a bow)

12-15 ounce choepeg corn (comes in a can) (rinse, drain, and put in a bowl)

2 avocados, large dice

½ cup chopped tomatoes

⅔ cup cilantro, chopped

⅔ cup green onions, chopped

¼ cup olive oil

¼ cup red wine vinegar

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 teaspoon cumin

¾ teaspoon salt

⅛ teaspoon pepper

  

Mix everything together. Chill. Serve.
