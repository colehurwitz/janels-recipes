250 grams butter, softened

130 grams confectioner’s sugar

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

2 egg yolks

356 grams flour

Powdered sugar for dusting 

  

Raspberry jam or other jam

  

Put butter and confectioner’s sugar in large bowl and beat together until pale and fluffy. Add vanilla and egg yolk. Sift in flour and mix into a firm dough. Work dough into a smooth ball with your hands, then wrap it in saran wrap and leave in fridge for an hour.

  

Preheat oven to 190C/375F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper. Place dough on lightly floured surface and roll out to about 3mm thick. Using 4 cm biscuit cutter, cut out an even number of circles. Then use a mini heart cutter to make little hearts in the center of half the circles. 

  

Place circles on prepared baking sheets and bake for 10-12 minutes or until they are a pale golden color. Transfer to wire rack to cool. While they are still warm, sprinkle with superfine sugar. Once they are completely cool, sandwich them together with jam.
