For sauce:

1 cup heavy cream

7 ounces bittersweet chocolate, finely chopped

  

For pancakes:

½ cup dutch-processed unsweetened cocoa powder

1 ¼ cups flour

½ cup sugar (up to 1 cup - to taste)

Dash salt

½ teaspoon baking soda

2 whole eggs

1 egg yolk

¾ cup well-shaken buttermilk

¼ cup vegetable oil

1 teaspoon vanilla

  

Make sauce:

Bring cream to a boil. Pour hot cream over chocolate in gowl, gently whisking until smooth. Keep warm or at room temperature.

  

Make pancakes:

Sift together cocoa, flour, sugar, baking soda, and salt. Whisk in eggs, yolk, buttermilk, oil, and vanilla until combined.

Heat griddle and coat with butter.

Cook on griddle; serve.
