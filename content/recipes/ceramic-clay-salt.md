1 cup salt

½ c cornstarch

¾ cups cold water

  

Mix in a double boiler and cook. It will follow the spoon around as it cooks (3-4 minutes). When thick, scoop onto wax paper, knead while warm. Store in air tight container. Hardens like ceramic. Can be painted with watercolors.
