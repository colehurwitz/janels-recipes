For the crust:

  

¾ cup flour

¼ cup cornstarch

½ cup confectioner’s sugar

Dash salt

½ cup (1 stick) cold butter, cut into 1 inch pieces

 

For the filling:

  

12 tablespoons (1 ½ sticks) butter  ½ teaspoon vanilla 

¾ cup brown sugar    Dash salt

3 tablespoons honey    2 tablespoons whipping cream

3 cups coarsely chopped pecans

  

For the crust:

  

Cover a 9 inch square pan with aluminum foil. Push foil neatly into corners and up sides of pan; overlap edges. Spray with cooking spray. Place flour, cornstarch, confectioner’s sugar, and salt in a food processor. Pulse a few times to mix. Add butter and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse meal. Dump into prepared pan and press firmly with your fingers into an even layer over the bottom. Refrigerate for 15 minutes. Preheat oven to 350F. Bake until crust is set but not browned, about 17 minutes. Let cool.

  

For the filling:

  

In saucepan over medium heat, combine butter, brown sugar, honey, vanilla, and salt. Stir with a wooden spoon until sugar dissolves. Turn up heat and boil gently for 3 minutes. Stir in heavy cream and chopped pecans.

  

To assemble and bake:

  

Pour pecan mixture over crust. Bake until filling is bubbling and caramel in color, about 20 minutes. Cool completely on rack. Use foil overhang to remove from pan and cut on cutting board.
