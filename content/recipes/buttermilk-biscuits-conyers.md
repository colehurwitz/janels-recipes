2 cups flour

⅓ cup cold butter

2 ½ teaspoons baking powder

¼ teaspoon baking soda

1 tablespoon sugar

½ teaspoon salt

  

Mix dry ingredients. Cut in ⅓ cup butter. Add ¾ cup buttermilk. Mix/knead as little as possible. 

  

Bake at 425F for 10-12 minutes.
