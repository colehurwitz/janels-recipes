1 cup butter

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla

¾ cup brown sugar

½ cup white sugar

1 ½ cup flour

3 cups oats

1 teaspoon baking powder

1 teaspoon baking soda

2 teaspoons cinnamon

1 cup raisins

  

Cream butter and sugar together. Add eggs and vanilla. Add remaining ingredients except raisins. Add raisins at the end, mixing in.

  

Bake at 350F for about 10 minutes until done. (Baked goods should have a 200F internal temperature).
