3 sticks butter, softened

1 cup sugar

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

3 ½ cups flour

Dash salt

1 egg beaten with 1 tablespoon water for egg wash

7 ounces sweetened flaked coconut

Raspberry or apricot jam

  

Preheat oven to 350F.

  

Cream butter and sugar, then add vanilla. Add flour and salt. Add sugar. Mix until dough comes together. Dump on floured board and roll together into a flat disk. Wrap in plastic and chill for 30 minutes.

  

Roll dough into 1 1/4 inch balls. Dip each ball into the egg wash and then roll it in coconut. Place on ungreased cookie sheet and press a light indentation into the top of each ball with your finger. Put 1/4 teaspoon jam into each indentation. 

  

Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until coconut is golden brown.
