title: "Lentil (Red) Curry with Sweet Potatoes and Spinach"

3 tablespoons olive oil

1 pound sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into ¾ inch cubes

1 medium onion, chopped

3 tablespoons Thai red curry paste

3 garlic cloves, minced (about 1 tablespoon)

1 inch fresh ginger, peeled and grated (about 1 tablespoon)

1 red chile, halved, seeded, and minced (optional for more heat\!)

1 teaspoon turmeric

1 cup red lentils, rinsed

4 cups chicken stock (or vegetable stock)

2 teaspoons kosher salt

1 13 ounce can coconut milk

1 (4-5 ounce) bag baby spinach

½ lime, juiced

  

In a Dutch oven, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium-high. Add the sweet potatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, 5-7 minutes. Transfer to plate and set aside.

  

Add the remaining olive oil and set to medium-low. Add the onion and cook until translucent, 4-6 minutes. Add the curry paste, garlic, ginger, chile, and turmeric. Cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

  

Add the lentils, stock, salt, browned sweet potatoes to the pot and bring to a boil over high heat. Lower heat and simmer, uncovered, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are just tender, 20-25 minutes.

  

Add the coconut milk and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the liquid has reduced and the lentils are creamy, 15-20 minutes.

  

Add the spinach and stir just until wilted, 2-3 minutes. Off heat, stir in lime juice and season with salt to taste.

  

Serve over rice if desired.
