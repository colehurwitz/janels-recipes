2 ½ cups flour (3 ¾)

1 ½ cups sugar (2 ¼

1 teaspoon baking soda (1 ½)

Dash salt

1 teaspoon cocoa powder (1 ½)

1 ½ cup oil (2 ½ 

1 cup buttermilk (1 ½)

2 eggs (3)

1 teaspoon white distilled vinegar (1 ½)

1 teaspoon vanilla (1 ½)

Frosting (possibly cut in half):

1 pound cream cheese, softened

2 sticks butter

1 teaspoon vanilla

4 cups powdered sugar (Start with 2 cups and add to desired sweetness)

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Line two 12 cup muffin tins with cupcake liners.

  

In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, sugar, baking soda, salt, and cocoa powder. In a large bowl, gently beat together the oil, buttermilk, eggs, food coloring, vinegar, and vanilla. Add sifted dry ingredients to the wet.

  

Divide batter evenly among cupcake tins about ⅔ full. Bake in oven, 20 to 22 minutes. Remove from oven and completely cool before frosting.

  

For frosting: In bowl, beat cream cheese, butter, and vanilla together until smooth.  Sift powdered sugar before adding. Add sugar; beat on low speed beat until incorporated. Beat until light and fluffy.
