1 pound penne pasta

2 tablespoons olive oil

4 ounces diced pancetta (alternately can use diced bacon)

1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped

2 garlic cloves, minced

½ teaspoon red pepper flakes

¾ cup vodka

1 - 28 ounce can crushed tomatoes

Salt and freshly ground pepper to taste

¾ cup heavy cream

¼ cup grated parmesan cheese plus more for serving

Prepare to cook pasta (heat water, etc.)

  

Prepare the sauce. Heat oil in skillet. Add pancetta (or bacon) and fry until crispy, about 3-5 minutes. Add the onion, garlic, and red pepper flakes and cook until the onion is translucent, about 3 minutes. Turn heat to medium-low and add vodka, cook until reduced by about half, 2-3 minutes.

  

By now you should be cooking the pasta, because when the sauce is done, you mix the pasta into the sauce.

  

Stir in tomatoes and then fill the can with a little water (¼ to ½ cup), swishing the sides to loosen up the leftover tomato. Add the water to the pan. Simmer until the sauce begins to thicken, about 10 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Reduce heat to low, add the cream and cook, stirring until the sauce becomes an even pinkish-rust color, about 1 minute.

  

Stir in cooked pasta and ¼ cup cheese; toss to coat. Season with salt and pepper. If desired, sprinkle with fresh oregano and parsley.
