1 ½ cups flour

3 tablespoons confectioner’s sugar

2 teaspoons baking powder

Dash salt

1 ¼ cups milk

4 tablespoons butter, melted and cooled

½ teaspoon vanilla extract

1 large egg plus 3 large egg whites

¼ teaspoon cream of tartar

  

Whisk together the flour, confectioner’s sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl. Whisk together the milk, melted butter, vanilla and egg yolk in a medium bowl. Beat egg whites and cream of tartar in another large bowl with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form. Stir the milk mixture into the flour mixture until just combined. Stir one-third of the beaten egg whites into the flour-milk mixture. Then gently fold the remaining egg whites until just combined. Don’t overmix.

Spray four 3 inch wide x 2 ½ inch tall ring molds with cooking spray.

  

Heat skillet over medium-low heat. Put ring molds on skillet and fill each with ½ cup batter. Cover skillet with lid and cook until the batter rises to the tops of the ring molds and is golden on the bottom, about 5 minutes. Release the bottom of the pancakes with a spatula. Flip. Cover and cook until golden on other side. Serve with butter and maple syrup. Eat before they deflate\!
