For the crust:

  

1 ¼ cups finely crushed graham cracker crumbs

2 tablespoons sugar

4 tablespoons butter, melted

  

Preheat oven to 375F. Combine crumbs, sugar, and melted butter. Stir with fork, then use hands until mixture is well combined. Press the crumbs firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a deep dish pie pan. Bake 10 minutes. Let cool.

  

For the filling:

  

Two 14 ounce cans sweetened condensed milk

1 cup plain Greek yogurt (2%) or Skyr yogurt

1 tablespoon grated lime zest

¾ cup fresh lime juice

  

Lower oven to 350F. Whisk together condensed milk, yogurt. If lumpy, push through fine mesh. Add lime zest, and lime juice. Pour into crust. Bake 15 minutes until filling is almost set; it should wobble a bit. Let cool 30 minutes, then place in refrigerator for 3 hours.

  

For the topping:

  

1 cup cold heavy cream

2 tablespoons sugar

1 teaspoon grated lime zest

  

Beat cream until soft peaks. Add sugar and meat till medium peaks form. Top pie with whipped cream. Decorate with lime zest. Store in refrigerator until ready to serve.
