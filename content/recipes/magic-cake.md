4 eggs at room temperature

¾ cup sugar

8 tablespoons butter, melted

1 teaspoon vanilla

¾ cup flour

2 cups lukewarm milk

Powdered sugar for dusting

  

Preheat oven to 325F degrees. Grease an 8x8 inch baking dish or line it with parchment paper so that it’s easier to get the cake out. 

  

Separate the eggs and beat the egg yolks with the sugar until light and fluffy. 

  

Add butter and vanilla extract and continue beating for another minute or two after which you can add the flour and mix it in until fully incorporated.

  

Slowly start adding the milk and beat until everything is well mixed together.

  

Beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form.

  

Add the egg whites to the cake batter and gently whisk them in. Don’t whisk them in completely. You still want to see some of the white bits floating at the top.

  

Pour batter into the baking dish and bake for 40 to 70 minutes or until the top is lightly golden. The baking time varies greatly, depending on your oven. Peak at 40 minutes and see how it looks. The cake is done when it only jiggles slightly but feels firm to the touch.

  

Sprinkle some powdered sugar after the cake has cooled.
