Apple Topping:

3 apples (1 ½ pounds) peeled cored and sliced ¼ inch thick

½ cup dark brown sugar

4 tablespoons unsalted butter

½ cup walnuts (optional)

  

Muffins:

2 cups flour

¾ cup dark brown sugar

1 tablespoon baking powder

1 teaspoon cinnamon

Dash teaspoon salt

8 tablespoons butter (melted)

2 large eggs lightly beaten

¾ cup sour cream

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

  

Heat the oven to 375F. Grease a 12 cup muffin tin. In a large skillet stir together apples, ½ cup brown sugar, and 4 tablespoons butter. Cook, stirring until the apples are tender about 15 minutes. Add apples to the muffin cups. Add walnuts (if using on top of apple slices). 

  

To make muffins: whisk together flour, ¾ cup brown sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt. In a separate bowl, mix ¼ cup butter, eggs, sour cream, and vanilla. Pour wet ingredients into dry and fold until smooth. Distribute the batter on top of the apples.

  

Bake until muffins are slightly puffed 20-25 minutes. Allow the muffins to cool partially in the pan. Serve while warm.
