2 cups thick yogurt

1 cup cucumber, finely chopped

1 clove garlic, crushed

2 teaspoons olive oil

1 tablespoon dill

1 tablespoon lemon juice

  

You can thicken the yogurt by placing it, mixed with salt, in dish towel set in sieve, letting moisture drip through, for about 2 hours. If you use Greek yogurt, you can still thicken the yogurt but it is not absolutely necessary. Sprinkle salt over cucumber; let stand 15 minutes and press dry. Combine all ingredients. Chill.
