2 packets active dry yeast (4 ½ teaspoons)

½ cup sugar, plus a pinch

7 cups all purpose flour, plus more for dusting

1 tablespoon salt

2 large eggs

½ cup vegetable oil

(1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips)

(½ teaspoon instant coffee)

  

Mix the yeast, pinch of sugar, and 1 cup warm water (not too hot) in a small bowl. Set aside until frothy, about 10 minutes.

  

Whisk 3 ½ cups flour, remaining ½ cup sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Pour in the yeast mixture and stir with a wooden spoon. Add 1 egg, the vegetable oil, and 1 cup warm water (stir in instant coffee), stir until combined. Gradually stir in the remaining 3 ½  cups flour until the dough starts to come together. (Mix in chocolate chips).

  

Knead dough until smooth, about 8 minutes. Transfer to large bowl, cover, and let rise in warm place until doubled in size, 1-1 ½ hours.

  

Divide the dough into 3 equal portions, on a floured surface, roll each portion into a ball, then roll each ball into a 1 ½ to 2 inch wide strand, about 16 inches long. Lay the strands side by side on a sheet of parchment paper and pinch them together at one end. Let rest 1 hour.

  

Braid the 3 strands. Let rest for 20 minutes.

  

Preheat oven to 375F. Beat the remaining egg with 1 tablespoon water in a small bowl, then brush the egg wash over the loaf. Bake until golden brown, about 30 minutes.
