Combine 2 cups whipping cream, 

1/2 cup cocoa (sifted to remove lumps), 

1 cup powdered sugar (sifted or it will look lumpy), dash salt (optional).

  

 Whip together. 

  

(It’s good-light and fluffy - especially good for a rich cake)
