1 ¾ cups flour

1 ½ teaspoons baking powder

1 teaspoon baking soda

2 cups buttermilk

4 tablespoons butter, melted

2 eggs, lightly beaten

  

Preheat waffle iron. In large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda. Add buttermilk, melted butter, and eggs; whisk until smooth.

  

When waffle iron is ready, pour batter in and let cook according to directions.
