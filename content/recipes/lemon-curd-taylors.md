⅓ cup fresh lemon juice (can use more, up to ¾  cup)

4 egg yolks

6 tablespoons unsalted  butter

1 tablespoon lemon zest (but can add another tablespoon if needed)

⅔ cup sugar (but can use less depending on tartness so add slowly)

  

In a 2 quart saucepan over low heat, combine lemon juice, lemon zest, sugar, eggs. Whisk until completely blended, then continue to whisk to prevent from curdling. Cook over medium-low heat until thick enough to hold marks from whisk, and first bubble appears on surface, about 6 minutes. 

Remove pan from heat. Cut butter into 6 separate pieces, then whisk into the curd. Strain through fine sieve if needed. Let cool about an hour. It will thicken as it cools.
