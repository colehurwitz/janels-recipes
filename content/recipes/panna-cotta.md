1 cup 1% milk

1 tablespoon unflavored powdered gelatin

3 cups whipping cream 

⅓ cup honey

1 tablespoon sugar

  

Place milk in small bowl. Sprinkle gelatin over. Let stand for 3 to 5 minutes until softened. Pour gelatin mixture into a heavy pan and stir over low heat just until the gelatin dissolves but the milk does not boil, about 5 minutes. Add the cream, honey, and sugar. Stir until sugar dissolves, 5 to 7 minutes (no boiling). Remove from heat. Pour into dishes. Cool slightly. Refrigerate until set, about 6 hours.
