2 pounds chicken, cut up

1 can chicken broth

1 teaspoon salt

1 bay leaf

pepper

¼ teaspoon thyme

3 tablespoons flour

2 medium carrots, cut up

2 medium onions, cut up

1 stalk celery, cut up

2 tablespoons lemon juice

  

Put chicken in pot with onions, celery, carrots, spices, lemon juice, chicken broth, and water to cover. Simmer until all is tender.  Remove chicken and vegetables. Bring liquid to boil. Blend with the flour with water until smooth. Mix in with broth, stirring until thickened. Put on dumplings. Cook uncovered 10 minutes, covered 10 minutes. 

  

Dumplings:

  

1 cup flour     ½ teaspoon salt

2 teaspoons baking powder  1 tablespoon butter

1 teaspoon white sugar   ½ cup milk

  

Cut butter into dry ingredients until crumbly. Stir in milk to make soft dough. Drop by spoonfuls into boiling stew. Cover and simmer 15 minutes without lifting lid. Serve.
