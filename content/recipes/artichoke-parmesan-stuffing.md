1 pound sourdough bread cut into ½ inch cubes

1 tablespoon butter

1 pound mushrooms sliced

12 cloves minced garlic

1 ½  teaspoons poultry seasoning

2 jars marinated artichoke hearts

¾ teaspoon salt; pepper to taste

1 large onion, chopped

2 cups chicken broth

½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

1 egg, beaten

  

Spread bread cubes in single layer on baking sheet and toast at 350F until very crisp, about 25 minutes. Make up to 2 days ahead.

  

Melt butter in pan; add onions, mushrooms, celery, and garlic.  Cook until soft.

In large bowl, mix vegetables with bread, artichoke hearts, Parmesan cheese, and seasonings. Whisk together 2 cups broth and egg and pour over mixture. Stir until moistened. Bake and serve\!
