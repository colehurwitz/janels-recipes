1 cup butter

1 ½ cups sugar

2 eggs

2 ¾ cups flour

2 teaspoons cream of tartar

1 teaspoon baking soda

Dash salt

Mix together:

2 tablespoons cinnamon

2 tablespoons sugar

  

Heat oven to 400F. Mix butter, sugar, eggs. Blend in flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt. Shape dough by rounded teaspoonfuls into balls. Roll balls in cinnamon mixture. Place 2 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheet. 

  

Bake 8-10 minutes or until done. Remove from baking sheet.
