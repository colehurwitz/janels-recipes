Preheat oven to 450F. Put 2 tablespoons butter in 5 ½ quart dutch oven. Heat pan, melt butter.

Into blender put: 1 ½ cups milk and six eggs. Whip one minute. Add: 1 ½  cups flour, 2 heaping tablespoons sugar, 1 teaspoon vanilla, whip one minute. Put into dutch oven with lid on. Bake for 20 minutes.
