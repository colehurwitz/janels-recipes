2 cups sundried tomatoes, dry, packed

2 cups boiling water

¼ cup slivered/sliced almonds

½ cup cooked white beans drained

2 cloves garlic

¼ cup olive oil

2 tablespoons lemon juice

⅛ teaspoon salt

Pepper to taste

  

Place tomatoes in a bowl and pour the boiling water over top of the tomatoes. Cover with a plate and let soak for 15 minutes. In a food processor, grind almonds to a powder. Remove tomatoes from water and add to almonds (save the tomato water). Add remaining ingredients and purée. Add tomato water as needed (up to ¼ cup).
