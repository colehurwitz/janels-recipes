1 cup all purpose flour

¾ cups cornmeal

2 to 4 tablespoons sugar

1 tablespoon double-acting baking powder

Dash salt

1 egg

⅔ cup milk

⅓ cup butter, melted, or oil

  

Preheat oven to 425F. Grease an 8x8 baking pan. In medium bowl, with a fork, mix together flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder, and salt.

  

In a small bowl with a fork, beat together egg, milk, and butter or oil and pour egg mixture all at once into the flour mixture.

  

Stir just until flour is moistened and pour batter into prepared baking pan. 

  

Spread the batter evenly and bake for 25 minutes or until golden. Cut into squares to serve.
