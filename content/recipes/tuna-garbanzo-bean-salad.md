1 - 15 ounce can garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed

¾ cup chopped parsley

30 pimiento stuffed olives chopped

¼ cup olive oil

¼ cup fresh lemon juice

1 7.5 to 10 ounce can albacore tuna, drained

4 large whole green onions, chopped

  

Combine garbanzo beans, parsley, olives in a bowl. Add oil and lemon juice.

In a small bowl, break up tuna into large chunks and add to garbanzo beans. Add the green onions and salt and pepper to taste. Stir to combine. Cover and chill for several hours before serving.
