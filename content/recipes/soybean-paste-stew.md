Serves 3

Serve with rice

  

1 medium potato, cut into bite sized chunks

1 medium onion, roughly chopped

1 zucchini, diced

2 tablespoons rice wine (Mirin)

1 tablespoon ssamjang

1-2 teaspoons gochugaru powder

4 garlic cloves, minced

(I used 1/2 lb ground pork, but recipe actually calls for 8 shrimp, no tail, cut into bite sized pieces)

2 ½ cups rice water (save water from rinsing rice)

5 tablespoons soybean paste

12 ounces tofu (1/2 inch cubes)

  

Add 2 tablespoons olive oil to the pan. Cook ground pork. Add in onion, zucchini, garlic (and shrimp if you’re using it).

  

Cook 1-2 minutes.

  

Add water.

  

Bring to boil. Stir in soybean paste, ssamjang, gochugaru, rice wine.

  

Cook until potato is tender. Add in tofu. Cook 3 minutes.

  

Serve with rice\!
