7 ounces bittersweet chocolate, broken

1 cup unsalted butter

4 eggs, separated

1 cup granulated sugar (divided)

½ cup unsweetened cocoa

⅛ teaspoon salt

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Grease the sides and bottom of a 10 inch springform pan. Line the bottom of the pan with parchment paper; grease the paper.

  

Melt chocolate and butter together in a double boiler. Let cool slightly. Put the egg yolks into a medium bowl and add about half the sugar. Beat until the mixture is light and thick. Fold the chocolate mixture into the egg yolk mixture, then sprinkle the cocoa powder and salt over the mixture and fold gently until well-blended. 

  

In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites until they form soft peaks; gradually add the remaining sugar, beating until firm, glossy peaks form. Add about a quarter of the egg whites to the chocolate mixture and gently fold until blended; fold in remaining whites.

  

Gently slide the batter into the prepared cake pan. Bake until a toothpick comes out almost clean, approximately 40 minutes. Let cake cool in the pan. Then run a knife around the inside of the pan to release the cake and unlatch and remove the side of the pan. Let cool completely before serving.
