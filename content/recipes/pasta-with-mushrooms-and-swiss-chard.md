8 ounces pasta

6 ounces mushrooms

2 cloves garlic

½ bunch Swiss chard

Rosemary (about 1 teaspoon)

2 tablespoons butter

2 tablespoons heavy cream

1 cup grated Romano cheese

  

Separate chard leaves and stems. Small dice the stems. Thinly slice the leaves. Pick rosemary off stems, discard the stems. Peel and slice garlic. Thinly slice mushrooms. 

In a large pan, heat a thin layer of olive oil. Add rosemary, cook until crisp and fragrant. Transfer to paper towel. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside.

  

Add the mushrooms, cook until softened and browned. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside. Add more oil, add garlic and cook about 30 seconds. Add chard leaves and stems and about 2 tablespoons of water. Season with salt and pepper. Cook about 1 to 2 minutes, until chard leaves have wilted. Remove pan from heat. 

  

Cook pasta. Reserve a bit of cooking water. Add cooked pasta to pan, along with all the ingredients, butter, heavy cream and Romano cheese. Add pasta water if needed. Remove from heat and season with salt and pepper. 

  

Sprinkle with rosemary and remaining Romano.
