8 1” slices of challah bread, cut into 1” cubes

Combine 2 cups milk with 1 cup heavy cream. Add ¾ cup Amaretto.

Mix together ¾ cup sugar and 6 eggs.

Add sugar and eggs to milk.

  

Place bread cubes in bowl. Pour milk mixture over bread. Let soak up to an hour.

  

Pour into a pan and bake at 325F for 50 minutes to an hour.

  

Serve with Creme Anglaise (Recipe is in this document)
