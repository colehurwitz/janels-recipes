2 medium onions, chopped   1 teaspoon salt

1 tablespoon finely chopped garlic  1/2 teaspoon cayenne to taste

2 pounds lean hamburger    2 - 16 ounce cans tomatoes,

2 tablespoons chili powder      chopped finely or ground

1 tablespoon cumin    2 cups broth

1 teaspoon cinnamon    1 ½ cups water

1 square (1 ounce) unsweetened          1-2 tablespoons sugar

   chocolate, chopped

1 package (16 ounces) pasta

  

Heat oil over medium heat. Add onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 5 minutes. Add garlic to pot, cook 1 minute longer. Transfer to bowl.

  

Cook ground beef, breaking up meat with spoon, until meat is browned. Discard fat. Stir in chili powder, cumin, cinnamon, salt, oregano, and ground red pepper; cook one minute longer.

  

Add tomatoes with their juice. Stir in broth, water, chocolate, browned beef, and onion-garlic mixture; heat to boiling. Reduce heat; cover and simmer 2 hours 30 minutes. Add 1 to 2 tablespoons sugar to taste. 

  

Remove cover and simmer until thickened, about 30 minutes longer.

  

Meanwhile, prepare pasta. Drain. Serve chili over pasta.
