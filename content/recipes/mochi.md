2 cups glutinous rice flour

¾ cup to 1 cup sugar

1 ½ cups water

  

Mix ingredients in a greased bowl. Use steamer for about 20 minutes to cook in the bowl.

Lightly sprinkle the working area with potato starch. Divide mochi evenly into small pieces and make each piece into a wrap (do this ¼ of mochi at a time, keeping the rest in the steamer).

Prepare stuffing in advance, then wrap in mochi.
