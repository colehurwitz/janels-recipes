1 cup flour

1 tablespoon sugar

2 teaspoons baking powder

½ teaspoon baking soda

1 ¼ cups buttermilk

3 tablespoons butter, melted

1 large egg

  

Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda. Add buttermilk, melted butter, and egg; stir just until flour is moistened.

  

Heat griddle; brush lightly with butter. Pour batter by scant ¼ cups onto hot buttered griddle (or into a hot buttered pan), making a few pancakes at a time. 

  

Cook until tops are bubbly, and edges look dry. Flip and cook another 2 minutes (approximately).
