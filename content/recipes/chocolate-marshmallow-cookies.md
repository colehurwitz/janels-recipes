1 cup bittersweet chocolate, chopped

6 tablespoons butter (85g)

¾ cup flour (90g)  

6 tablespoons cocoa (31g)

¾ teaspoon baking powder

Salt to taste

¾ teaspoon cinnamon

⅛ teaspoon cayenne (optional)

⅔ sugar (132g)

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla

Marshmallows

  

Place the chocolate and butter in a medium heatproof bowl. Set the bowl over a pot gently simmering water and cook, stirring occasionally, until the chocolate has melted and the mixture is smooth. Alternately, to use the microwave, add the butter and chocolate to a medium microwave-safe bowl and heat at half power in 30 second intervals, stirring in between bursts until smooth.

  

In medium bowl, whisk flour, cocoa, baking powder, cinnamon, and cayenne until well-combined.

In bowl of stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment, combine the sugar and eggs. Whip at medium speed until the mixture is voluminous, pale, and ribbons into the bowl when the whisk is lifted, about 5 to 6 minutes.

  

Add melted chocolate mixture and vanilla to the bowl. And mix until well combined, about one minute. Switch to flat beater and add the flour mixture. Mix until almost no flour remains, less than 30 seconds. Finish incorporating any dry bits with a spatula. Cover and regfigerate until firm, at least 1 ½ to 2 hours.

  

Heat oven to 325F, line baking sheet with parchment paper or silicon mat. Scoop 1 ½ tablespoon-portions (about 30g) of cookie dough. Form into balls. Bake 8-9 minutes, until cookies are puffy and matte, quite soft and underbaked. Gently press marshmallows into center (½ a big one or 7-8 minis). Return to oven for 3-5 minutes, until cookies are set and marshmallows have puffed. If desired, carefully broil the cookies until marshmallows are browned. Let cool.
