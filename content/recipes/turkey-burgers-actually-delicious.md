1 pound ground turkey

3 tablespoon breadcrumbs

3 tablespoon diced onions

1 egg, slightly beaten

1 small clove garlic, peeled and minced

1 teaspoon salt

⅛ teaspoon pepper (optional)

  

Mix all ingredients together. Form patties. Grill or pan fry to an internal temperature of 160F.
