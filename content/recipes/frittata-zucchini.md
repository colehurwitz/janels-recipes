3 medium sized zucchini

1 onion

Salt and pepper to taste

Turmeric

Italian seasoning

4 eggs

Feta cheese

  

Slice the zucchini thinly, about ⅛ inch thick. Lay on top of paper towel and sprinkle with salt. This will allow the water from the zucchini to be released and make the frittata less watery. Let it sit for about 30 minutes. Rinse it to remove salt. Pat dry.  

  

Quarter the onion and then slice each quarter into ¼ inch slices. In olive oil, saute the onions until they are soft and partially carmelized. Heat olive oil in a cast iron pan. When it’s hot, layer in zucchini. Sprinkle the top with about 1 teaspoon turmeric, about ¼ teaspoon Italian seasoning, and ground pepper on top. I didn’t add any salt. Then cook it about 3 minutes. Do your best to flip it. Sprinkle it again with the same amount of turmeric and Italian seasoning. Mix in the spices. Cook another 3 minutes, and flip it. Keep doing it until all the zucchini is cooked. Some of it can be browned. 

  

Beat the eggs with a fork.

  

Mix the onions into the zucchini mix. Sprinkle about ⅔ of a 6 ounce container of crumbled feta on top and mix it in. Pour the eggs on top. Cover the pan with a lid and let it cook 3 minutes. Push the zucchini around to let the eggs mix in better (kind of like cooking an omelette). Scrape the bottom to make sure it’s not sticking. Then compress it, cover it, and let it cook another 3 minutes. If needed, push the zucchini around to let the eggs mix in. Cover it until the eggs are not runny. You can try to flip it to cook the top. Keep checking until the eggs are mostly cooked and then turn it off, leave it covered, and let it sit for about 10 minutes. 

  

You could try using parmesan cheese instead of feta.
