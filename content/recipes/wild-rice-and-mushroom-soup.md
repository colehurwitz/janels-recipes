⅔ cup wild rice, cooked

 2 tablespoons olive oil

2 cups (10 oz) cremini mushrooms, sliced

1 yellow onion, chopped

2 cloves garlic, minced

3 carrots, chopped

3 ribs celery, chopped

1 teaspoon fresh minced thyme

½ teaspoon salt

4 tablespoons butter or use vegan butter such as Miyoko’s brand

¼ cup flour

¼ cup sherry

4 cup broth

½ cup cashew crema (recipe under Miscellaneous)

  

In large pot over medium heat, heat olive oil. Add mushrooms, stir to coat in olive oil, and cook without moving until they release liquid and it evaporates and mushrooms start to brown, about 3-5 minutes. Cook stirring about 2 minutes more.

  

Add onions and garlic until onions turn translucent, 2-3 minutes. Add carrot, celery, thyme, and salt. Cook 2-3 minutes.

  

Add butter and melt. Sprinkle in flour and stir to combine. Slowly drizzle in sherry, stirring until thick about 1 minute.

  

Add broth 1 cup at a time, stirring to avoid lumps. Bring to simmer and decrease heat to medium low. Add cooked rice and cook uncovered until slightly thickened, about 15-20 minutes. Add cashew crema and stir thoroughly to combine. Adjust seasonings as needed.
