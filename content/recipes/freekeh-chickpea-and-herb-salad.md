1 cup freekeh

½ teaspoon salt, more to taste

½ cup chopped flat leaf parsley

¼ cup chopped fresh mint

1 cup thinly sliced celery, plus 3 tablespoons chopped leaves

1 bunch scallions, finely chopped

1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed

¼ cup lemon juice, more to taste

½ teaspoon cumin

1 garlic clove, minced

6 tablespoons olive oil

  

Heat a medium-sized saucepan over medium heat and add freekeh. Toast in the dry pan, shaking or stirring, until freekeh becomes fragrant, 2 to 3 minutes. Add 2 cups water and salt and bring to boil.

Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer, 20 to 25 minutes or until water has been absorbed. Turn off heat and uncover. Place a clean dish towel over the pot and return lid. Let sit at least 10 minutes. Uncover and let cool another 10 minutes.

  

In a large bowl, combine freekeh, chopped herbs, celery, scallions and chickpeas and toss together. 

  

In a small bowl, whisk together lemon juice, cumin, garlic, salt, and olive oil; toss with salad. Taste and add more lemon juice if desired. Let sit up to 1 hour before serving.
