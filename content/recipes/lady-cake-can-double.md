1 ¾ cup cake flour 

  

Sift flour with:

  

2 teaspoons baking powder

Dash salt

  

Beat until creamy:

  

¾ cup ROOM TEMPERATURE (softened) butter

  

Add 1 cup sugar to butter and beat until very light. Add one teaspoon vanilla.

  

1 cup sugar

  

Add the flour mixture to the butter mixture in 3 parts, alternating with ½ cup milk.:

  

Stir the batter a few minutes after each addition. 

  

Whip until stiff (aka egg whites are more that foamy and are firm):

  

3 egg whites

  

Fold egg whites gently into the cake batter.

  

Bake in a greased tube pan at 350F for about 45 minutes. Or bake cupcakes for about 20 minutes. Or bake in a 9x13 pan for about 30 minutes. 

  

Cover with chocolate icing (Karen’s).
