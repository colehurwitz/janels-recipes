1 pound 80/20 ground beef

1 large onion, finely chopped

2 carrots, finely chopped

2 tablespoons red curry paste

1 15 ounce can tomato sauce

1 pound pasta

1 cup heavy cream

Parmesan

  

Bring salted water to boil. 

  

In dutch oven, add beef and smush and spread in single layer. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Then cook, stirring to break into tiny bits and cooks through. 

  

Push meat to one side, add onions and carrots to the beef fat. Sprinkle with salt and pepper, cook, stirring beef occasionally, until onion is translucent.

  

Add 3 tablespoons curry paste. Stir until darkened and blended, 2-3 minutes. Pour in tomato sauce. Fill can ⅓ way with water and swish out rest of sauce, adding water to sauce. Bring to simmer, then reduce heat to low. Cook pasta in boiling water until al dente. Save ½ cup pasta water. Drain and add pasta to sauce.

  

Stir in cream. If needed, add pasta water to thin.

  

Sprinkle with parmesan. Serve\!
