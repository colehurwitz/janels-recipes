6 squares (6 ounces) unsweetened chocolate

1 cup light cream

1 ½ cups sugar

¼ cup butter

2 teaspoons vanilla

  

Heat all ingredients except vanilla in fondue pot at high heat, stirring occasionally until chocolate is melted. Reduce heat to medium. Continue cooking, stirring constantly for about 5 minutes, until thickened. Set at low to serve.
