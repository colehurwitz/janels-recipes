Tart Crust:

  

1 cup flour

⅓ cup almond flour

¼ cup sugar

½ cup chilled butter cut into small ½ ” cubes

1 egg yolk

1 teaspoon almond extract

  

Filling:

½ cup apricot preserves

¼ cup sugar

⅛ to ¼ teaspoon ground cardamom

9-12 large apricots, halved and pitted

  

Fo-r the crust:

In a food processor, add butter to flours, sugar, salt and cut in using on/off pulsing. Add 2 tablespoons cold water, egg yolk, and almond extract. Pulse until it becomes a ball.

Press dough into bottom of 9” tart pan with removable bottom. Chill for 30 minutes.

For the filling:

Set rack at lowest position in oven. Preheat to 375F. Stir preserves over low heat to melt. Place a pan or foil under to catch drippings.

1.  Pour 1/4 cup sugar over bottom of crust. Sprinkle cardamom over sugar. Arrange apricots rounded side up fit snugly in crust.
2.  Brush with melted preserves.

Bake 1 hour or until fruit is tender and looks nicely cooked.

Cool for an hour.
