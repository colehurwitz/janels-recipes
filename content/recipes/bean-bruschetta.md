¼ cup chopped flat leaf parsley

1 ½ tablespoons lemon juice

½ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

1 large garlic clove

1 15 ounce can cannellini beans

2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

2 teaspoons water

1 baguette

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Place parsley, lemon juice, salt, pepper, garlic and beans in a food processor. Purée until smooth. Drizzle in olive oil, add water 1 tablespoon at a time until creamy. 

To make bruschetta, slice baguette, brush with olive oil, and bake until toasted. About 5 minutes.
