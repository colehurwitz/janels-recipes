Melt 2 ounces unsweetened chocolate in a pot.

  

Stir in slowly into the melted chocolate:

  

½  cup sugar

1 ¾  cups milk

Dash salt

  

Heat these ingredients to the boiling point.

  

Dissolve:

  

3 tablespoons cornstarch

in:

¼  cup milk

  

Dissolve the cornstarch as stated above or the cornstarch will create lumps\! Stir the cornstarch slowly into the hot milk mixture. Cook for another 5-10 minutes, stirring constantly. (Technically you are supposed to cook this pudding in a double boiler and then cover and cook another 10 minutes, but I never do this). Cool by stirring very gently.

  

Add:

  

1 teaspoon vanilla.

  

To prevent a skin from forming as the pudding cools, stir every couple of minutes. Alternately press Saran Wrap on top of the pudding. Remove when it’s cool.
