---
title: Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies
category: Desserts
notes: Chilling the dough for an hour makes them chewier and deepens the toffee flavour.
---

Nutty browned butter takes the everyday chocolate chip cookie somewhere special.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2¼ cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat until it smells nutty and turns
   amber, then cool slightly.
2. Whisk in both sugars, then the eggs.
3. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt, then the chocolate chips.
4. Chill the dough for at least 1 hour.
5. Scoop onto lined trays and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 10–12 minutes.
