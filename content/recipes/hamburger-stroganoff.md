1 pound lean hamburger

1-2 packages mushrooms, sliced

1 large onion chopped

1 can Campbell’s Cream of Mushroom soup

Sour cream (about half a cup, maybe more)

Salt and Pepper

  

Saute hamburger until cooked and crumbled; discard fat. Remove from pan. Cook onions until tender. Add hamburger back in to pan. Mix in mushroom soup and mushrooms. Simmer until mushrooms are cooked, stirring occasionally. 

  

While on low heat, add sour cream, stirring in, and adding more to achieve desired color/consistency. Season with salt and pepper.

  

Serve over rice. Or you can serve it over noodles if you prefer.
