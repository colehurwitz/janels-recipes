Raspberry filling:

 

Make raspberry puree (seedless) using about 1 pound of raspberries, about 1 tablespoon of cornstarch dissolved in 2 tablespoons water and sugar to taste. Start with a 1/4 cup of sugar, taste, and add sugar till tart-sweet.

  

To make, put seedless puree in pan, heat till almost boiling, adding sugar until you use an amount you’re happy with. Add cornstarch mixture. Cook until mixture is clear and thickened. Remove from heat. If you’d like, you can add a little lemon juice (1 teaspoon?) and lemon zest to enhance flavor. I usually don’t use lemon juice or zest.

  

Lemon Raspberry Cake 

  

13 ounces cake flour

1 cup sugar

dash salt

2 teaspoons baking powder

½ teaspoon baking soda

8 ounces butter

8 ounces buttermilk

3 ounces vegetable oil (Safflower oil)

3 large eggs

2 tablespoons lemon zest

2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

1 teaspoon lemon extract

2 tablespoons flour for dusting berries

10 ounces raspberries

  

Heat oven to 335F. Have ingredients at room temperature. Place 4 ounces buttermilk in a separate measuring cup. Add oil to the buttermilk; set aside. To the remaining buttermilk, add eggs, extract, lemon juice and lemon zest.

  

Put dry ingredients in mixing bowl. Add paddles attachment, and turn on slowest speed. Add chunks of softened butter. Let mix with dry ingredients until it resembles coarse sand.

Add milk/oil mixture all at once and mix on medium speed for about 2 minutes. Scrape the bowl. Slowly add the rest of your liquid ingredients in 3 parts, stopping to scrape the bowl one more time.

Lightly grease two 8” cake pans and line with parchment paper. Add about 1/4 cup of fresh raspberries dusted with flour sprinkled on top. Don’t stir them in. 

  

Bake about 30 minutes. Check for doneness. A toothpick inserted in the center will come out with a few crumbs.

  

After cakes have cooled 10 minutes, flip cakes and remove from pans and cool on rack. Wrap in plastic wrap and chill in refrigerator. 

  

I used a cream cheese/whipped cream frosting (8 ounces whipped cream, ¼  to ½  cup whipping cream, 3 cups confectioner’s sugar, mix in about ½  cup of the puree. Make sure to soften and beat cream cheese for a while before adding any other ingredients. 

  

You can make a lemon curd to put between the layers and you can pour the remaining raspberry puree over the top of the cake to make it pretty and tasty\!
