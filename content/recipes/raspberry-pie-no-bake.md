Make a pie crust for one pie; bake for about 10 minutes at 375 until tinged golden (won’t be a uniform color). When you bake a pie crust for an empty pie, you need to put weights on top on the pie for the first 7 minutes or so. You can buy pie weights or you can use dry beans, like lima beans.

  

Berry Filling:

  

2-3 cups fresh raspberries

1 ½ cups raspberry purée

@¾ cup sugar (taste purée to check tartness - add more or less sugar, but remember fresh raspberries will add tartness)

3 generous tablespoons cornstarch

Dash salt

  

Whisk cornstarch, sugar, and salt in small bowl. Then whisk mixture into purée in 2 quart saucepan. Bring purée to boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. When mixture reaches a boil and is thickened to consistency of a pudding, remove from heat. Pour most of purée into pie crust. Mix fresh raspberries with the remaining purée mixture in sauce pan. Toss to coat. Pour tossed raspberries into purée in pie crust. 

  

Loosely cover pie with plastic wrap; refrigerate until chilled and set, about 3 hours.

Serve with slightly sweetened whipping cream, flavored with a dash of vanilla extract.
