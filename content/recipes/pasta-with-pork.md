(To feed 5-6, double or triple)

4 ounces olive oil

1 pound pork shoulder, diced

1 teaspoon salt and pepper to taste

¾ large onion, diced

1 ½ carrots, diced

2 stalks celery, diced

6-7 cloves garlic, minced

5 ounces tomato paste

2 tablespoons flour

1 quart red wine

Sugar to taste (1 tbsp at a time)

1 bay leaf

1 sprig rosemary

1 sprig thyme

1 sprig sage

  

Prepared pasta

  

Heat oil over medium heat. Season meat with salt and pepper and add to pan. Once meat is browned, add onions, carrots, celery, and garlic. Reduce heat and cook until the moisture is gone. Add tomato paste and flour. Add red wine and herbs. Add sugar to taste. Cover and cook about 2 hours, stirring occasionally. The sauce is done when the meat is fork tender.

  

Serve over pasta.
