4 ounces Chèvre goat cheese

¼ cup water, divided

2 teaspoons honey

¼ cup flour

¾ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon pepper

½ cup sparkling water

2 cups coconut oil

24 fresh sage leaves, washed and dried

  

Combine the goat cheese, 2 tablespoons of water, and honey in a food processor; pulse until smooth, adding more water as needed until the mixture is thin enough to use as a dip. Spoon into a small bowl and place on a serving dish.

  

Combine the flour, salt, and pepper in a bowl; add the sparking water, whisking until smooth.

  

Place the coconut oil in a large cast-iron skillet over high heat. Line a plate with a paper towel. 

  

Drop a small amount of batter into the oil; if it sizzles, the oil is hot enough to fry.

  

Dip the sage leaves into the batter and carefully drop them into the hot oil, being careful not to crowd the pan. 

  

Fry for 3 minutes, turn, and fry 3 additional minutes or until golden brown. Repeat until all the leaves are done. 

  

Arrange on plate with dip and enjoy\!
