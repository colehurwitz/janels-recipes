(Can double)

  

Works for blind baking - 1 9” crust

½ cup butter, diced and chilled

1 ¼ cup flour

dash salt

2-4 tablespoons ice water

  

Combine flour and salt in food processor. Pulse once to mix.

  

Add butter and process until it resembles coarse crumbs. Add 1 tablespoon of water and pulse. Add water, one tablespoon at a time, pulsing after each addition, until the dough forms a ball.

  

Turn dough out onto lightly floured silicone baking mat. Press dough ball into flat disc and roll to about 1/4 inch thickness. Place in pie pan and press to fit. Decorate edges. Chill 1-2 hours for blind baking; 30 minutes for filled crust.

  

For blind baking (empty crust) - prick bottom of pie crust with fork. Layer a sheet of parchment paper in the bottom and fill with pie weights and bake at 400F for about 15-20 minutes. Remove parchment and pie weights carefully. Then continue baking until cooked through and golden brown.

For filled crust - After chilling, fill and then bake as directed in recipe.
