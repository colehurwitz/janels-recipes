2 tablespoons butter    ⅓ cup whipping cream

1 tablespoon olive oil    ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

½ pound pancetta, diced   Salt and pepper to taste

4 egg yolks      16 ounce pasta

Additional Parmesan to sprinkle on top

  

Melt butter with oil in a saucepan. When butter foams, add pancetta. Sauté over medium heat until lightly colored. Keep warm. Beat egg yolks in a shallow serving dish. Beat in cream, ½ cup Parmesan, salt and pepper. Prepare pasta. Drain spaghetti and place in a dish with egg yolk mixture. Toss quickly. Add sautéed pancetta and toss gently until mixed. Serve with additional Parmesan cheese.
