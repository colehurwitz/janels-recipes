9 inch prepared pie crust (Graham Cracker crust works best)

  

½ cup sugar

⅓ cup flour

Dash salt

2 ¼ cups milk

4 egg yolks

1 tablespoon butter

2 teaspoons vanilla extract

1 cup heavy cream

  

In 2 quart saucepan, mix sugar, flour, and salt. Stir in milk until smooth. Over medium heat, cook mixture, stirring constantly, until mixture is thickened and begins to boil (about 10 minutes). Boil 1 minute. Remove from heat. 

In small bowl, beat egg yolks with a small amount of hot milk mixture. Slowly pour into pan, stirring rapidly. Cook until very thick (do not boil) and mixture mounds when dropped from spoon.

Remove from heat; stir in butter and vanilla. 

Cover surface of filling with plastic wrap; refrigerate until cooler, about 1 hour. If you wait too long it will be too thick to spread. Peel and slice 3 medium bananas over bottom of pie crust; top with filling; chill until set, about 2 hours. Spread whipped cream evenly around top of pie.
