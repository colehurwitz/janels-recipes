2 jars marinated artichokes drained

1 can cannellini or great northern beans, rinsed and drained

1 clove garlic

½ cup grated parmesan cheese

2 tablespoons chopped flat leaf parsley

1-2 tablespoons lemon juice 

⅓ cup olive oil

Salt and pepper to taste

  

In food processor puree artichokes, beans, garlic, cheese and parsley. Add lemon juice and drizzle in olive oil, salt and pepper.
