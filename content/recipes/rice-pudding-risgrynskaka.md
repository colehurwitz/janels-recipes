1 cup rice

1 tablespoon butter

1 cup water

5 cups milk

Dash salt

Generous ½ cup sugar

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

  

Scald rice with boiling water. Melt half of butter, add rice and water and boil 10 to 15 minutes until water disappears. Add milk, eggs, and cook slowly 45 minutes or until rice is tender. Flavor with vanilla extract. Serve.
