½ cup butter

½ cup sugar

½ cup molasses

2 teaspoons cinnamon

2 teaspoons ginger

¼ teaspoon cloves

1 ¾ cups flour

1 teaspoons baking soda

⅔ cup sour cream or plain yogurt

  

Work butter and sugar until white and fluffy; add eggs and spices. Add flour and baking soda sifted together alternately with sour cream. Stir until well blended. 

  

Pour into buttered 9 inch square pan and bake in 325F oven for 15 minutes. Reduce heat to 250F and continue to bake 30 minutes longer. Serve with whipping cream.
