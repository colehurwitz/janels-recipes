4 small zucchini

2 tablespoons olive oil

1 pound Italian sausage

Salt

Pasta

3 cloves garlic

1 tablespoon chopped rosemary

¼ cup flat leaf parsley

1 cup diced tomatoes

½ cup heavy cream or half and half

¼ cup romano cheese, grated

  

Bring water to boil; add 2 tablespoons salt; cook pasta to al dente (as you’re doing the rest of the recipe).

  

Cut zucchini in ½; scoop out pulp; chop. Cut shells into ¼ inch slices.

  

Add oil. Cook sausage.

  

Add zucchini pulp, garlic, rosemary, and parsley with sausage and cook for 3 minutes.

  

Add diced tomatoes; cook 5 minutes more.

  

Add heavy cream; cook 2 minutes.

  

Stir in Romano.

  

About 30 seconds before pasta is done, toss zucchini slices into boiling water with pasta. Drain pasta and zucchini. Add to sauce, toss. Add pasta water as needed.
