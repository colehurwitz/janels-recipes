3 pounds chicken skinned (dark/white meat combo), cut into small pieces

  

Marinade:

  

1 medium onion

1 inch piece of ginger

5 cloves garlic

3 tablespoons yogurt

  

Grind together in a blender. Add 1 teaspoon salt, 2 teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoon coriander powder and chili powder to taste. Pour the marinade mixture over the chicken pieces. Mix well and refrigerate overnight. Pierce chicken with a fork for better absorption.

  

Basting Mixture (usually we double this):

  

¼ cup olive oil

2 tablespoons ketchup

2 tablespoons soy sauce

1 teaspoon coriander

1 teaspoon cumin

  

Mix the above ingredients well together. Apply with a basting brush on each side as it is being cooked. Reapply mixture as it dries out, keep the chicken moist. 

  

Serve with Channa and basmati rice or nan.
