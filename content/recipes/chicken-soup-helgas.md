1 three to four pound chicken, whole

16 cups water

1 ½ tablespoons salt

1 teaspoon pepper

5 stalks of celery with green leaves, diced

4-5 carrots, diced

1 large onion, chopped

  

Put everything in a large pot, bring to boil, and simmer for about 2 hours with lid on. Prepare matzoh balls according to package (Manischewitz brand only - the other ones aren’t as good). Remove entire chicken and cool off. Discard skin. Pell and shred meat. Skim fat. Return meat to pot. Bring back to boil. Cook matzoh balls according to the package. Cook until they are done (recipe says about 20 minutes total). Serve\!
