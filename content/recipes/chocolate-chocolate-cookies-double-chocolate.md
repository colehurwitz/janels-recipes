6 ounces unsweetened chocolate, melted

⅓ cup flour

¼ teaspoon baking powder

¼ cup (½ stick) butter

2 eggs

1 ⅓ cups sugar

¼ teaspoon salt

1 ½ teaspoons finely ground coffee

1 teaspoon vanilla

½ cup bittersweet chocolate chips

Preheat oven to 350F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper.

  

Whisk together flour and baking powder. Set aside.

Place butter and chocolate in a large metal bowl or the top of a double boiler set over a pan of gently simmering water (or melt on low power in microwave). Stir occasionally until melted and smooth. Remove from heat and set aside.

Meanwhile combine eggs, sugar, salt, coffee, and vanilla. Add the melted chocolate mixture. Fold in dry ingredients by hand. Fold in bittersweet chocolate chips. 

Drop the dough in heaping tablespoons onto the prepared baking sheets. Bake about 12 minutes. When they are set, the cookies will have a matte look rather than a shiny one, and their tops will gently crack. Allow the cookies to cool on a rack.
