4 cups berry puree and 4 cups berry with pulp

4 cups sugar

4 cups light corn syrup

1 tablespoon lemon juice

  

(If using blueberries, use 2 cups sugar and 2 cups corn syrup. Use crushed blueberries)

Place juice, sugar, corn syrup in saucepan. Boil for 3 to 5 minutes. Place boiling hot syrup in clean jars, add lids, place in boiling water bath and process for 10 minutes. Makes about 7 pints.
