APRICOT:

  

5 cups chopped apricots

¼ cup fresh lemon juice

7 cups sugar

  

Measure exact amounts. Stir 1 package SURE JELL pectin into prepared fruit (and lemon juice) in pot. Add ½ teaspoon butter to reduce foaming. Bring to rolling boil (a boil that doesn’t stop when it’s stirred).

  

Add sugar. Return to rolling boil, stirring constantly; boil 1 minute stirring constantly. Remove from heat.

  

Ladle into prepared jars\* to within ¼ of top of rim. Wipe jar rims and threads (so that you get a good seal between lid and jar). Cover with two piece lids (screw top and lid - always use new lids). Place in boiling water in canner. Cover with 2 inches of water. Cover pot. Boil 10 minutes (if at sea level, add 5 minutes if at 1001 to 3000 feet, 10 minutes if 3001 to 6000 feet). Remove from pot. Allow to cool. Test lids by pressing center of lid. If it springs back, it is not properly sealed and you should store this jar in the refrigerator.

  

To prepare jars, wash them in dishwasher and keep them there until ready to use. Place new lids in pan with simmering water while you prepare the jam.

  

BLACKBERRY/RASPBERRY JAM

  

5 cups prepared fruit

7 cups sugar

  

Crush berries; if desired, sieve ½ to remove seeds.

  

BLUEBERRY JAM

  

4 cups chopped blueberries

4 cups sugar

  

Mash blueberries

  

CHERRY SOUR JAM

  

4 cups chopped fruit

4 ¾ cups sugar

  

Pit and dice fruit.

  

PEACH JAM

  

4 cups chopped peaches

2 tablespoons lemon juice

5 ½ cups sugar

  

Peel, pit and finely chop peaches.

  

STRAWBERRY JAM

  

5 cups crushed strawberries

7 cups sugar

  

Crush strawberries

  

GRAPE JELLY

  

5 cups prepared juice

7 cups sugar

  

CURRANT JELLY

  

6 ½ cups prepared juice

7 cups sugar
