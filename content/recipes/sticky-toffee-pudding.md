16 pitted and chopped Medjool dates (about 1 cup)

3/4 cup hot water      Dash salt

1 teaspoon baking soda     2 teaspoons vanilla

175 grams/ 1 ¼ cups flour    3 tablespoons dark brown sugar 

1 teaspoon baking powder    55g/4 tablespoons butter,

2 eggs        melted and cooled

  

Toffee sauce:

  

¼ cup dark brown sugar

115 grams/ ¼ cup butter, melted and cooled

60 grams/ ¼ cup heavy cream at room temperature

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Butter and flour an 8 inch round cake pan. Line bottom with parchment paper.

  

Put dates in a pot; add boiling water. Add the baking soda; stir to dissolve. The baking soda will soften the skins of the dates. Bring to boil; cook over very low heat until dates are soft and water is mostly absorbed by dates.

  

With a food processor, combine dates (and water) and process until smooth. Move to mixing bowl. Add flour, baking powder, salt, vanilla, and brown sugar and mix until nicely combined. Add butter, eggs and process until mixed.

  

Pour into prepared pan. Bake for 30 to 45 minutes or until the cake is golden brown and springs back when you press it in the center. Remove from oven; let cool.

  

Meanwhile make sauce. In medium bowl, whisk together brown sugar, melted butter  until combined. Add cream. Cook until boils; remove from heat; let sauce sit at room temperature as cake bakes. Poke holes in cake; pour sauce evenly over top of cake while warm. When cake is completely cool, invert onto plate and again onto serving plate so it is right side up. 

  

Serve with sauce. I also served with vanilla ice cream. 

  
  
  
  

**VARIATION THAT I LIKE:**

  

Sticky Toffee Sauce: 

  

2 cups (480 ml) heavy cream

½ cup (99g) dark brown sugar

2 ½ tablespoons molasses

Pinch salt

  

For the “pudding”: 

  

6 ounces (170 g) pitted, chopped dates (I would compare this amount to the amount      in the original recipe and use the larger amount)

1 cup (240 ml) water

1 teaspoon baking soda

1 ¼ cups (150 g) flour

1 teaspoon baking powder

 ½ tsp salt

4 tablespoons (57 g) unsalted butter

¾ cup (149) granulated sugar (I would compare this amount to the amount and type in the original recipe and potentially use the smaller amount)

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla (the other recipe calls for 2 teaspoons vanilla - use your judgment)

  

Preheat oven to 350F and butter an  8 ½ inch baking dish.

  

Make the toffee sauce by bringing the cream, dark brown sugar, molasses, and salt to a boil in a medium saucepan, stirring often to melt the sugar.

Lower heat and simmer, stirring for about 5 minutes, until the mixture is thick and coats the spoon. 

Pour half the sauce into the prepared baking dish and place it in the freezer. 

Save the other half of the sauce for serving.

  

To make the “pudding,” in a medium sauce pan, heat the dates and water. Once the water begins to boil, remove from heat and stir in the baking soda. Set aside.

  

In a small bowl, whisk together the flour baking powder and salt.

  

In a bowl, beat the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Gradually beat in the eggs, then the vanilla.

  

Stir in half the flour mixture, then date mixture, then the remaining flour mixture. Don’t overmix.

  

Remove baking pan from freezer and scrape batter into the pan. Bake for 50 minutes or until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs attached.

  

Remove from oven and let cool slightly. Spoon portions into serving bowls and douse with additional warm toffee sauce. Top with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream if desired.
