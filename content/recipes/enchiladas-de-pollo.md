2 whole chicken breasts    1/2 cup cilantro leaves

Water       3/4 cup whipping cream

½ onion      1 egg

1 bay leaf      oil

8 peppercorns     8 corn tortillas

Salt       4 ounces grated cheese 

½ medium onion, chopped   Guacamole

3 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese Sour cream

1 - 4 ounce can green chiles   Olives 

1 - 13 ounce can tomatillos

  

Place chicken breast in a large pot or Dutch oven. Add water to cover, onion, bay leaf, peppercorns and salt to taste. Bring to boil; reduce heat; cover and simmer 45 minutes or until shreddably tender. Cool. Drain reserving broth. 

  

Shred chicken with 2 forks or your fingers. Mix shredded chicken, chopped onion, parmesan cheese, and 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese. Taste and add salt if needed. Set aside.

  

In a food processor, combine green chiles, tomatillos, cilantro, whipping cream, and egg. Blend until smooth. Add salt to taste. Set aside. Preheat oven to 350F.

  

Heat oil in small skillet. With tongs, carefully place tortilla in hot oil. Hold in oil for 3 to 5 seconds until softened. Quickly turn tortilla and soften other side. Drain on paper towels. 

  

Place ⅛  of chicken mixture on each tortilla, pressing mixture to make it compact. Roll tightly and place seam-side down in a 13x9 inch pan. Pour chile-cream mixture over enchiladas and sprinkle evenly with shredded cheese. 

  

Bake 20 minutes or until heated through.

  

Serve with guacamole, olives, and sour cream.
