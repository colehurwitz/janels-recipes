1 small head cauliflower, cut into pieces

1 large onion, chopped

3 carrots, cut into pieces (not necessary to peel)

2-3 potatoes, cut into pieces (2 large or 3 medium)

Chicken broth to cover (you can use water but will need more salt)

Salt and pepper to taste

  

Half and half (to taste)

1 cup (or more if you like) packed shredded extra sharp cheddar cheese

  

Put cauliflower, onion, carrots, potatoes into pot, use enough chicken broth to barely cover ingredients. It will be at least one quart, possibly two. Or you can use one quart chicken broth and use water for the rest. Season with some salt and pepper. Start with about 1 teaspoon salt if you solely use chicken broth. Let it cook for a while and then taste it and add salt until you are satisfied.

  

Bring ingredients to a boil; reduce to simmer. Let cook until potatoes are tender (everything else will be tender if potatoes are tender). Turn off heat. Use immersion blender (google it) to puree (alternately you can puree in a blender doing ¼ of the ingredients at a time). 

  

Once it is pureed, taste again and adjust seasonings. Then add ¼ cup half and half and see if it adds enough to make the soup like creamy. If not, add a little more until you are satisfied with the appearance. Then mix in shredded cheese, stirring until it melts. Even if you don’t like sharp cheese, you need to use it because if you don’t use it you won’t even notice there’s cheese in there\!
