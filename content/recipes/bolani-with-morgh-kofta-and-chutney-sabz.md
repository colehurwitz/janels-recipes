(AFGHAN STUFFED FLATBREAD WITH CHICKEN)

  

For the Bolani:

  

3 ¾ cups (485g) flour

2 teaspoons kosher salt

1 ½ teaspoons instant yeast

1 ½ cups (360 ml) lukewarm water

1 tablespoon sunflower oil, plus for for frying

  

For the Morgh Kofta filling:

  

2 ½  tablespoons sunflower oil

1 large yellow onion, finely chopped

1 pound ground chicken (preferable dark meat)

2 teaspoons kosher salt

1 teaspoon garlic powder

1 teaspoon ground red pepper, such as cayenne

1 teaspoon coriander

freshly ground pepper to taste

  

For the Chutney Sabz:

  

1 large handful fresh mint leaves

1 large handful fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems

2 fresh long green chiles, such as serrano, seeds removed, coarsely chopped

1 garlic cloves, minced

Juice of 2 limes

2 ½ tablespoons white vinegar

2 ½ tablespoons cold water

1 teaspoon kosher salt

Plain yogurt for serving

  

Make the Bolani dough: In a medium bowl, stir the flour, salt, and yeast to combine. Create a well in the center of the mixture and slowly add enough water to just wet the ingredients, about ¼ cup (60 ml), mixing to distribute evenly.

  

While continuing to mix, add the oil, then slowly add the remaining water and knead until dough is firm. If the dough feels too dry, add a little more water; if it feels too wet, add a bit more flour.

Oil the inside of a medium bowl. Shape the dough into a ball, place it in the bowl, and cover with a clean kitchen towel. Set aside dough to rest for about 30 minutes or until doubled.

  

Make the filling: In a large skillet over med-high head, heat the oil until just shimmering. Add the onion and cook, stirring until golden and soft, about 4-6 minutes. Add ground chicken, salt, garlic powder, red pepper, coriander, and black pepper and stir to combine well. Cook until the chicken is cooked through but not overly browned, about 10 minutes. Transfer to colander and allow excess oil to drain.

  

Make the chutney: Rinse the mint and cilantro and put them, along with the chiles and garlic, into a food processor or blender and process into a coarse paste. Add the lime juice, vinegar, water, and salt and pulse to just combine.

  

Return to the dough: Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Divide the dough into 6 equal portions and shape into a small ball. Place on baking sheet, at least 2” apart. Let rest 10 minutes, or until doubled in size. 

  

On a lightly floured surface, using a lightly floured rolling pin, roll out one ball of dough to a 5-6” circle. Place about ¼ cup of chicken filling on one half of the circle and spread to cover half, leaving ½ ” border. Fold empty half of the circle over the filling to form a semicircle, firmly press the edges together. Repeat with remaining dough and filling.

 

In a heavy bottomed skillet over medium-high heat, add just enough oil to cover the base of the pan and heat until shimmering. Lift the bolani carefully into the pan and fry, turning once, until golden brown and crisp, about 4 minutes on each side.

  

Serve hot with chutney and plain yogurt.
