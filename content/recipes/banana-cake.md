3 cups flour

1 cup sugar 

1 ½ teaspoons baking soda

¾ cups butter, softened

1 ½ cups mashed bananas

2 teaspoons vanilla extract

1 ½ cups buttermilk

3 eggs

2 teaspoons lemon juice

  

Preheat oven to 275F. Grease and flour 9x13 pan. In a small bowl, mash bananas with lemon juice; set aside. Mix flour, baking soda; set aside. Cream butter and sugar. Beat in eggs, then vanilla. Beat in flour alternately with buttermilk. Stir in crushed banana. Pour batter into prepared pan and bake 1 hour. Place directly into freezer for 45 minutes. 

  

Frosting (this frosting also works for other cakes, like carrot, etc.):

  

8 ounces cream cheese, room temperature

4 tablespoons butter, softened

1 ¾ cups powdered sugar, sifted to remove lumps

1 teaspoon vanilla

Juice of 1 lemon (to taste)

Lemon zest

  

Mix cream cheese until smooth. Add butter. Add vanilla. Mix in powdered sugar (start with less and add until you reach desired sweetness). Add juice of 1 lemon and zest (add more lemon juice if you like).
