16 ounces lentils

1 pound sausage

2 tablespoons olive oil

1 ½ cups onions, diced

1 cup carrots, diced

1 teaspoon salt

Pepper to taste

1 quart chicken broth

2 tablespoons dijon mustard

1 tablespoon red wine vinegar

Heavy cream to taste (or use cashew crema\!)

3 cups spinach, roughly chopped

  

In a heavy bottomed pan add olive oil and heat. Remove casing from sausage (if you use links), crumble and cook sausage. Add onions, carrots, salt and pepper to pot. Cook until crisp tender. Add lentils, sausage, and chicken stock. Stir and bring to boil. When soup starts to boil, reduce heat to low and simmer until lentils are tender. If soup is too thick, add more chicken broth. When lentils are done, add cream, mustard, vinegar, and spinach. Simmer an additional 5 minutes. Serve\!
