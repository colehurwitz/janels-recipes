Greek Almond Cookies with Pistachio

  
  

2 cups (192 grams) almond flour

1 ¼ cups (150 grams) powdered sugar, sifted to remove lumps

¼ cup egg whites (55 grams), unbeaten

2 teaspoons almond extract

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

¼ cup finely ground raw pistachios, unsalted

  

Mix powdered sugar and almond flour in mixing bowl. Mix in unbeaten egg whites and vanilla and almond extracts. Shape into 1 tablespoon (approximately) sized bell shapes, roll top of cookie in pistachios. Place 2 inches apart on a parchment-lined cookie sheet.

  

Bake in oven at 325F for 18-20 minutes.
