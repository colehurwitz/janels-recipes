Generous 2 cups cooked wild rice

⅓ pound sausage

¼ pound mushrooms, sliced

½ onion, chopped

3 cloves garlic, crushed

⅔ tablespoon marjoram

⅔ tablespoon sage

½ pound spinach

½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

½ cup white wine

Salt, pepper to taste

  

Add sausage to frying pan, breaking into small bits until lightly browned. Transfer to paper towels, then put in large bowl. Add 2 tablespoons olive oil and onion to frying pan. Add garlic, mushrooms, marjoram, and sage. Stir often until vegetables are limp. Add spinach. Stir until greens are wilted and liquid has evaporated. Add to bowl. Add rice, cheese, ½ cup wine. Season with salt and pepper. Heat in 350F oven; serve\!
