6 eggs, whisk

75g caster sugar

300g cake flour

Whisk together gently.

Add 750ml milk, little by little

Stir after adding.

Pour in 100g melted butter until incorporated.

Sift batter again.

Cover; refrigerate 30 minutes.

Stir again after chilled - it should have thickened.

  

Heat pan and add a little oil.

  

Use about 1/2 cup batter for 22 cm pan.

  

40 seconds first side (until it starts to bubble)

20 seconds second side.

  

This recipe makes about 19 crepes.

  

You can use this to make a crepe cake with mango or strawberry or just eat individually with different toppings\!
