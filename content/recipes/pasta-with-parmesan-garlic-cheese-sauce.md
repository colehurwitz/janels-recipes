---

⅓ cup butter

¼ cup flour

3 cups 2% milk

1 tablespoon chicken bouillon

1 clove garlic, minced

1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

  

Melt butter and add garlic. Then add flour. Stir until well-blended. Gradually stir in milk and bouillon. Cook, stirring constantly until sauce is thickened. Stir in grated Parmesan cheese. Serve with pasta.
