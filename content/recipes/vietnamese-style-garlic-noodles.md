4 tablespoons unsalted butter

20 medium garlic cloves, minced

4 teaspoons oyster sauce

2 teaspoons soy sauce

2 teaspoons fish sauce

1 pound pasta

Parmesan/Romano grated

Thinly sliced scallions

  

Melt the butter in a wok over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant but not browned. Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, and fish sauce, and stir to combine. Remove from heat.

  

Meanwhile, bring cook pasta until just shy of al dente. 

  

Using tongs, transfer the cooked pasta to the garlic sauce along with whatever water clings to it. Turn heat to medium, add the cheese to the wok, and stir until the sauce is creamy and emulsified. Stir in scallions. Serve\!
