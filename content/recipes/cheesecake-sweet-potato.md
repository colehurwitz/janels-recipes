Crust:

  

1 ¼ cups graham cracker crumbs

¼ cup sugar

¼ cup melted butter

  

Filling:

  

1 ¼ cup pureed sweet potato

3 - 8 ounce packages cream cheese

¾ cup plus 2 tbsp sugar

⅓ cup sour cream

¼  cup whipping cream (can use half n half)

3 eggs

  

Topping:

  

¾ cup brown sugar, packed

¼ cup butter

¼ cup whipping cream (can use half n half)

1 cup chopped pecans

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Mix together graham cracker crumbs, ¼ cup sugar, ¼ cup melted butter. Press mixture onto bottom of 9 inch springform pan. Bake 10 minutes; remove from oven; set aside. Keep oven on.

  

Beat cream cheese, ¾  cup plus 2 tbsp sugar until smooth. Mix in sour cream, ¼ cup whipping cream, 1 ½ cup pureed sweet potatoes.  Beat in eggs one at a time, blending well. Pour filling into crust.

  

Bake in 350F oven until tester inserted near center comes out clean, about 1 hour. Turn off oven, let cake stand 1 hour in oven with door ajar.

  

Combine brown sugar and ¼ cup butter in small saucepan. Stir until sugar is dissolved. Increase heat, and bring to boil. Mix in ¼ cup cream, then nuts. Pour hot topping over cheesecake.
