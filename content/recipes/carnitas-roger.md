1 boneless pork shoulder roast, about 3 pounds

1 teaspoon kosher salt

½ teaspoon black pepper

2 tablespoons avocado oil or other oil with high smoke point

2 yellow onions, sliced

1 sofrito puck 

½ cup chicken broth

  

Cut pork roast into 4 inch cubes and season with ½ teaspoon salt and all of the pepper (pieces can be irregular). Select Saute setting on the Instant Pot, adjust the heat level to More, and heat the oil. Using tongs, place the pork cubes into the pot. Sear pork for about 6 minutes until well browned on the first side. Flip the pieces over and sear for about 6 minutes longer, until well browned on the second side. Using the tongs, transfer the pork to a plate. Add the onions and remaining ½ teaspoon salt and saute for about 5 minutes, until onions are softened. Stir in the broth and the sofrito puck then return the seared pork to the pot and stir to coat it with the sauce. Arrange pork pieces in a single layer.

  

Secure the lid and set the Pressure Release to Sealing. Press the Cancel button to reset the cooking program, then select the Meat/Stew setting and set the cooking time for 50 minutes at high pressure.

Perform a quick release by moving the Pressure Release to Venting. Open the pot and, using the tongs or a slotted spoon, transfer pork to a carving board. Shred.

  

Use as basis for burritos.

  

SOFRITO PUCK recipe (makes 8 pucks): 

  

1 large yellow onion     1 tablespoon capers, rinsed

1 seeded red bell pepper     2 teaspoons kosher salt

8 cloves garlic      1 teaspoon black pepper

1 large bunch cilantro     1 teaspoon oregano

¼ cup pimiento-stuffed green olives   ½ cup olive oil

  

Combine all ingredients into a food processor until a thick paste forms. Divide in 8. Freeze and store for future use.
