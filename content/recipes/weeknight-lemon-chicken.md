title: Weeknight Lemon Chicken
category: Dinner

Bright, garlicky, and on the table in half an hour.

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs
- 2 lemons (juice of both, zest of one)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Pat the chicken dry and season well with salt and pepper.
2. Whisk the lemon juice, zest, garlic, oil, and oregano.
3. Sear the chicken skin-side down in a hot pan until golden, about 6 minutes.
4. Flip, pour in the lemon mixture, and simmer 12–15 minutes until cooked through.
5. Spoon the pan sauce over the top to serve.
