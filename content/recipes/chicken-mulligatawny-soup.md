2 tablespoons olive oil

1 large onion, chopped (about 2 cups)

2 ribs celery, chopped (about 1 cup)

2 carrots chopped (about 1 cup)

2 bay leaves

4 teaspoons yellow curry powder

1 ¼ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs (can use breasts as well)

2 cups chicken stock

2 cups water

1 ½ teaspoons salt

¼ cup uncooked rice

2 tart apples, cored, peeled, and chopped (about 2 cups)

¼ cup heavy whipping cream (or use cashew crema\!)

  

Saute onions, celery, carrots in olive oil. Add bay leaves, curry powder. Mix to coat. Add the chicken thighs and stir to coat with the curry mixture. Add the stock and water to the pot. Add salt. Bring to a simmer and reduce the heat to maintain a simmer. Cover and cook for 20 minutes. Remove chicken pieces from the pot. Place on cutting board and allow to cool. Add the rice and chopped apples to the soup. Return to a simmer on high heat, then lower the heat to maintain a low simmer. Cover and cook for 15 minutes, or until the rice is cooked through. Shred the chicken. Add back into the pot. Heat for 5 minutes, stir in cream (or use cashew crema\!). Serve\!
