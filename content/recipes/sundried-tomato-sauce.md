12 ounces pasta

1 pound shrimp or other protein

½ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon pepper

1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

½ teaspoon paprika

  

4 tablespoons butter

4 good sized cloves garlic, minced

½ teaspoon to 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes

8 ounces sundried tomatoes, drain but keep oil (3 ounces chopped; 5 ounces pureed)

2 tablespoons tomato paste

1 cup white wine (or whatever leftover wine you have)

1 cup (to taste, less or more) heavy cream

3 cups spinach

Parmesan cheese, grated

  

Cook pasta, save ¼ cup pasta water.

Season shrimp with salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, paprika. Heat skillet over medium-high heat. Once hot, add shrimp. Cook 2-3 minutes per side. Remove shrimp from pan. If what remains is watery, cook water off. Then:

  

Add butter to skillet. Once melted, add garlic, red pepper flakes, all of the sundried tomatoes, onions. Saute a couple of minutes. Next add tomato paste, cook 1 minutes, stirring constantly. Add in white whine, scrape brown bits, and simmer till liquid is reduced. Pour in heavy cream. Add in spinach (no need to chop). Cook till reduced. Turn up heat, add pasta water. Let simmer 2 minutes until its slightly reduced. Add in shrimp and pasta. Serve with grated parmesan.
