½ pound smoked salmon

½ pound cream cheese

2 tablespoons sour cream

1 tablespoon mayonnaise

Juice of one lemon

1 teaspoon tabasco

Pepper to taste

¼ teaspoon dill

Pinch of paprika

  

Flake salmon, add remaining ingredients, blend.
