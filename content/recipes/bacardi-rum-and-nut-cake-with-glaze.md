1 package yellow cake mix

1 package (3.5 ounces) vanilla instant pudding mix

½ cup rum

1 cup pecans or walnuts

4 eggs

½ cup vegetable oil

½ cup cold water

Preheat oven to 325F. Grease and flour a bundt pan. Sprinkle nuts in the bottom of the bundt pan. In a large mixing bowl, combine cake mix, vanilla pudding mix, eggs, rum, oil, and water. Beat at low speed until moistened. Then beat at high speed for 2 minutes. Pour batter over nuts in bundt pan. Bake for 1 hour or until a toothpick inserted into center comes out clean. Cool 15 minutes, then invert onto serving plate and glaze.

Glaze:

½ cup rum

½ stick butter

1 cup sugar

¼ cup water

  

Melt butter in saucepan. Stir in sugar, water, and rum. Boil 5 minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from heat. Poke holes in the top of the cake with toothpick or skewer. Spoon glaze evenly over the top and sides. Allow cake to absorb glaze. Repeat until all the glaze is absorbed.
