1 Bunch Chard

1 lemon, juice and zest

1 ½ cups crushed croutons\*

¾ cup grated parmesan cheese

¼ cup olive oil

Small garlic clove, minced

Salt to taste

  

Remove stems from chard. Roll them like a cigar and cut into thin ⅛” slices. Put in salad bowl. Zest lemon over chard.

  

Put lemon juice in small bowl. Add a pinch or two of salt. Add minced garlic. Slowly whisk in olive oil. 

  

Chop croutons\* finely in chopper. Sprinkle over chard. Add grated Parmesan or Romano. Pour in ⅔ of dressing. Toss to coat. Add more dressing if desired.

  

\*If you don’t have tasty croutons available, you can make them by chopping plain croutons or seasoning breadcrumbs by adding a  minced garlic clove, 3-4 tablespoons of olive oil, a dash or two of sea salt, and cooking it all together until they are toasted. Don’t burn :). Set aside to cool.
