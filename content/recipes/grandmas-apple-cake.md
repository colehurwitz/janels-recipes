A cosy, spiced single-layer cake studded with soft apples — the kind that fills
the whole kitchen with cinnamon while it bakes.

## Ingredients

- 3 medium apples, peeled and chopped
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 large eggs
- ½ cup neutral oil
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1½ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and butter a 9-inch round pan.
2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt together.
3. In a larger bowl, beat the eggs, sugar, oil, and vanilla until smooth.
4. Stir in the dry mix until just combined, then fold in the apples.
5. Spread into the pan and bake 40–45 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
6. Cool 15 minutes before slicing.
