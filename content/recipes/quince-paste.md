4 pounds quince, washed, peeled, cored, roughly chopped

1 vanilla pod, split (optional)

2 strips (½”x2”) lemon peel (only yellow, no white)

3 tablespoons lemon juice

About 4 cups sugar

(You make a smaller batch by reducing the amount of quince and sugar)

  

Place quince pieces in a large saucepan and cover with water. Add vanilla pod, lemon peel, lemon juice, sugar (add less sugar and taste to make sure you don’t add too much), and bring to a boil. 

  

Reduce to simmer, cover, and let cook until the quince pieces are fork tender (30-40 minutes) and mixture reaches 220F.

  

Strain extra water from the quince pieces. Discard vanilla pod but keep lemon peel with quince. The quince paste should be thick and have a deep orange color.

  

If after cooling you don’t think it’s thick enough to be a paste, you can cook it at 125F in the oven (line a 8x8 baking pan with parchment paper, grease parchment with a thin coating of butter). 

  

Smooth out the top of the paste so it is even. Cook for about an hour to help it dry. Remove from the oven and let cool.

  

To serve, take a small slice and put it on top of Manchego or other cheese.
