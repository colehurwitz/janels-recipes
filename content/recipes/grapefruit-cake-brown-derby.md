Cake:

  

1 ½ cups cake flour or all purpose flour

¾ sugar (on the shy side)

1 ½ teaspoons baking powder

¼ teaspoon salt

¼ cup water

¼ cup vegetable oil (Safflower)

3 eggs, separated

¼ cup grapefruit juice

Zest of 1 grapefruit

½ teaspoon cream of tartar

  

Frosting:

  

1 - 8 ounce package cream cheese

2 teaspoons lemon juice

1 teaspoon lemon zest

½ cup powdered sugar

1 teaspoon grapefruit zest

2 teaspoon grapefruit juice

Grapefruit segments (membrane removed), crushed, and mixed into frosting

¼ cup whipping cream

  

Decoration:

  

Grapefruit segments to layer on frosting (remove membrane)

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Butter and flour 9 inch round cake pan; line with parchment. Beat egg whites and cream of tartar until whites are stiff but not dry. Set aside. Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in mixing bowl. Make a well in center of dry ingredients. Add water, oil, egg yolks, grapefruit juice, lemon and grapefruit zest. Beat until smooth. Gradually pour egg yolk mixture over whites, folding gently with a rubber spatula until just blended. Do not stir mixture. Pour into an ungreased pan. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until cake springs back when lightly touched with finger. Let cool; remove from pan. With serrated knife, gently cut layer in half.

  

Let cream cheese soften at room temperature. Beat cream cheese until fluffy and smooth, about 5 minutes. Add lemon juice, grapefruit juice, and zests. Add in sugar. Beat until well-blended. Add 1/4 cup whipping cream and whip till good consistency (taste for preferred sweetness; add more sifted powdered sugar if you prefer it sweeter or add lemon juice if it should be more tart). Mix in grapefruit sections (about 2-3 tablespoons).

  

Spread frosting on top of bottom half of cake. Top with several grapefruit sections. Cover with second layer. Frost top and sides, and garnish with remaining grapefruit pieces.
