Buy puff pastry or make puff pastry:

2 cups minus 2 tablespoons flour

¼ teaspoon salt

¾ cup plus two tablespoons water

16 tablespoons unsalted butter room temperature, stirred to smooth

  

For the custard:

3 tablespoons flour     ⅔ cup water

1 ¼ cups milk, divided    ½ teaspoon vanilla

1 ⅓ cups granulated sugar   6 egg yolks

1 cinnamon stick     Powdered sugar

Cinnamon

  

To make the dough:

  

In a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook, mix the flour, salt, and water until a soft, pillowy dough forms that cleans the side of the bowl, about 30 seconds. Generously flour a work surface and pat the dough into a 6 inch square. Cover with plastic wrap; let rest 15 minutes.

Roll the dough into an 18 inch square. Lift dough with scraper to make sure the underside is not sticking. Brush excess flour off the top, trim uneven edges, spread ⅔ of the dough with ⅓ of the butter to within 1 inch of the edge. Neatly fold over the unbuttered right third of the dough, brush off excess flour, then fold over the left third. Pat down to release air bubbles, pinch edges closed. Brush off excess flour.

  

Turn dough 90 degrees to the left, lift dough and flour work surface. Roll out dough to an 18 inch square, then dot and spread the left ⅔ of the dough with ⅓ of the butter. Fold as described above. Roll as described above. Roll out to 18x21 inch rectangle. Spread remaining butter over entire surface. Roll into tight log. Trim ends and cut the log in half. Wrap in plastic wrap and chill for 2 hours.

  

Make the custard:

  

Whisk flour and ¼ cup milk until smooth. Set aside. Bring sugar, cinnamon, and water to boil in a small saucepan and cook to 220F. Do not stir. 

Meanwhile in another small saucepan, scald the remaining 1 cup milk. Whisk the hot milk into the flour mixture.

  

Remove the cinnamon stick; then pour the sugar syrup in a thin stream into the hot milk and flour mixture, whisking briskly. Add the vanilla and stir for a minute until very warm but not hot. Whisk in the yolks, strain the mixture into a bowl, cover with plastic wrap; set aside.

  
  
  
  

Assemble and bake:

  

Heat oven to 550F. Remove pastry log from refrigerator, roll until it is about an inch in diameter and 16 inches long. Cut into scant 3/4 inch pieces. Place a piece cut side down in each well of a non-stick mini-muffin tin. Allow dough to soften until pliable.

  

Dip your thumbs into the water, then flatten the dough blob to the bottom and rise up the sides, creating a raised lip about ⅛ inch above the pan. The pastry should be thinner than the bottom.

Fill each cup ¾ full with the slightly warm custard. Bake until the edges of the dough are brown, about 8 to 9 minutes.

  

To serve:

  

Remove oven from the pan an allow the pastels to cool a few minutes in the pan. Then transfer to a rack and cool until just warm. Sprinkle generously with powdered sugar, then cinnamon and serve.
