1 pound ground beef

¾ cup chopped onions

¾ cup chopped green bell pepper

½ teaspoon garlic powder

1 tablespoon mustard powder

⅓ cup ketchup

3 tablespoons brown sugar

Salt to taste

Pepper to taste

  

Cook and drain ground beef in pan. Add onions, green pepper, spices. Cook until onions and green pepper are tender. Serve over toasted bun.
