Biga:

½ cup flour

¼ cup lukewarm water

pinch of yeast

  

Dough:

2 cups flour

¾ cup water

2 tablespoons olive oil

1 ½ teaspoons salt

1 teaspoon instant yeast

  

Biga: Mix the biga ingredients in a small bowl, let the ingredients rest overnight.

  

Dough: In a large bowl, combine the biga and the remainder of the dough ingredients, stirring until the dough starts to leave the side of the bowl. Transfer the dough to a lightly greased surface, oil your hands, and knead it for 5-7 minutes, or until it’s supple but the surface is still somewhat rough. 

  

Transfer the dough to a lightly greased bowl, cover the bowl, and allow the dough to rise for two hours, turning it over and gently deflating it after one hour.

  

To make the pizza, preheat oven and baking stone to 500F for at least an hour. Roll the dough very thin and lay it on a piece of parchment paper cut to size. Top it lightly with your favorite toppings. 

  

Using a peel, transfer the pizza and parchment to the hot stone and bake until crisp, 10-15 minutes. Makes one 14 inch pizza.
