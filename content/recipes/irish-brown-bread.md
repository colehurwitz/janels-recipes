150 ml (5 ounces), plus 275 ml (10 ounces) tepid water (425 ml total)

1 tablespoons molasses

2 ½ teaspoons active dry yeast

1 teaspoons salt

1 cup (100 grams) bread flour 

3 cups (442g) wholemeal flour

  

Spray a 9” loaf pan.

  

Mix the flours with the salt in a medium bowl.

  

Pour 150ml water into a small bowl and stir in the molasses, then stir in yeast. Let stand until it starts to foam on top, about 10 minutes.

  

Pour yeast mixture and the remaining 275 ml water into the flour and stir until a batter is formed, which will have the consistency of oatmeal (dough will be sticky and rather wet). Let stand 10 minutes.

  

Scrape dough into a prepared pan, smooth the top with a spatula or dampen your hand and use that. Then drape a kitchen towel over the top loosely and let rise in a warm place until the dough reaches the top of the pan, about 20 minutes.

  

Before the dough has almost reached the top of the pan, preheat the oven to 450F. When the dough has reached the top of the pan, bake the bread for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes, decrease the heat to 400F. Release bread from pan, and place the loaf upside down directly on the baking rack and bake another 15 minutes until it sounds hollow when you tap the bottom (using an instant read thermometer, bread is done at 190F.
