2 onions

1 package sliced beef (sukiyaki beef)

1 cup dried shiitake mushrooms

Spinach, carrots

Vegetable oil

1 teaspoon Dashi powder

2/3 cup soy sauce

5 teaspoon sugar

⅓ cup sake

⅓ cup mirin

½ cup water

  

Soak mushrooms in water to rehydrate. Cook the beef and onions until done. Cook the spinach and carrots. Mix Dashi, soy sauce, sugar, sake, mirin, and water as needed. Simmer beef and vegetables with sauce. Ladle over rice.
