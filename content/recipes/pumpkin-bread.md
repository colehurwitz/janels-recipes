1 cup butter

¾ cup sugar

¾ cup packed dark brown sugar

4 eggs

1 cup pumpkin

2 ¾ cups flour

2 teaspoons baking powder

1 teaspoon baking soda

1 ½ teaspoons cinnamon

½ teaspoon ginger

¼ teaspoon nutmeg

1 cup chopped pecans (optional)

  

Cream butter and sugar. Add eggs, beat in pumpkin. Add flour, spices, baking powder, baking soda (and pecans) to the pumpkin mixture. Mix just until blended. Spoon into two greased and floured loaf pans. 

  

Bake at 350F for 1 hour 10 minutes. Cool 10 minutes. Remove from pans, cool on wire racks.
