15 ounce can black beans (drained and rinsed)

2 tablespoons lemon juice

½ teaspoon salt

3 pinches black pepper

¼ cup olive oil

6 medium garlic cloves, minced

  

Combine beans, lemon juice, salt, pepper in a food processor and purée until smooth, scraping down the side of the bowl to get everything. Preheat a small pan over low heat. Cook the garlic in olive oil for 3 minutes (DO NOT brown it). Add the garlic and olive oil to the mixture in the blender and purée. Taste for salt, pepper and lemon; add as needed.
