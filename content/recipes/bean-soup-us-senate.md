1 pound dried navy beans

1 meaty smoked ham shank

1 large onion, peeled and chopped

3 carrots diced

Salt and pepper to taste

  

Rinse beans and place in a stockpot with the ham hock, carrots and onion and 2 quarts of water. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Add about 1 teaspoon salt. Uncover, reduce heat and simmer until beans are tender, about 1 hour. When beans are cooked, reduce heat to low. Retrieve ham shank from the pot and set aside until cool enough to handle. Remove meat from bone, discarding skin, bone, and excess fat. Dice meat and add back into soup. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
