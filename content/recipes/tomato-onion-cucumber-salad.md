Vidalia Onion

Seedless cucumber

1 red tomato

1 yellow tomato

Chopped green onions

¼ cup sugar

½ cup white vinegar

¼ cup olive oil

Crab -  optional

  

Marinate cucumbers and onions (sliced) in ¼ cup vinegar and ¼ cup sugar, salt, pepper for 3-4 hours. Toss with olive oil. Add crab if desired.
