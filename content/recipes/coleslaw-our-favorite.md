1 medium head cabbage, shredded

½ large red onion, diced

1 cup grated carrots

½ cup white sugar

½ cup white or apple cider vinegar

½ cup vegetable oil

½ tablespoon salt

1 tablespoon dry mustard

Pepper to taste

  

In a large bowl, combine cabbage, onion, carrots. 

  

In a small sauce pan, combine vinegar, sugar, oil, salt, dry mustard and pepper. Bring to boil. Pour hot dressing over cabbage mixture and mix well.

  

Chill in fridge.
