Preheat oven to 350F

Yield: 10 donuts

  

150g glutinous rice flour

1 teaspoon baking powder

¼ teaspoon salt

50g unsalted butter, melted

50g brown sugar

1 large egg, room temperature

200g milk

1 teaspoon vanilla

  

Preheat oven to 350F and lightly grease a donut pan with cooking spray. Set aside.

  

In a small bowl, stir together glutinous rice flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.

  

In another small mixing bowl, stir together melted butter and brown sugar. Add in the egg and stir until the yolk has broken up and is combined. Add in milk and vanilla extract.

  

Add dry mixture to wet mixture, stirring to combine until smooth.

  

Bake 25-30 minutes until slightly browned on the edges. Stop at 25 minutes for softer donuts or 30 minutes for crisper donuts but be careful not to overbake. Let cool on wire rack before glazing. 

  

When slightly warm, glaze.

  

Yuzu glaze: 1 cup powdered sugar, 2 tablespoons yuzu juice, and 1-2 tablespoons milk to desired thickness.
