2 cups flour (120g each) 

1 ¾ cup sugar

¼ teaspoon salt

½ teaspoon baking soda

½ teaspoon baking powder

1 ⅓ cups olive oil

1 cup whole milk

3 eggs

2 tablespoons grated orange zest (3-4 oranges)

½ cup orange juice

½ cup grand marnier or cointreau liqueur

  

Heat oven to 350F. 

  

In a large bowl, whisk wet ingredients: orange zest, olive oil, milk, orange juice, eggs, liqueur.

  

In a smaller bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, salt, baking powder and soda.

  

Combine ingredients.

  

Pour into greased springform pan OR use a 9” cake pan greased with parchment on the bottom. The 9” inch cake pan needs to be 2 inches tall on the side. If it’s not, use two cake pans.

  

Bake 1 hour until top is golden and tester comes out clean (or the internal temperature is 200F - but make sure to check the top carefully because in my experience, it cooks from the bottom up, and the top can be not quite done when the rest of the cake seems done).

  

Cool 30 minutes. Release Springform pan or remove from cake pan by flipping and then flipping back upright. Dust top with powdered sugar.
