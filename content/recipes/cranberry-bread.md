4 cups flour

3 teaspoons baking powder

2 cups raw cranberries cut in half

2 cups sugar

4 tablespoons melted butter

zest of two oranges

1 teaspoon baking soda

4 tablespoons hot water

2 eggs beaten

1 cup orange juice

  

Mix dry ingredients, then add eggs and other wet ingredients. 

  

Bake in two greased and floured loaf pans for 1 hour 10-15 minutes at 350F. 

  

Remove while hot, wrap in waxed paper and refrigerate.
