3 ripe avocados (Can also use fewer and adjust seasonings below)

Juice of about ½ lemon, start with about 1 ½ tablespoons lemon juice

¼ to ½  teaspoon minced garlic

¾ small onion diced 

1 large tomato, diced (bigger dice than onions)

Salt (start with about ¼ to ½ a teaspoon)

  

All of this is to taste, so start with less and add as needed. Mash avocados, add lemon juice and salt, mix together. Add minced garlic. Add diced onion. Add diced tomato. Taste. Figure out what it needs more of and add it. Does it need more salt? More lemon juice? Usually it won’t need more garlic because the longer it sits, the more you taste the garlic. But it could need more tomatoes or onions or salt or lemon juice. It’s really to your preference. Some people add cilantro, you also could mince a jalapeno and add it.
