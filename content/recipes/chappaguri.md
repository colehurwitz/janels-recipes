Makes enough for 2 - add more of everything to feed more:

  

1 package Chapaghetti ramen

1 package Neoguri ramen

8 ounces filet mignon steak cut into cubes

Sliced mushrooms (my variation, so optional)

Salt 

Pepper

Vegetable oil

Butter

  

Cook mushrooms until crisp tender (I don’t like them too mushy). Remove from pan. Combine steak, salt, pepper, and vegetable oil in pan. Mix well. Cook about 2-3 minutes. Add butter, cook 1 more minute until medium rare.

  

Bring enough water to boil for the number of packets you are using. Open both packages and take out noodles and packets. Add noodles and vegetable flakes to boiling water. Cook 2-3 minutes.

Strain noodles but keep water.

  

Put noodles in pan with steak and mushrooms. Add black bean seasoning and spicy powder from ramens. Stir and add ramen water until desired consistency. Stir in oil packs.

  

Serve\!
