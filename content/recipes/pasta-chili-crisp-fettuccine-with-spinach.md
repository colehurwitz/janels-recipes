4 tablespoons butter

2 tablespoons chili crisp

1 cup heavy cream

6 ounces or more spinach

¾ cup parmesan

  

Bring water to a boil. In large skillet, melt butter with chili crisp. Whisk in cream. Keep warm on low.

  

Cook pasta. Transfer pasta to cream mixture, reserving a cup of pasta water. Add spinach (can chop or be whole leaf). Turn to coat noodles. Spinach will wilt. Toss in parmesan.
