1 16 to 18 ounce box white cake mix

1 cup toasted unsweetened shredded coconut

Eggs (as called for by cake mix)

Oil (as called for by cake mix)

Coconut milk (use instead of water in cake mix)

1 teaspoon coconut extract

1 can sweetened condensed milk

1 cup coconut milk

1 cup heavy whipping cream

1/4 cup powdered sugar

1 teaspoon coconut extract

1 cup toasted flaked coconut

  

In a bowl, place cake mix, eggs, shredded coconut, oil, coconut milk, and 1 teaspoon coconut extract. Mix and bake in a 9x13 pan according to directions.

  

When done, poke holes all over cake with wooden skewer. In a small bowl, mix together the sweetened condensed milk with 1 cup coconut milk until well blended. Slowly pour over still hot cake, making sure to get mixture into holes. Let cake cool completely, up to overnight.

  

In a medium bowl, whip cream until it resembles texture of whipped cream. Add coconut extract and powdered sugar and whip until well combined. Spread whipped cream over top of cooled cake. Sprinkle toasted coconut over whipped cream. Serve.
