2 cups cake flour

1 cup sugar

2 teaspoons baking powder

2 teaspoons baking soda

Dash salt

1 cup neutral oil (safflower)

1 cup ripe bananas mashed

2 ½ teaspoons vanilla

⅓ cup buttermilk

2 egg yolks

2 egg whites

1 teaspoon cream of tartar

½  cup sugar

  

Rum Syrup: ¼ cup rum, ¼ cup sugar

  

Whipped Cream:  ¾ cup confectioner’s sugar, 2 cups whipping cream, ¼ cup mascarpone, 1 teaspoon vanilla

  

3 bananas sliced

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder and soda. Add buttermilk, oil, bananas, vanilla, and egg yolks. Make meringue - beat egg whites, add cream of tartar, then ½ cup sugar gradually. Fold into batter.

  

Prepare a 12x17 jelly roll pan. Butter edges, line with parchment, and flour it. Bake 17-20 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes.

  

Make rum syrup. Combine ¼ cups sugar and rum. Simmer until it thickens, about 2 minutes. Flip cake onto cutting board. Stab with toothpick. Spoon rum sauce over cake.

  

Make frosting. Whip cream and marscapone, gradually adding sifted confectioner’s sugar and vanilla.

  

Make into a 3 layer cake (cut cake into thirds). Top frosting with banana slices between layers.
