1 ½ cups (188g) flour

1 teaspoon baking powder

¼ teaspoon baking soda

½ cup oil (I melted butter)

⅔ cup sugar

3 eggs

¾ cup Greek yogurt or sour cream 

1 teaspoon vanilla

1 tablespoon lemon juice

1 teaspoon lemon zest

Lots of peaches (4 cups?-enough to cover first and second layer of cake construction)

  

Preheat oven to 350F. Grease springform pan (or I used a cast iron pan).

  

Whisk together flour, baking powder and soda in large bowl.

  

In medium bowl, whisk oil, sugar, eggs, yogurt/sour cream, vanilla, lemon juice and lemon zest.

  

Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients; whisk until combined.

  

Spread half batter on bottom of pan (will be thin layer). Cover batter with peaches placed on top. Spread remaining batter over peaches. Cover batter with peaches placed on top. Bake for 50-55 minutes. Tent with foil after 30 minutes if needed to avoid over browning. Serve with ice cream or whipped cream.
