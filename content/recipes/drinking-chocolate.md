½ cups milk

¼ cup superfine sugar (can use regular sugar)

3.5 ounces 70% chocolate bar, chopped finely

¼ cup unsweetened cocoa

  

Combine milk and sugar in medium saucepan. Heat mixture over medium until it reaches rolling boil. Add chopped chocolate and cocoa powder. Whisk these ingredients into milk mixture and return to rolling boil. Mixture will thicken.

  

Blend for 5 minutes with an immersion blender or a regular blender for ½ minute or until thick and foamy.  Serve\!
