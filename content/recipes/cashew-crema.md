Place two cups raw, unsalted cashews in a medium saucepan and cover with water. Bring to boil. Reduce heat to medium. Cook until nuts begin to plump, about 15 minutes.

  

Drain. Transfer to a blender. Add 1 cup cold water, 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice, and ½ teaspoon salt.

  

Puree. Add water if it’s too thick.
