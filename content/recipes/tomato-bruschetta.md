2-3 tomatoes (vine ripened are best)

3 medium cloves garlic, minced

2 tablespoons fresh basil, chopped

¾ teaspoon salt

½ cup to ¾ cup olive oil

  

Chop tomatoes; put in bowl. Mince garlic, add to bowl. Chop basil, add to bowl. Sprinkle salt over mixture. Stir together.

  

Pour olive oil over mixture. Mix together.

  

Let sit in fridge for a couple hours, stirring once in a while, to let flavors blend.

Serve over fresh baguette or toasted baguette or good french bread with goat cheese or feta. Yum\!
