1 cup nut butter 

¾ cup brown rice syrup

3 tablespoons coconut oil

¾ cup brown sugar

2 teaspoons vanilla

  

Cook in microwave to boiling - 212F. (I set microwave to 3 minutes, then take it out and stir in. Put it back in for 3 minutes, but keep an eye on it and take it out when it starts to boil really fast).

  

Add and mix in:

  

2 cups oatmeal

2 cups rice krispies

1 cup dried fruit (I like golden berries but there are a lot of options\!)

1 cup nuts, broken into pieces (again your choice\!)

½ cup unsweetened shredded coconut

¼ cup chia

¼ cup flax 

  

Spread in 9x13 pan. Press into pan using parchment paper. Let cool. Cut into bars.
