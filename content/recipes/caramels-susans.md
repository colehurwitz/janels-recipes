1 cup butter

2 ¼ cup packed brown sugar

dash salt

1 cup light corn syrup

1 15 ounce can sweetened condensed milk

1 teaspoon vanilla

  

Melt butter. Add sugar, salt, and stir. Stir in corn syrup. Add milk. Cook over medium heat to 245F, 12-15 minutes.

  

Remove from heat. Stir in vanilla. Pour into buttered 9 x 13 pan.

  

Can transform them into Chocolate Covered Caramels as follows:

  

Melt chocolate (semi-sweet or bittersweet) with 1 tablespoon butter; stir until smooth. Dip caramel squares in chocolate and place on waxed paper to cool.
