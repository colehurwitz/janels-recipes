½ pound beef, sirloin tip, flank

1 pound broccoli

1 clove garlic

1 onion sliced

1 cup water or beef stock

Seasoning:

Mix in bowl

2 tablespoon soy sauce

1 tablespoon cornstarch

1 tablespoon mirin

1 teaspoon sugar

½ teaspoon salt

Ginger, peeled, chopped finely or can use 1 teaspoon dried ginger powder

Dash of pepper

  

Slice beef very thin and soak in seasoning for 10 minutes or more. Heat oil until shimmering. Add the steak and cook, stirring frequently until medium rare, about 4 minutes. Transfer to plate.

  

Cut broccoli into 2 inch lengths and slice onion. Add onion and broccoli to pan. Stir until onion softens, about 3 minutes. 

  

Return beef to pan. Whisk in soy sauce marinade. Cook until thickens. If the sauce is too runny, mix 1 teaspoon cornstarch and a little water together and pour it into pan. Cook until it thickens. 

  

Serve over rice.
