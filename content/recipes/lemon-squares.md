Bake in 9x9x2 pan

  

1 cup sifted flour

¼ cup powdered sugar

½ teaspoon baking powder

½ cup butter

Grated peel of one lemon

Mix together and press into pan. 

  

Bake 15 minutes at 375F.

  

Prepare topping:

  

2 eggs, beaten

1 c white sugar (to taste - maybe less)

¼ cup flour

Juice and zest of 1 or 2 lemons depending on juiciness

  

When crust is done, pour lemon mixture on and return to oven for 25 minutes. Cool and dust with powdered sugar. Cool, cut and serve.
