2 squares (2 ounces) unsweetened chocolate

⅓ cup water

½ cup sugar

3 tablespoons butter

¼ teaspoon vanilla

  

Heat chocolate and water in saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly until chocolate is melted and mixture is smooth. Add sugar. Bring to boil. Boil 2 to 3 minutes or until slightly thickened, stirring constantly. Remove from heat; stir in butter and vanilla.
