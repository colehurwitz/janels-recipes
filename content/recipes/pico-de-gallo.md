1 ½ pounds roma tomatoes

Juice from 1 ½ limes

½ cup cilantro

1 cup finely chopped white onion

1 jalapeño ribs and seeds removed 

¾ teaspoon salt

  

Dice onions, tomatoes and jalapeño and put in large bowl. Zest and juice limes. Add to bowl. Rough chop then throw in cilantro. Season with salt. Let sit one hour.
