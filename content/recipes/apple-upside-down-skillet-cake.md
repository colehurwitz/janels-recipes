Topping:

  

Combine: 6 tablespoons butter, ½ cup brown sugar. Melt in round cast iron skillet, cook 1 minute to thicken. Remove from heat, whisk in ½ teaspoon cinnamon and ¼ teaspoon vanilla.

Apples: Peel and core about 4 apples. Slice into ¼ inch thick slices. Toss in lemon juice.

  

Cake:

  

1 ½ cups (188g) flour

1 ½ teaspoons baking powder

¼ teaspoon baking soda

1 ½ teaspoons cinnamon

½ cup butter, softened

½ cup brown sugar

2 eggs

1 teaspoon vanilla extract

6 tablespoons whole milk

½ cup sour cream or plain yogurt

1 whole small apple peeled, diced

  

Mix together butter and brown sugar. Add eggs, vanilla extract. Add dry ingredients along with milk and sour cream. Fold in the one diced apple when everything else is combined.

  

Place the 4 sliced apples on top of brown sugar in the cast iron pan decoratively\! Spread cake mixture on top of apples. 

  

Bake at 350F for 30 minutes. Test with a toothpick. Especially check the center to make sure it’s done.
