You can double the recipe using 6 chicken thighs. If you use even one more than 6 thighs, triple the recipe.

  

3 chicken thighs, cut into 1-2 inch pieces

1 ½  tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili paste)

½  tablespoon gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)

1 tablespoons soy sauce

1  tablespoons brown sugar

½  tablespoon minced garlic

½ teaspoon minced ginger

1 tablespoon Mirin 

1 tablespoon sesame oil

  

In a large mixing bowl, mix together gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, Mirin and sesame oil. Add cut up chicken thighs and toss together to coat evenly. Let marinate 2 hours or so (recipe says let sit 10 minutes, so if you’re in a hurry, it’s not necessary to let it marinate that long).

  

You can either broil, grill, or pan cook the chicken. I pan cooked it on medium or medium plus heat, depending on how hot your stove is, cooking the pieces about 4 minutes per side. I added some sesame oil to the pan and heated it up before adding the chicken pieces. If you broil the chicken, the recipe says to cook the chicken about 4 minutes per side. Temperature should be 165F.

  

Serve over rice.
