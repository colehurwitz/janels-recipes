½ cup oil

4 squares (4 ounces) unsweetened chocolate, melted

2 cups sugar

4 eggs

2 teaspoons vanilla

2 cups flour

2 teaspoons baking powder

Dash salt

Powdered sugar to roll the dough in

  

Mix oil, chocolate, sugar. Blend in eggs. Add vanilla. Stir in flour, baking powder, salt. Chill several hours. 

  

Preheat oven to 350F. 

  

Roll 1 inch balls out of cookie dough; roll in powdered sugar.

  

Bake 12 minutes. Do not over bake - but don’t underbake\!
