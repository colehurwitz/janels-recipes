1 pound ground lamb

1 pound ground beef

  

Grind in blender:

  

1 cup flat leaf parsley 

½ cup mint

1 onion

6 cloves garlic

1-3 teaspoons 7 spice (sebat baharat)

1 ½ teaspoons salt

  

Mix with ground meats. Form patties, cook. 

  

Serve with pita bread, and make into a sandwich with tzatziki, tomato, onion, and whatever else you’d like.
