3 tablespoons neutral oil

2 large onions, peeled and sliced (or chopped)

Pinch of kosher salt, or to taste

1 pound ground pork

4 cloves garlic peeled and chopped

10-15 cylindrical rice cakes, rice noodles, or pasta

1 inch fresh ginger, peeled and chopped

3 tablespoons gochujang

1 tablespoon soy sauce

1 tablespoon brown sugar

1 teaspoon arbol chile powder (you can buy toasted arbol chilis and grind them in     the cuisinart)

1 bunch kale or cooking green (I used spinach)

4 scallions thinly sliced for garnish

  

Heat oil in a wok over medium high heat. When it shimmers, add the onions and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onions have released their moisture and are starting to brown, approximately 10 minutes. Then turn heat to low, and continue cooking, stirring every few minutes, until they have turned golden brown and sweet, an additional 20 minutes or so.

  

Tip onions into a bowl, and return the wok to high heat over the stove. Add remaining oil, then pork, and cook, breaking the meat up with a spoon, until it is just cooked, but not yet browning, approximately 10 minutes. Add the cooked meat to the reserved onions.

  

Put a large pot of salted water over high heat and bring to a boil.

  

Return wok to stove over medium heat and cook the garlic and ginger in fat remaining from pork. When the garlic and ginger soften, add the gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, and arbol chile powder.  Add ½ to 1 cup of water, enough to loosen the gochujang and make a sauce, then return pork and onions to the wok and stir to combine. Adjust seasonings.

  

Bring sauce to a simmer, and add the chopped greens, then stir to combine and cook until they have started to soften, approximately 5 minutes. Prepare pasta, then drain and add to sauce. Garnish with scallions.
