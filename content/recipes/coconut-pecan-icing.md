Combine in pan:

  

⅔ cup sugar

⅔ cup evaporated milk

2 egg yolks

⅓ cup butter

½ teaspoon vanilla

  

Cook and stir constantly over low heat about 10 minutes or until the mixture thickens. Do not boil.

  

Remove from heat and add:

  

1 ⅓ cups flaked coconut

⅔ to 1 cup chopped pecans
